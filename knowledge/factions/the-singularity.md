# The Singularity

One of the Midnight renown factions. Home turf is **Voidstorm**.

⚠ **As of 12.1 there are 6 Midnight renown factions** — Silvermoon Court,
Amani Tribe, The Hara'ti, The Singularity, Slayer's Rise, and the new
**Zul'jarra's Forces** (`factions/zuljarras-forces.md`), the Coiled Isle's
own track. **The Singularity's own renown track is unchanged by 12.1**: same
20 ranks, same quartermaster, same reward list — neither the 12.1 content
update notes nor the 12.0.7 hotfix log mentions this faction. Coiled Isle
content feeds Zul'jarra's Forces, **not** this faction.

## Quartermaster + location

- **Void Researcher Anomander** — quartermaster, eastern Howling Ridge
  in **Voidstorm** (~52.6, 72.8).
- Spends **Voidlight Marl**; item costs range ~150–8,000. ⚠ Voidlight Marl
  is the **shared Midnight catch-all currency**, not a Singularity-only
  reputation currency — it is also spent at Hara'ti and Slayer's Rise
  vendors, and 12.1 made it a Coiled Isle currency too.

## Renown track highlights (key rewards per rank)

- **Rank 2:** ilvl 180 gloves.
- **Rank 7:** *Crucible of Erratic Energies* — ilvl 246 Champion trinket
  (requires completing the Stormarion Assault quest).
- **Ranks 3–18:** progressive housing/decor unlocks (thrones, tables,
  barrels, banners, lanterns, crates, training dummies).
- **Rank 17:** *Ravenous Shredclaw* (ground mount).
- **Rank 19:** *Voidbound Stormray* (flying mount).
- **Research Console completion:** *Lab-Grown Stormray*.
- Renown caps at 20; past 20 yields Paragon Bags.

## Weekly renown sources

- World Quests & Special Assignments in Voidstorm.
- World Quests with **Contract: The Singularity** active.
- **Stormarion Assault** world event (weekly quest "Stand Your Ground").
- Silvermoon dungeon weekly quests.
- Bountiful Caches from Delves. ⚠ **Not available during the 12.1 pre-season
  week (Aug 11–17, 2026)** — Bountiful Delves do not return until Season 2
  opens **2026-08-18**. This source is dark for that one week only.
