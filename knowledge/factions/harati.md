# The Hara'ti

One of **6** Midnight renown factions (Silvermoon Court, Amani Tribe,
The Hara'ti, The Singularity, Slayer's Rise, and — new in 12.1 —
**Zul'jarra's Forces** on the Coiled Isle; see `factions/zuljarras-forces.md`).
The Hara'ti are the Haranir of **Harandar** — the renown faction tied to that
zone's world content.

Renown caps at **20** (each rank ~2,500 rep), with Paragon rewards beyond.
No 12.1 "Curse of Ula'tek" changes to this faction were found in the content
update notes or the 12.0.7 hotfix log — the Hara'ti are not mentioned in either.
Content below is unchanged from 12.0.5 and has **not** been re-sourced since
2026-06-19; treat the reward ilvls in particular as Midnight-launch-era numbers
(see the caveat under the renown track).

## Quartermaster + location

- **Naynar**, "Renown Quartermaster for the Hara'ti."
- Northern portion of the **upper level of The Den** (Harandar).
- Currency: **Voidlight Marl** (some recipes also cost profession-specific
  Artisan Moxie, e.g. Artisan Tailor's/Alchemist's/Enchanter's Moxie).
  ⚠ As of 12.1, **Voidlight Marl and Artisan Moxies are shared with Zul'jarra's
  Forces** — the Coiled Isle quartermaster (Jan'sari the Watchful, Tokka's
  Landing) spends the same currencies, so marl now has two competing sinks.

## Renown track highlights (key rewards per rank)

- **Rank 2** — Moth-hunting access; belt gear (~ilvl 180).
- **Rank 8** — Champion waist armor (~ilvl 246).
- **Rank 14** — *Munchy* battle pet.
- **Rank 16** — *Fierce Grimlynx* ground mount.
- **Rank 19** — *Cerulean Sporeglider* flying mount.
- **Rank 20** — "Honorary Hara'ti" title + four class-specific armor ensembles.

The vendor also stocks cosmetic transmog (the Hara'ti weapon arsenal),
toys, profession recipes, and knowledge books.

⚠ **The ilvls above are Midnight-launch (12.0.5) values from Tier-3/4 sources
and were not re-verified in the 12.1 sweep.** They are almost certainly no
longer worth chasing for power: Season 2 gear spans **ilvl 269 → 334**, so a
rank-8 "Champion waist ~246" is well below even the S2 Adventurer floor. Read
this track as a **cosmetic / mount / pet / recipe** track in 12.1, and confirm
any gear number in game before acting on it. @verify-ingame

## Weekly / repeatable renown sources

- World quests and special assignments in Harandar.
- World quests anywhere in Midnight with **Contract: The Hara'ti** active.
- Main + side storyline quests.
- Rare mob kills (once per rare).
- **Legends of the Haranir** world-event weekly quest.
- Bountiful Caches in Delves. ⚠ **Unavailable during the pre-season week of
  2026-08-11** — Bountiful Delves do not return until Season 2 opens
  **2026-08-18**. This renown source is simply off the table until then.
- Dungeon weekly quests in Silvermoon. ⚠ The dungeon pool rotated for Season 2;
  the weekly still exists but points at the new 8-dungeon S2 rotation.
