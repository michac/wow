# Zul'jarra's Forces

The **fifth** Midnight renown faction, added in **12.1 "Curse of Ula'tek"**
(live 2026-08-11) alongside Silvermoon Court, Amani Tribe, The Hara'ti and
The Singularity. It is the **primary renown track for the Coiled Isle** — the
fog-shrouded island off the east coast of Zul'Aman. Faction hub is **Tokka's
Landing**.

(Slayer's Rise, the sixth file in `knowledge/factions/`, is **not** a renown
faction — Slayer's Duellum is old-school Friendly→Exalted standing and is
character-specific. See `factions/slayers-rise.md`.)

Story framing: Zul'jarra pursues her brother **Zul'jan** to the isle; her forces
are the expedition you are earning standing with.

> **Available now.** The faction and its renown track went live with the 12.1
> patch on 2026-08-11, during the **pre-season week**. Season 2 opens
> **2026-08-18**, and a few *reputation sources* below are gated behind it
> (Bountiful Delves, the higher Tidebound Grotto difficulties) — the renown
> track itself is not.

## Renown structure

**20 ranks, 2,500 reputation each** (the standard Midnight faction track), earned
**account-wide**.

## Quartermaster + location

- **Jan'sari the Watchful**, a forest troll at **Tokka's Landing, The Coiled
  Isle** (`/way #2512 58.8 46.0`).

## Currencies

- **Voidlight Marl** — the primary quartermaster currency, and the same
  Midnight-wide currency the other renown vendors take (it drops from activities
  across all Midnight zones).
- **Artisan Moxies** — the per-profession knowledge currency (Artisan Scribe's
  Moxie, Artisan Enchanter's Moxie, …), spent here on **profession items**:
  knowledge tomes and recipes such as *Technique: Contract: Zul'jarra's Forces*
  (150 Artisan Scribe's Moxie). This currency is **not new in 12.1** — it
  predates the isle (it is also a Hara'ti currency family).
  ⚠ **Tome price is unconfirmed.** Tier-4 guides quote **750 Voidlight Marl +
  75 or 150 of the matching Artisan Moxie** per knowledge tome; the Tier-1
  article gives no price. `systems/professions.md` carries the same quote and
  the same caveat — keep the two in step. @verify-ingame: read the tome prices
  off Jan'sari at R6.

## Earning renown

**Before landfall.** Renown starts accruing *before you reach the isle*, via a
series of **Zul'Aman quests** with **Lady Liadrin**, **Orweyna** and
**Zul'jarra** — Blizzard states this explicitly, so do not tell anyone to fly out
first.

One-time sources (~4,200 rep total):

| Source | Count | Rep each |
|---|---|---|
| Lore objects | 10 | 250 |
| Treasures | 22 | 50 |
| First kill of each rare | 12 | 50 |

Plus the **Coiled Isle campaign and side quests**, which are the single largest
one-time chunk.

Repeatable sources:

- **Weekly dungeon quest** — 1,000 rep.
- **Weekly Spark quest** — 1,000 rep.
- **Vaults of Atal'Utek** — a weekly quest, plus daily quests at ~50 rep each.
- **Curse Surges** — ~50 rep per completion (rare elites at five rotating
  locations across the isle).
- **World Quests** — rotating, daily.
- **Lair: Nymrissa Wavecaller** (Tidebound Grotto) — 200 rep, weekly lockout.
- **Bountiful Delves** — a large weekly chunk. ⚠ **Not available during the
  pre-season week**; Bountiful Delves return **2026-08-18**.

Weekly quests are the time-efficient core; the treasures/lore-objects/rares
sweep is a one-time ~1.7 ranks.

## Renown track highlights (key rewards per rank)

- **Rank 2** — *Cloak of the Hash'ura*; **Cursebreaker's Bracers I** (Veteran
  wrist).
- **Rank 3** — camp/housing decor (Supply Sack, Storage Crate, Wayfarer's Torch)
  and **Spoils of Coils** gathering objects.
- **Rank 4** — your first **Curse Surge** boss of the day gains a chance to drop
  a **Corrosive Soul**.
- **Rank 5** — profession recipes (Alchemy, Enchanting, Engineering, Tailoring,
  Jewelcrafting, Leatherworking) and the **Ula'tek Snakehead Lure** fishing
  recipe.
- **Rank 6** — profession **knowledge tomes**; **Zul'jarra's Bounty I** (a
  Voidlight Marl payout).
- **Rank 7** — furnishings (bags, weapon rack).
- **Rank 8** — **Spirit of Corrosion I** conversion; Curse Surge bosses gain a
  daily chance at **Warbound Veteran** equipment.
- **Rank 9** — **Cursebreaker's Bracers II** (Champion wrist); Scout Satchels.
- **Rank 10** — *Tabard of the Hash'ura*; the **Spirit of Tok'jara** mount
  questline opens (reported to need ~6 daily-quest completions to finish).
- **Rank 11** — decorative urns (Scaleskin, Vilescar, Blisterfang).
- **Rank 12** — **Snek'zali** battle pet; **Zul'jarra's Bounty II**.
- **Rank 13** — *Idol of Ula'tek*.
- **Rank 14** — **Spirit of Corrosion II** conversion.
- **Rank 15** — ritual candles/totems; cosmetic arsenal.
- **Rank 16** — *Shoulderguards of the Hash'ura*.
- **Rank 17** — **Indigo Coiled Horror** ground mount (**6,000 Voidlight Marl**).
- **Rank 18** — reliquaries and ritual monoliths; cosmetic ensembles;
  **Zul'jarra's Bounty III**.
- **Rank 19** — **Violet-Backed Skyfang** mount.
- **Rank 20** — title **"Hash'ura of Zul'jarra"**; *Mantle of Nalorakk* and
  *Axe of the Amani*.

**Three mounts** on the track: Spirit of Tok'jara (R10 questline), Indigo Coiled
Horror (R17 purchase), Violet-Backed Skyfang (R19).

**Gear on the track is wrist only** — Cursebreaker's Bracers I (Veteran) at R2
and II (Champion) at R9. Everything else that is gear comes indirectly, via the
Curse Surge drop upgrades at R4 and R8. (Season 2's upgrade tracks are fed by
**Mistcrests**: Veteran spans ilvl **282–295**, Champion **295–308** — from
`CurrencyTypes` DB2 @ `12.1.0.69214`, the Tier-1 floor. The bracers' own base
ilvl is not published; assume the first step of each track until seen in game.)

## Adjacent systems (not this faction's track)

- **Altar of Corrosion** — the zone progression system: a **zone-scoped talent
  tree** bought with **Corrosive Coins**, granting exploration/combat/mobility
  and reward bonuses (including reducing the potency of the isle's venom). The
  **Spirit of Corrosion I/II** unlocks at renown 8 and 14 feed it — you bring a
  *Corrosive Relic* / *Corrosive Bijou* to **Er'inye** to convert. Details live
  in `knowledge/systems/coiled-isle.md`.
- **Captain Tokka** — the tortollan sea captain at Tokka's Landing runs a
  **separate friendship faction** (not renown). He teaches **Cursed Fishing**;
  **Venom Fishing** unlocks at a Curse Surge location once you kill its rare
  elite.

## Confidence + open items

Tier-1 (the Blizzard Coiled Isle article) establishes the faction, the
pre-landfall Zul'Aman start, the quartermaster and the two currencies. The
**per-rank reward list and all reputation numbers are Tier-3** (Icy Veins /
Method) and should be treated as good-but-unconfirmed until seen in game.

Two things were corrected against game data rather than editorial prose:

- **Indigo Coiled Horror is a mount, not a pet** (one guide listed it as a pet;
  the item DB page — [item 276802](https://www.wowhead.com/item=276802) — and
  [Warcraft Wiki](https://warcraft.wiki.gg/wiki/Indigo_Coiled_Horror) both have
  it as a **ground mount**, BoP, sold by Jan'sari at Renown 17 for **6,000
  Voidlight Marl**). Both URLs are in this file's `sources:`.
- **"Artisan Moxies"** is the existing per-profession family, not a new
  Coiled-Isle currency.
