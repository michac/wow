# Amani Tribe

One of the Midnight renown factions. Home turf is **Zul'Aman**; the faction
hub is **Amani'Zar Village**.

⚠ **12.1 adds two new Midnight reputation tracks, and neither of them is this
one.** Alongside the five that already existed (Silvermoon Court, Amani Tribe,
The Hara'ti, The Singularity, Slayer's Rise):

- **Zul'jarra's Forces** (`factions/zuljarras-forces.md`) — the Coiled Isle's
  primary renown track, 20 ranks, quartermaster Jan'sari the Watchful at
  Tokka's Landing.
- **Captain Tokka's Crew** — the tortollan sea captain's smaller
  friendship-style track tied to Cursed/Venom Fishing, **5 ranks**: Stranger →
  Doomed Sailor → Cursed Angler → **Venom Trawler** → **Bloodsworn Crew**.
  Quartermaster **Second Mate Sluggs** at **Tokka's Folly**; the top rewards
  (Sea-Dwelling Isle Serpent mount, the Envenomed weapons) are gated on
  *"Bloodsworn Crew or above"*. No KB file yet — see `_meta/kb-inbox.md`.

That makes seven Midnight reputation tracks in 12.1 — the five above plus these
two — of which `knowledge/factions/` has files for six. They are not all the
standard 20-rank renown shape, so don't quote a bare "N renown factions" count
without saying which shape you mean. **The 12.1 notes make no change to the
Amani Tribe track itself** — same 20 ranks, same quartermaster, same reward
list. Coiled Isle
content feeds Zul'jarra's Forces and Tokka's Crew, **not** this faction, so
don't expect Amani Tribe renown from the new zone.

## Renown structure

20 ranks, 2500 reputation each (standard Midnight faction track).

## Quartermaster + location

- **Magovu**, in the main building at **Amani'Zar Village, Zul'Aman**
  (~45.8, 65.8).

## Weekly renown sources

- Zul'Aman campaign / zone quests, World Quests, and Special Assignments.
- Zul'Aman rare enemies.
- **Abundance** world event + the **Weekly: Abundance / Abundant Offerings**
  weekly quest.
- **Bountiful Caches** from Delves. ⚠ **Not available during the 2026-08-11
  pre-season week** — there are no Bountiful Delves live right now; they return
  with Season 2 on **2026-08-18**. Until then this source contributes nothing.
- Silvermoon dungeon weekly quests — Halduron Brightwing's weekly, paying one
  lump of **choosable renown** you can point at this faction. **Still available
  in the pre-season week:** it completes on a Midnight dungeon at *any*
  difficulty, so the absence of Mythic+ keystones until 2026-08-18 doesn't block
  it — but the dungeon pool itself changed with 12.1 (Season 2's eight
  dungeons, Altar of Fangs included). See
  `planning/activities/renown-dungeon-weekly.md` for the current pool and the
  disputed renown amount.

## Renown track highlights (key rewards per rank)

- **Rank 8:** unlocks the **Altar of Blessings** (Loa Blessings system, below).
- **Rank 9:** unlocks **Magovu's quest "An Abundance of Wealth"** — complete one
  **Abundance** world event → a **neck at ilvl 246** (choice of *Amani
  Heartstring Pendant* / *Amani Totemstring*). This is **quest-granted, not a
  straight vendor purchase**, so it shows as a quest at Magovu, not a buyable
  item — and a known bug can keep the quest from appearing until you `/reload`
  with an Abundance event active. (Corrects an earlier KB gap that implied Amani
  had no gear reward.)
  ⚠ **The old KB label "Champion 1/6" does not fit 246 and has been dropped.**
  Tier-1 game data puts Season 1's Champion Dawncrest band at **250–263** — so
  Champion 1/6 was **250**, and 246 falls inside the **Veteran** band (237–250)
  instead. One of the two was wrong when it was written; the ilvl is the value
  three lower-tier guides agree on, so the **246 is kept and the track label
  removed** rather than the reverse. Confirm the exact neck item names, that
  rank 9 grants this quest, and the neck's actual ilvl + upgrade track from its
  in-game tooltip. @verify-ingame
  ⚠ **246 is a Season 1 ilvl either way.** 12.1 did not restate this reward, and
  Season 2 gear spans **269 → 334** (the Mistcrest bands), so this neck now sits
  well below the S2 floor — take it for the collection, not as gearing.
- **Rank 12:** **Naloki** companion pet.
- **Rank 17:** **Amani Blessed Bear** mount.
- **Rank 19:** **Amani Windcaller** flying mount.
- **Rank 20:** **Crown of the Loa-Speaker** helm + **"Loa Speaker"** title.

Magovu also stocks cosmetics, decor, profession recipes, and player-power
items gated behind their respective ranks.

## Loa Blessings (Lorewalking with the Loa)

Activated at the **Altar of Blessings** (Rank 8). Combine one **major** loa
with one **minor** loa for zone-specific buffs while inside Zul'Aman.

- **Four major loas** (earned through the main storyline): **Akil'zon,
  Halazzi, Jan'alai, Nalorakk.** As of 12.1 each has a **housing shrine** —
  30 Community Coupons apiece from Griftah's "Knock-off Amani" Endeavor, at
  its third milestone (below).
- **Minor loas** (quests, fishing, farming, or purchase): Kulzi, Filo,
  Mot'amra, Wila'ma, Shadra, Dundun, Puul, Oe, Pahk.

## 12.1 "Curse of Ula'tek" — where the Amani story went

12.1 went live **2026-08-11**; **Midnight Season 2 opens 2026-08-18**, so the
raid half of this story is still upcoming. The 12.0.7 Zul'jan chapter paid off
exactly as advertised: it fed straight into 12.1.

**The story.** The mists part off the east coast of Zul'Aman, revealing **The
Coiled Isle** — a land steeped in ancient Amani history. **Zul'jarra** pursues
her brother **Zul'jan**, who — *"guided by the dark whispers of Malacrass"* —
has freed the forgotten broods of **Ula'tek** from their prison beneath the
isle. Beats, in order:

- **Zul'Aman prelude** — quests with **Lady Liadrin**, **Orweyna** and
  **Zul'jarra** before you make landfall. These already award renown with
  Zul'jarra's Forces.
- **Altar of Fangs** (3-boss dungeon, inside the Vaults of Atal'Utek) — the
  ancient Amani sealed the vaults with the **Fang of Ula'tek**, the only blade
  that can unseal them. Zul'jan enters "in a misbegotten attempt to bring lost
  glory back to the Amani" and performs the final ritual, still in Malacrass's
  sway — unaware the Fang is also his sister's only hope of survival.
- **The Venomous Abyss** raid (**opens Aug 18**) — Zul'jarra calls on the
  Champions of Azeroth to stop her brother before the weapon is loosed on the
  world; Ula'tek is the 8th and final boss.

**The Amani in the new zone.** The Vaults of Atal'Utek are an **ancient Amani
complex**; the Altar of Corrosion talent tree sits in the **Amani Foothold**
inside them, and **Amani Windcallers** ferry you around the Vaults (and can
briefly hide you from hostiles). All of that pays into **Zul'jarra's Forces**
renown — see `factions/zuljarras-forces.md`.

**Amani Pass is no longer a dismount.** The pass between **Eversong Woods and
Zul'Aman** no longer dismounts players riding through it — a long-standing
annoyance on the Amani'Zar Village run, fixed in 12.1.

**Housing: "Knock-off Amani" neighborhood Endeavor.** One of four new Endeavors,
and **the only one available immediately at 12.1 launch** (the rest rotate in
afterwards); it ties into Coiled Isle activity. **Griftah** and his travelling
troupe of traders sell "traditional Amani goods" that are transparently not
Amani — neighbors buy them anyway. Vendor: **Griftah**. New currency:
**Griftah's Token of Appreciation**, occasionally granted for an Endeavor task
depending on difficulty, spent back at Griftah (e.g. **Bag of Totally
Legitimate Amani Goods**, 1 token).

**The reason to care even if you don't decorate:** *"While this endeavor is
active, players will be able to access a portal directly to The Coiled Isle."*
That is a neighborhood-side shortcut into the new zone for as long as the
Endeavor is running.

Everything else on Griftah's table is bought with **Community Coupons**, and
the list is staged behind Endeavor milestones:

- **Available immediately:** Amani Building Peg (2) · Forest Troll Fencepost
  (10) · Steamy Romance Tablet (10) · Forest Troll Fence (15).
- **First milestone:** Amani Wicker Crate (10) · Amani Road Marker (15) ·
  Woven Forest Troll Rug (15).
- **Second milestone:** Amani Anvil (15) · Amani Decorative Plinth (20).
- **Third milestone:** Amani Water Well (15) · Witch Doctor's Punch Bowl (15) ·
  and the **four loa shrines at 30 each** — **Shrine of Halazzi, Loa of the
  Hunt** · **Shrine of Akil'zon, Loa of Victory** · **Shrine of Nalorakk, Loa
  of War** · **Shrine of Jan'alai, Loa of Fire**.

So the range runs **2–30 Community Coupons**, and the four most expensive items
are shrines to exactly the four major loas of the Loa Blessings system above.

## 12.0.7 "Revelations" additions (historical)

Three Amani-troll story beats landed with **12.0.7 (live 2026-06-16):**

- **Zul'jan Campaign Chapter** — the Midnight campaign chapter that opened
  **July 7, 2026**. Zul'jan attends a special **haranir** gathering as the
  Amani Troll representative and is drawn into an ancient mystery about the
  Amani Trolls' legacy (involves his uncle **Kinduru**). This chapter
  **led directly into the 12.1 Coiled Isle campaign** (above).
- **Jan'alai egg-hatching** — a short Zul'Aman questline helping **Loa Speaker
  Brek** welcome the Fire Loa Jan'alai's first new clutch of eggs into the
  world. Reward: the **Emberlyn** companion pet.
- **Troll Lorewalking** — story content with **Assistant Lorewalker Li Li**
  exploring loa and troll-tribe lore across the **Darkspear, Drakkari,
  Gurubashi, and Zandalari**. Lore/flavor content, not high-pressure
  progression.
