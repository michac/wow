# Slayer's Rise (zone) / Slayer's Duellum (reputation)

> **Correction (2026-06-03)**: earlier KB notes called this "one of the 5
> renown factions" — wrong. It is an **open-world PvP zone + old-school
> reputation**, not a renown track.

> **Scope note**: this file is also the KB's de-facto landing spot for
> **game-wide PvP rules** (see `_meta/moving-values.md`, which routes the PvP
> trinket / battleground / DR rows here). The zone material is unchanged in
> 12.1; the PvP-rules material below is not.

**Slayer's Rise** is an area in northern **Voidstorm** run by two Domanaar
lords — **Vidious** (Domanaar of Grief) and **Ziadan** (Domanaar of Hate) —
staging battle exercises against Xal'atath.

## Slayer's Duellum reputation

- **Old-school standing** (Friendly → Exalted), **character-specific** —
  unlike the Warband-wide renown factions (Silvermoon Court, Amani Tribe,
  Hara'ti, The Singularity, and as of 12.1 Zul'jarra's Forces).
- Earned from: zone world quests, Master's Perch quests, winning PvP events.
- **War Mode**: optional for world quests/exploration; **required only for
  the timed PvP events**. Toggleable by talking to either Domanaar.
- The zone's marquee War-Mode objective is the **Sparring Grounds**, a
  capturable point contested by faction (a 12.0.7 hotfix on 2026-06-18 fixed a
  bug where capturing it drained the wrong faction's reinforcements).
- Paragon: 1 cache per 10,000 rep past Exalted.

## Rewards — cosmetics only, no combat gear

- Quartermaster **Thraxadar**, north end of Master's Perch; currency
  **Voidlight Marl**.
- Décor (banners, barricades, orbs), companion pet.
- Exalted: **Prowling Shredclaw** / **Frenzied Shredclaw** mounts —
  **6,000 Voidlight Marl** each.
- Paragon cache (**Slayer's Duellum Trove**, per 10,000 rep past Exalted):
  chance at **Duskbrute Harrower** mount, **Nova** pet.
- ⚠ **Voidlight Marl is now spent in two places.** 12.1's new Coiled Isle
  quartermaster (**Jan'sari the Watchful**, Zul'jarra's Forces) also takes
  Voidlight Marl, so Marl banked for the Shredclaw mounts now competes with
  Coiled Isle purchases. Marl earned there spends here and vice versa.

## Implications

- **Not a gearing priority** — purely collectibles. Relevant for the
  Lady Liadrin spark weekly only insofar as zone WQs count toward it.
- The Sparks of War War-Mode spark weekly rotates through Voidstorm; the
  zone pairs naturally with that week.

## PvP season state — 12.1 (as of 2026-08-11)

**Midnight PvP Season 1 is over.** Competitive PvP ended at **22:00 the night
before regional maintenance** the week of 2026-08-11; PvP percentile cutoffs
were calculated when realms went down. **Season 2 does not start until the week
of 2026-08-18** — this week is an official **pre-season**:

- **Live now (pre-season week)**: unrated Battlegrounds, outdoor-world PvP, and
  the new **Training Grounds: Arenas** — **3v3 versus bots**, via Group Finder →
  Player vs. Player tab (the same on-ramp as the existing bot Battlegrounds).
  **No rated PvP.**
- **Week of 2026-08-18**: all rated PvP opens — ranking, Conquest/Honor gear,
  Elite sets, titles, achievements and toys.

**Season 2 mounts** (both S2-exclusive, gone when S2 ends):
**Venomous Gladiator's Goredrake** (Gladiator — 50 3v3 wins at Elite/2300) and
**Vicious Lightbloom Boar** (Vicious Saddle — wins above 1000 rating in any
bracket). *(Blizzard's notes spell the first "Venemous"; "Venomous" is the
correct form.)*

None of this touches Slayer's Rise itself: the zone is **War-Mode open-world
PvP**, not seasonal rated content, so its rep, currency and rewards run
uninterrupted through the season rollover.

## Game-wide PvP rules changed in 12.1

These are **PvP-only** and must not be written into PvE rotation guidance.

- **Gladiator's Distinction** (PvP trinket set bonus): tank/DPS now **+15%
  primary stat** (was 12%) and **+5% Stamina** (was 10%); healers **+10%
  Stamina** (was 15%). A meaningful re-weighting — DPS gain throughput and lose
  effective health, healers lose effective health.
- **Battlegrounds: players receive 20% less healing.** Stated intent is to make
  healers and off-healing less dominant in BG survivability.
- **No knockback while under Fear or Disorient** — knockback effects no longer
  affect players under those crowd-control types.
- **Diminishing-return categories reset after 20 seconds** (was 16); the chain
  limit is still 2 CCs before immunity.
- **Solo Shuffle / Battleground Blitz**: missing a queue applies a **1-minute
  re-queue debuff** that **stacks for consecutive misses** within a window and
  is **account-wide** (anti win-trading / queue manipulation).
- **Spoils of War** grants **+50% Conquest** once Conquest is uncapped for the
  season (was 30%) — catch-up for players starting a season late.
- **Game-wide snare tier-down**: auto-applied rotational movement slows are
  reduced "a tier" in PvP — 70% → 50%, 50% → 30%, and so on. Every class has
  affected lines; some *activated* slows keep their strength. Per-class numbers
  live in `_meta/patch-notes/12.1.md` under each `▶ CLASS` heading.
- Also relevant: **max-level player health and creature damage were raised 25%**
  game-wide in 12.1, which shifts every absolute PvP burst/healing number.

## Historical — 12.0.7 PvP gearing changes

12.0.7 "Revelations" reworked **rated** PvP gearing: the Galactic
Gladiator/Aspirant/Warmonger and crafted PvP sets gained **+9 PvP ilvl**, and
the **Galactic Voidsliver** / **Galactic Void Matrix** upgrade currencies were
removed (existing copies refunded as gold). That was Season 1 material and is
now superseded by the Season 2 gear ladder opening 2026-08-18. It never touched
Slayer's Rise, which uses **Voidlight Marl** for **cosmetic-only** rewards (no
combat gear, no rated ilvl).
