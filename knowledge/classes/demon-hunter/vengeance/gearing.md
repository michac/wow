Vengeance is a **tank**; gearing is survivability-first. Haste is the top
secondary in both raid and M+ (damage + cooldown reduction on [Soul Fragment](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203795)
generators and [Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720), i.e. mitigation uptime). The rest of the
priority splits by content — see below. **Always sim your own gear**; a static
stat priority is only a starting point.

## ⚠ 12.1 weapon requirement — do NOT equip daggers

12.1 made **Demon Hunters able to equip daggers** (for Devourer's Intelligence
weapons) and, in the same pass, gated Vengeance's core buttons on weapon type:

> **Fracture and Soul Cleave now require equipped Warglaives, Axes, Swords, and
> Fist Weapons.** (Tier-1 patch notes)

So a dagger in either hand **disables your builder and your main spender** —
and, transitively, the tier-set bonuses that key off them. Vengeance stays on
**Warglaives / Axes / Swords / Fist Weapons**; treat any dagger that drops as
Devourer loot, never a Vengeance upgrade regardless of item level. (Havoc got
the same restriction on Demon Blades / Blade Dance / Chaos Strike.)

## Stat priority

**Raid** (maxroll, sim-verify): Haste is the most sought-after stat (damage +
CDR on soul-fragment generators and [Demon Spikes](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=203720)). **Versatility is slightly
better than Critical Strike** for survivability in raid — more consistent, and
many bosses have tank busters / unparryable hits, so crit's parry value is
reduced. Prefer Versatility for progress raiding.

**Mythic+** (maxroll, sim-verify): Haste again top. **Critical Strike is highly
valued in M+** — it boosts damage substantially and increases parry, which is a
large amount of damage reduction when taking many melee hits.

**12.1 context for these weights** (Tier-1 notes; *reasoning below is ours, not
sourced*): the patch raised **player health and creature damage by 25% at max
level** and re-tuned tank self-healing, and Vengeance's damage→healing
conversions all went up — **Soul Cleave, Fel Devastation and Feast of Souls
healing +25%**, **Charred Warblades 5% of Fire damage** (was 4%), **Frailty 10%
of damage dealt to afflicted targets** (was 8%), **Revel in Pain shields for 6%
of Fire damage** (was 5%), on top of **all damage +5.5%**. More of your
mitigation is now scaled off damage dealt, which nudges throughput stats and
Leech up relative to flat avoidance. **Sim it** before rebuilding gear around it.

**Tertiary stats** (from the capture):
- **Avoidance** — reduces AoE damage taken; heavy diminishing returns starting at
  ~20% (2000 rating), no hard cap. **Best tertiary for raid** min-maxing survival.
- **Leech** — instantly returns health from outgoing damage/healing (1% Leech =
  1% of damage & healing returned). **Best tertiary for M+**; also better than
  Avoidance in raid on fights with many long-lived, hard-to-tank adds.
- **Speed** — movement speed.

All secondary stats are subject to [diminishing returns](https://maxroll.gg/wow/resources/stat-diminishing-returns).

## Tier set — Midnight Season 2 (Abyssal Doomhound's, Venomous Abyss)

The Season 2 Demon Hunter set is **Abyssal Doomhound's** (The Venomous Abyss).
The set bonuses changed completely from Season 1 — this is a **Sigil of Flame**
set, not a Fracture set:

- **2-Set:** [Sigil of Flame](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=204596)'s damage over time is increased by 50%.
  [Soul Cleave](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=228477) increases the duration of Sigil of Flame on your primary
  target by 2 sec.
- **4-Set:** [Immolation Aura](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=258920) and [Sigil of Spite](https://www.wowhead.com/spell=390163) deal 100% increased damage
  against targets affected by your Sigil of Flame.

*(Tier 3, corroborated across maxroll + Icy Veins. ⚠ The maxroll **raid**
capture's tier-set box still prints the **Season 1** bonuses — Fracture +35% /
Fracture detonation — under a "Season 2 Tier Sets" heading; the M+ capture and
every other S2 listing carry the Sigil of Flame text. We treat the raid box as
stale, not as a second opinion.)*

**Season 1 set, for recognition only** (Devouring Reaver's): 2-Set Fracture
damage +35%; 4-Set Fracture had a 30% chance to detonate for Fire damage. Any
guide still quoting those is describing last season.

Tier slots in the S2 BiS list: **Shoulder, Chest, Gloves, Legs** (Head and Cloak
BiS are non-tier drops — see the table). Class-set armor is also buyable from
**Kirana**, who **moved to next to the Catalyst in Silvermoon** in 12.1 and now
stocks Season 2 sets for **Slumbering Coil Curios**; the Catalyst itself now
**inherits the source item's secondary and tertiary stats plus certain cantrip
effects** when converting (Tier 1 — see `endgame/catalyst.md`).

## Embellishments & crafted gear

- **1x** [Hunter's Ritual Stone](https://www.wowhead.com/item=273060) — best overall choice of stat budget.
- **1x** [Arcanoweave Lining](https://www.wowhead.com/item=240166)

**Remaining Sparks:** crafted items are **331** item level vs **334** for regular
items at max ilvl, so it's a small loss to equip crafted gear outside your 2x
embellishments unless you lack high-ilvl gear in that slot (maxroll, sim-verify).
That 334 ceiling agrees with the Tier-1 crest bands — Season 2 runs **269 → 334**
(Myth Mistcrest upgrades 321–334); see `_meta/moving-values.md`. **Crafting
Sparks began dropping in the pre-season week.**
