### Opener for Retribution Paladin

For your opener, you will want to cast the abilities in the order listed here before moving on to the regular rotation priority. Your opener is the same in both single-target and AoE scenarios.

  1. Cast  Blade of Justice.
  2. Cast  Avenging Wrath.
  3. Use  Potion of Recklessness and any on-use trinkets.
  4. Cast  Execution Sentence.
  5. Cast  Wake of Ashes.
  6. Cast  Hammer of Light.
  7. Cast  Final Verdict.
  8. Cast  Final Verdict ( Divine Storm on AoE).
  9. Cast  Divine Toll.
  10. Continue with the regular priority.



Your goal in your opener should be to start generating Holy Power before your cooldowns and then begin your burst window.

Retribution follows a priority for its rotation, as listed below. Start from the top and work your way down the list until you reach an ability with all criteria met, and that will be the best one to cast for the situation.

  2. Cast  Execution Sentence.
  3. Cast  Hammer of Light if it is castable from using  Wake of Ashes.

## Midnight Season 2 Tier Set

The Season 2 Tier Set bonus increases the proc rate of  Divine Purpose and increases the damage of other Holy Power spenders after consuming a proc. To use it optimally you'll want to weave in  Divine Storm casts after consuming  Divine Purpose on single-target and  Final Verdict casts on AoE.

## Class and Spec Mechanics Explained

Resource Management Apex Talents

3.1.

### Resource Management

You need to manage two resources when playing Retribution, **Holy Power** and **time**. Most of your damage comes by generating and spending Holy Power, and increasing the efficiency of this loop will increase your damage in turn. To optimize Holy Power generation, you need to consider both the cooldowns of generators and periodic effects that provide it, like  Crusading Strikes.

Minimizing wasting Holy Power should be kept in mind at all times. You do not want to continue to generate Holy Power when you already have 5 just to put an ability on cooldown, and you need to consider whether you will gain Holy Power within a GCD before you use a generator to reach 5. With current design, waste is unavoidable, especially with effects like  Walk Into Light, so try to minimize avoidable waste while being aware that some kinds of waste are inevitable.

Currently Retribution is willing to spend Holy Power at a higher priority than it has historically, due to high rate of passive Holy Power generation. Other than keeping abilities like  Wake of Ashes on cooldown as fast as possible and spending an  Art of War proc at 2 stacks, you can simply spend Holy Power as soon as possible.

3.2.

### Apex Talents

The Apex Talent for Retribution,  Light Within, increases the power of  Art of War procs. You will want to use  Blade of Justice as soon as possible with two stacks of  Art of War, even at 4 Holy Power, but should spend first at 5.

## Important Cooldowns for Retribution Paladin

Avenging Wrath Execution Sentence Trinkets

4.1.

### Avenging Wrath

Avenging Wrath increases damage and Critical Strike chance of all abilities, so it should be used before other cooldowns. It also converts  Judgment into  Hammer of Wrath, upgrading its effects. With  Crusade, it provides a stacking Haste buff, but stacking this doesn't need to be prioritized over all other abilities as it used to be, since the damage and Crit bonuses remain flat.

4.2.

### Execution Sentence

Execution Sentence deals initial AoE damage, then after 10s deals damage to the primary target based on damage done to all initial targets hit. On AoE, you should continue using AoE abilities as normal for its duration. You should use this before other cooldowns that deal significant damage like  Wake of Ashes.

4.3.

### Trinket Usage

There are two types of on-use trinkets that you will use, trinkets that just deal damage and trinkets that provide a stat bonus. For trinkets that provide a stat bonus, usage is straightforward; you should use them with  Avenging Wrath unless the encounter will end before  Avenging Wrath will come off cooldown if their cooldowns do not naturally line up. You can use multiple on-use stat trinkets by simply using them with alternating cooldown sets.

For on-use damage trinkets, usage can depend on the trinket.  Avenging Wrath does not buff trinket damage, but it does increase the critical strike chance of the damage. For instant on-use damage from trinkets you will want to make sure you use it inside  Avenging Wrath.
