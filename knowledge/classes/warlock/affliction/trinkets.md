## Using them: the Darkglare snapshot rule

**On-use stat trinkets snapshot Summon Darkglare.** Darkglare reads your stats
instantly at summon, and only updates slowly once it is already out — so press
the trinket (and potion, and racial) **right before** Darkglare, never after
(maxroll, both guides).

✅ **New in 12.1 (Tier 1):** the **Cooldown Manager now tracks trinkets, potions
and racial cooldowns/durations**, and trinkets / health potions / combat potions
/ healthstones can be **pinged** from it. Lining an on-use trinket up with
Darkglare no longer needs an addon.

## Does it matter at a given gear level?

- **Choice matters at any ilvl** — good vs bad trinket is routinely
  **10%+ throughput**, far more than the ~6–7 ilvl between trinket ranks.
- **Season 2 gear-track bands (Tier 1 — `CurrencyTypes` DB2 @ 12.1.0.69214, via
  `_meta/moving-values.md`):** Adventurer **269–282** · Veteran **282–295** ·
  Champion **295–308** · Hero **308–321** · Myth **321–334**. Season 2 gear
  therefore spans **269 → 334**, a clean +45 shift off Season 1's 224 → 289.
- **Coming out of Season 1:** a fully-crested S1 character (Hero ~276 / Myth
  ~289) sits at the *bottom* of the S2 ladder. Season 2 Adventurer gear starts
  at **269**, and pre-season delves cap at **Adventurer 3/6** — so this week the
  realistic trinket upgrades come from the **S2 dungeon pool**, and the number
  worth chasing is the **Mythic 0 weekly lockout at Champion 1/6 (292)**
  (Tier 1). Heroic runs the same trinket table but Blizzard published no ilvl for
  the Heroic track — do not quote 292 for Heroic. The **Tidebound Grotto** lair
  on World difficulty (279, Veteran 1/6) is the other pre-season door, and it is
  the only route to Wavecaller's Seastone.
- **For a personal answer, run the tools, don't re-derive here:**
  `wowkb.plan --gear --character <name>` and the `/plan-character` command union
  the PlannerState dump + Blizzard API + Syndicator and already know what you
  have equipped.
