# Mythic+ — Midnight Season 1

> ⛔ **SEASON 1 IS OVER.** It ended when realms went down for the **week of
> 2026-08-11** maintenance (the 12.1 "Curse of Ula'tek" patch day). Everything
> below is a **historical record of a closed season** — the dungeon pool, the
> affix ladder and the reward tables described here are **not what is live**.
>
> For what is live now, see [`season-2-overview.md`](season-2-overview.md).
> Note that 12.1 shipped in two steps: the week of **Aug 11** is an official
> **pre-season week** (no keystones drop, new pool on Heroic / Mythic 0 only),
> and **Mythic+ Season 2 opens the week of 2026-08-18**.

Season 1 ran **2026-03-17 → 2026-08-11**; M+ itself unlocked the week of
**2026-03-24**. Roughly 21 weeks.

published a running "moving target" thread through the last weeks of the
season; these are the **final** posted values (posted 2026-08-04, last updated

## Practicing as Follower Dungeons

Follower Dungeons (Normal difficulty, AI companions; no affixes/timer) only
cover **Dragonflight / War Within / Midnight** dungeons. Of the S1 M+ pool,
the **4 Midnight-native** dungeons had a Follower version:

| S1 M+ Dungeon | Follower Dungeon? |
|---|---|
| Magisters' Terrace | ✅ (Midnight follower pool) |
| Maisara Caverns | ✅ |
| Nexus-Point Xenas | ✅ |
| Windrunner Spire | ✅ |
| Algeth'ar Academy | ⚠️ DF follower pool only (DF tuning/level, not Midnight) |
| Seat of the Triumvirate (Legion) | ❌ no follower version |
| Skyreach (WoD) | ❌ |
| Pit of Saron (WotLK) | ❌ |

Full Midnight follower pool (8): Windrunner Spire, Murder Row, Den of
Nalorakk, Maisara Caverns, The Blinding Vale, Magister's Terrace,
Nexus-Point Xenas, Voidscar Arena. Useful for learning layout/routes/boss
mechanics blind — **not** for M+ affix/tuning practice. Soft cap ~10/day
per account early-expansion. (Icy Veins follower-dungeon guide, upd.
2026-02-21; verified 2026-06-13.)

⚠️ Still relevant in S2 for the four Midnight dungeons that carried into the
new pool (Murder Row, Den of Nalorakk, The Blinding Vale, Voidscar Arena) —
those have follower versions and are now the ones worth learning blind.

## Affixes (Season 1 set — retired)

| Key level | Affixes |
|-----------|---------|
| +2 to +4 | **Lindormi's Guidance** (newcomer-help affix, teaches routes) |
| +5 to +11 | One rotating **Xal'atath's Bargain** (Ascendant / Voidbound / Pulsar / Devour) + Fortified **or** Tyrannical |
| +7 to +10 | Fortified/Tyrannical rotate weekly |
| +10 and up | Fortified **and** Tyrannical both active |
| +12 and up | **Xal'atath's Guile** replaces all Bargains; deaths more punishing |

The table above is the **Season 1** ladder. **Whether it carries into Season 2
is not settled** — the Tier-1 12.1 notes contain no affix section at all, while
the Tier-3 pre-season S2 guides (Icy Veins, Conquest Capped) describe the *same*
ladder carrying over with no new affix. Neither claim is confirmed; treat
[`affixes.md`](affixes.md) as the file of record, which needs live confirmation
once keys drop the week of 2026-08-18.

## Rewards (Season 1 values — superseded)

Vault cap was at **+10** (272 ilvl). Season 1's crest currency was the
**Dawncrest** family; Season 2's is **Mistcrests**, and every S1 crest source
has been re-pointed. See [`../dawncrests.md`](../dawncrests.md) and
[`../great-vault.md`](../great-vault.md) for the current tables.

12.0.5 notes (SignsOfKelani, 2026-04-26) — **all historical**:

- **+6 and up**: hero-track end-of-run drops.
- **+9 and up**: Myth Dawncrests every run (fastest myth-crest income).
- **+10 and up**: vault gave myth-track; **Nebulous Voidcore bonus
  rolls gave myth-track here** (`../../systems/void-forge.md`) — 2 cores
  + 1 vault slot = up to 3 myth pieces/week without ever timing higher.
- ~2 runs/hour with RNG loot vs ritual sites' ~1 guaranteed hero piece
  per ~5 runs — armor-stacked premades traded loot, pugs often didn't.

⚠️ **Season 1 Nebulous Voidcores converted to gold at the end of S1** and can
no longer be spent on S1 content. From the start of Season 2 Voidcores become a
**Great Vault reward** instead, the raid re-roll cost drops to 1, and bonus
rolls are **not** available in the first S2 vault — they arrive the week of
**Aug 25** and need at least 3 vault panes unlocked.
