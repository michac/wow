# M+ Rating & Seasonal Rewards — Midnight Season 2

> ⚠ **Pre-season week (Aug 11–17, 2026): there is no M+ rating to earn.**
> 12.1 went live 2026-08-11, but **Mythic Keystones do not drop and Mythic+
> difficulties are not available until maintenance the week of 2026-08-18**.
> This week the new dungeons exist on Heroic and Mythic 0 only (M0 on a
> **weekly** lockout for this week). Everything in the "Season 2 reward
> thresholds" section below becomes chaseable **on Aug 18**, not today.
> Season 1's ladder closed with the week-of-Aug-11 maintenance — it is
> archived at the bottom of this file.

New to M+? Your **Mythic+ Rating** (often called "IO" after the addon
raider.io) is the single number that tracks your dungeon progress. Almost
every reward below is gated on that number. This file explains how it's
earned and what each threshold unlocks. For how keys themselves work
(first key, upgrades, the resilient floor) see `keystones.md`; for loot
mechanics see `loot.md` and `../great-vault.md`.

## Mythic+ Rating (the score)

**Per-dungeon score.** Each completed run gives a base score plus a bonus
per keystone level and per affix. Completing a dungeon at all is worth a
base of **37.5**, and each keystone level adds **7.5** on top (so higher
keys = higher score). **Timing** the dungeon (beating the dungeon's
timer) gives the full score and upgrades your key; **over-time / depleting**
(finishing after the timer) still completes and scores the run, but you get
no time bonus and **no key upgrade** — so a timed run is worth meaningfully
more than the same level over-time. Your rating only goes **up**: a worse
run never lowers your score, so there is no penalty for trying a key and
finishing it slow. *(Formula measured in Season 1; no 12.1 note changes it.)*

**Single score per dungeon.** In pre-Midnight seasons you kept two separate
best runs per dungeon — one on a Fortified week, one on a Tyrannical week —
combined roughly 1.5x / 0.5x. Midnight ended that at the level that matters:
from **+10 and up, Fortified AND Tyrannical are both active on every run,
every reset**, so each dungeon contributes a **single best timed run**, not
two affix-split scores. (At +7–9 you still get only one of Fortified /
Tyrannical, but those levels are below where serious rating is built.)
⚠ **The Season 2 affix set has not been re-verified for 12.1** — see
`affixes.md`; treat the ladder below as carried-forward Season 1 behavior
until confirmed live.

**Total rating** is the **sum of your best score in each of the 8 dungeons**
in the current pool. The **Season 2 pool** (live from 2026-08-18) is:

| Dungeon | Origin |
|---|---|
| **Altar of Fangs** | **new** (Vaults of Atal'Utek, Coiled Isle) |
| Murder Row | Midnight |
| Den of Nalorakk | Midnight |
| The Blinding Vale | Midnight |
| Voidscar Arena | Midnight |
| Ruby Life Pools | Dragonflight (returning, with design/QoL updates) |
| Kings' Rest | Battle for Azeroth (returning, with design/QoL updates) |
| Temple of Sethraliss | Battle for Azeroth (returning, with design/QoL updates) |

To raise your total efficiently you push your **lowest-scoring dungeon**,
not your best one — every dungeon counts equally. **All eight Season 1
dungeons rotated out** (Magisters' Terrace, Maisara Caverns, Nexus-Point
Xenas, Windrunner Spire, Algeth'ar Academy, Seat of the Triumvirate,
Skyreach, Pit of Saron) and no longer contribute score.

## Season 2 reward thresholds

### Dungeon teleport portals
Timing a specific dungeon at **+10 or higher (within the timer)** unlocks a
**permanent teleport** to that dungeon's entrance ("Keystone Hero:
<Dungeon>"). Eight dungeons = eight teleports, and they are **kept
permanently** — they do not expire when the season ends. Season 1's eight
portals stay earned; Season 2's are a fresh set of eight.

### Titles, mounts and achievements (rating-gated)
| Achievement | Rating | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight Keystone Conqueror: Season 2 | **1,500** | Title **"the Venomous"** |
| Midnight Keystone Master: Season 2 | **2,000** | Mount **Breath of Blight** |
| Midnight Keystone Hero: Season 2 | **2,500** | Criterion toward "Sssensational!" *(reward item **Insidious Venomstone** per secondary sources — unverified)* |
| Midnight Keystone Legend: Season 2 | **3,000** | Mount **Breath of Ruin** |
| Midnight Keystone Myth: Season 2 | **not announced** | Title **"the Venomous Contender"** |

Percentile titles, awarded **at the end of the season** against the final
regional ladder (not a fixed number you can chase):

| Achievement | Cutoff | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| Venomous Champion: Season 2 | regional **top 1%** | Feat of Strength |
| Venomous Hero: Season 2 | regional **top 0.1%** | Title **"the Venomous Hero"** |

Notes:
- **1500 / 2000 / 3000 and the two mounts are Tier-1 confirmed** (Blizzard's
  Season 2 announcement); Icy Veins corroborates 2000 and 3000.
- The **Keystone Myth threshold is genuinely unannounced.** Secondary guides
  note Season 1's Myth tier sat at **3,400** and that Season 2 may reuse it —
  do **not** treat 3,400 as a Season 2 number until it's confirmed.
- **Breath of Blight** and **Breath of Ruin** are the two headline seasonal
  mounts (skeletal birds) and are **seasonal** — they stop being obtainable
  when Season 2 ends.

### Gear / Great Vault
**Gear is gated by keystone level, not rating**, but the two move together.
Vault rewards scale with the highest keys you time, and timing eight
dungeons in a week fills the M+ vault row. ⚠ Season 2 changed several vault
tracks (raid rows, the world row's pre-season cap, Voidcores as a vault
reward) — read **`../great-vault.md`** for the current table rather than
carrying a Season 1 ilvl over. End-of-run drop tracks are in `loot.md`.
Note also that during the **pre-season week the Great Vault pays out on your
final Season 1 week**, and Season 2 credit only starts accruing now.

## What to chase first (new player)

1. **Wait for Aug 18** — keys don't exist before then. Use the pre-season
   week for Heroic / Mythic 0 gearing and to learn the three returning
   dungeons (Kings' Rest, Temple of Sethraliss, Ruby Life Pools).
2. **Time every dungeon at +2 once.** Builds a base score in all 8 and
   teaches routes.
3. **Get all 8 dungeons timed and climbing together** — push your lowest
   dungeon, since total rating is the sum across all 8.
4. **Hit +10 timed in each dungeon** → unlocks all 8 **permanent teleports**
   (huge quality-of-life) and starts feeding the top vault row.
5. **Reach 2,000 rating → Keystone Master** → the **Breath of Blight**
   seasonal mount. This is the main goal for most players.
6. **Past KSM:** push toward **2,500 (Keystone Hero)** and **3,000
   (Keystone Legend, Breath of Ruin)** if you want the harder rewards.

|---|---|---|

| Achievement | Rating | Reward |
|---|---|---|

| Keystone Master: Season One | 2,000 | Mount **Calamitous Carrion** |

Windrunner Spire) drop from those dungeons and are **not** score-gated, so
they remain farmable outside the M+ rotation.
