# Weekly Checklist — Midnight **pre-season week** (12.1, level 90)

Weekly reset: **Tuesday** (US). This is the anchor doc for "what should I do
this week?" questions.

> ⚠ **This week is not a normal week.** 12.1 "Curse of Ula'tek" went live
> **2026-08-11** into an official **pre-season week**. **Midnight Season 2 does
> not open until Tuesday 2026-08-18.** Most of what you'll read about 12.1 —
> Mythic+ keystones, the Venomous Abyss raid, Bountiful Delves, Nightmare Prey,
> rated PvP — is **not live today**. Everything below is split into *live now*
> and *opens Aug 18*. Don't plan a week off the Season 2 column.

## Live now — week of 2026-08-11, in rough priority order

1. **Open last week's Great Vault.** Its rewards come from your **final Season 1
   week** — this is the S1 vault, not an S2 one. Season 2 vault credit starts
   accruing **now**, claimable Aug 18 (see `great-vault.md`).
2. **Mythic 0 dungeons — the headline of the week.** The new Season 2 dungeon
   pool is live on **Heroic and Mythic 0**, and **M0 is on a WEEKLY lockout this
   week only**, dropping **Champion 1/6 (ilvl 292)**. That makes 8 one-and-done
   Champion drops the best gear-per-hour in the pre-season, and it does not
   repeat — M0 returns to a **daily** lockout on Aug 18. Pool: **Altar of Fangs**
   (new, 3 bosses, inside the Vaults of Atal'Utek) · Murder Row · Den of Nalorakk ·
   The Blinding Vale · Voidscar Arena · **Ruby Life Pools** · **Kings' Rest** ·
   **Temple of Sethraliss**.
   **There are no Mythic keystones this week** — keys do not drop until Aug 18.
3. **Tidebound Grotto — the new Lair** (Coiled Isle, 1 boss: **Nymrissa
   Wavecaller**). **World difficulty only** this week: queue in solo, clear a
   two-part scenario while the instance fills, boss scales 5–40 players. Entrance
   is **underwater** below the isle, with a summoning stone like a Delve.
   Rec. ilvl **273 → drops 279 (Veteran 1/6)** + Veteran Mistcrest. Loot is
   **bind-on-pickup** and the lair is on a **weekly lockout** (see `lairs.md`).
4. **The Coiled Isle** — the new zone, and where the week's open-world time goes:
   - **Curse Surges** spawn rare elites at **five rotating locations**; killing
     the rare unlocks **Venom Fishing** at that spot.
   - **Vaults of Atal'Utek** — group content + rotating public events that build
     to a boss fight.
   - **Altar of Corrosion** — the zone's own talent tree (player power + QoL,
     e.g. blunting the isle's venom), fed by **Corrosive Coins**. (*Spirit of
     Corrosion* I/II are separate one-off grants at renown 8 and 14.)
   - **Zul'jarra's Forces** — the new renown faction, **20 ranks**; quartermaster
     **Jan'sari the Watchful** at **Tokka's Landing**. Renown starts accruing
     *before* landfall from the Zul'Aman quests with Lady Liadrin, Orweyna and
     Zul'jarra. (See `../systems/coiled-isle.md`, `../factions/zuljarras-forces.md`.)
5. **Delves** — Tiers **1–11** plus the **"?" Nemesis** difficulty.
   **No Bountiful Delves this week**, and the **maximum delve reward is
   Adventurer 3/6 gear + Veteran Mistcrests**. **Coffer Keys do not drop until
   Aug 18.** Three new delves: **The Ring of Glory**, **Gnarldor Isle**,
   **Venomfall Deeps** (Nemesis). Coffer Key Shard rates were retuned toward
   Coiled Isle content and Blizzard calls that tuning explicitly ongoing — treat
   any specific shard number as volatile (see `delves/overview.md`).
6. **Prey — Normal and Hard modes only.** **Hard Prey drops Season 2 Veteran
   gear.** Nightmare mode is **off** this week, so the 3-Nightmare-hunt weekly
   objective is not completable; it returns Aug 18 (see `prey.md`).
7. **Pinnacle Caches** are up during the pre-season and drop **Season 2 Veteran**
   gear.
8. **Crafting Sparks begin dropping during the pre-season** — the Liadrin
   world-event weekly and the spark pillars are worth doing again immediately
   (see `../systems/professions.md`).
9. **Ritual Sites** — retuned for Season 2: tiers 1–6 vault rewards now match
   **Season 2 Delve tiers 1–6**, and the sites award **Season 2 crests** at
   Delve-equivalent rates. Tiers 1–3 keep their Season 1 recommended ilvls;
   new recommended ilvls are **T4 259** (was 257) · **T5 268** (was 264) ·
   **T6 275** (was 274). ⚠ The **Tier 6 Advanced Ritual Studies quests no longer
   offer a Nebulous Voidcore bonus roll** — they remain completable for the
   achievement (see `../systems/ritual-sites.md`).
10. **Void Assault weekly** — rotates between Eversong Woods and Zul'Aman;
    requires the Void Incursion intro questline once. Void Strikes, Void
    Incursions and the weekly quest now pay **Adventurer Mistcrests** (the
    Season 2 Adventurer crest).
11. **Val and Naigtal** — world quests, rares and elites now grant **Adventurer
    Mistcrests** in both Normal and Heroic World Tier. Rare equipment stays
    Warbound-until-Equipped and now drops at **S2 Adventurer 1/6** (Normal) /
    **Adventurer 4/6** (Heroic). The World Boss + weekly quests give **Adventurer
    Mistcrests** (Normal) / **Veteran Mistcrests** (Heroic).
    ⚠ **World Boss drops remain Season 1 items and can no longer be upgraded** —
    same for the Mythic quest rewards from "Knocking off the Top (Heroic)". Kill
    it for the crests and the quest, not for the loot (see `world-events.md`).
12. **Housing weekly** — quest from **Vaeli**, outside the Silvermoon bank.
    12.1 adds **Blueprints** (export/import a whole house or a single room),
    **Pet Beds**, houses up to **level 12**, and four new neighborhood
    **Endeavors** (see `../systems/housing.md`).
13. **Renown** — now **six** factions: Silvermoon Court, Amani Tribe, The Hara'ti,
    The Singularity, Slayer's Rise, and **Zul'jarra's Forces** (new).
14. **PvP — unrated only this week.** Battlegrounds and outdoor PvP are live, plus
    the new **Training Grounds: Arenas** (3v3 vs bots, Group Finder → PvP tab).
    All rated PvP is off until Aug 18.
15. **Trading Post** — check the month's offerings / complete the traveler's log.

**No current-tier raid this week.** The Season 1 raids (The Voidspire, The
Dreamrift, March on Quel'Danas, Sporefall) remain open but are previous-tier;
the Venomous Abyss opens Aug 18. Note the class-set vendor **Kirana has moved**
from the March on Quel'Danas entrance to **next to the Catalyst in Silvermoon**,
and now also stocks **Season 2** class-set armor for **Slumbering Coil Curios**.
The **Catalyst** itself now carries over the source item's **secondary and
tertiary stats plus certain cantrip effects** when converting (see `catalyst.md`).

⚠ **Every spec and every instance is retuned this week, even the ones with no
content change.** Four 12.1 changes sit above the per-class notes and apply
game-wide: **player health and creature damage are both +25% at max level**
(health consumables rescaled; several DPS/Tank healing and absorb spells
adjusted to keep their relative impact) · **major DPS cooldowns lowered and
steady-state damage raised** for several specs · **interrupts now show a
"missed" visual and sound** when the target wasn't casting · **diminishing-return
categories reset after 20s** (was 16). So "nothing changed in that dungeon/raid"
means *no instance-specific change* — your throughput, survivability and any
absolute HP number written before 2026-08-11 have all moved.

Also new and quietly useful: the **Omnium Folio intro questline is now
account-skippable** once any one character has finished it, and the **Amani Pass**
between Eversong and Zul'Aman no longer dismounts you.

  **"Let Me Solo Him: Azta'rec"**.

  **"A Slithering Threat"** questline, four new serpent targets, **Ral'kala,
  Terror of the Isle** (burn **Ossified Relics** at **Haunted Braziers** in
  Nightmare mode — group content), **Afflicted Souls** (Champion-track) and

  Champion 3/6** for that first vault; **Hero 1/6** thereafter. Raid vault rewards
  now come in at the first step of the *next harder* difficulty's track (a Heroic
  raid vault reward is **Myth 1/6**).
- **Week of Aug 25** — **Voidcore bonus rolls return** to the vault; they are
  **not** in the first S2 vault and require **at least 3 panes unlocked**.
  Two Voidcore rules that are **current in Season 2**, not historical: the

- **Week 8 of Season 2** — **Orin Straylight** (relocated **near the Catalyst in
  Silvermoon**) provides **one extra Nebulous Voidcore per week**.
