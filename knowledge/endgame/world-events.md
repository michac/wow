## The Coiled Isle — new outdoor content (12.1)

The new zone's outdoor loop lives in `systems/coiled-isle.md`; the world-event
surface of it is:

- **Curse Surges** — regularly spawn rare elites at **five rotating locations**
  across the isle. Killing a rare elite **unlocks Venom Fishing** at that
  location and feeds the local story with the tortollan sea captain **Tokka**.
- **Vaults of Atal'Utek** — group content with **rotating public events** that
  build to a boss fight. Live from the pre-season week.
- **Zul'jarra's Forces** — the zone's renown faction (20 ranks; quartermaster
  **Jan'sari the Watchful** at **Tokka's Landing**). See
  `factions/zuljarras-forces.md`.

## Lairs — the new world-boss format (12.1)

**Lairs** are an **instanced** evolution of world bosses and are the format
Blizzard is building outdoor boss content on going forward. They sit at fixed
locations like Delves, with a **summoning stone outside**; difficulties are
**World / Normal / Heroic / Mythic (flexible 15–25)**. On **World** difficulty
you queue solo and play a two-part scenario (clear elites until the boss
appears) while the instance fills; the encounter **scales 5–40 players**. Loot
is **bind-on-pickup**, on a **weekly lockout**, and a Voidcore may be spent
**once per week per lair**.

- **Tidebound Grotto** (Coiled Isle, 1 boss: **Nymrissa Wavecaller**, a naga
  sorceress; entrance is **underwater** below the isle) is the first lair.
- **Pre-season week (now):** **World difficulty only**, dropping **Season 2
  Veteran** gear.
- **From Aug 18:** Normal, Heroic and flexible Mythic open.

Full reward table, mechanics and lockout detail: `endgame/lairs.md`.

⚠ This does **not** delete the existing outdoor world bosses (Lu'ashal,
Thorm'belan, Predaxas, Cragpine; Val/Naigtal's Pertinax and Leth'ir) — they
still spawn. What changed is that their **loot is frozen at Season 1** (below)
while Lairs carry the Season 2 track, so Lairs are where the gearing value now
is. See `planning/activities/world-boss.md`.

## Val & Naigtal — the two rotating worlds (added 12.0.7 "Revelations")

A portal in **Voidstorm** leads to the two worlds. The portal is **unstable and
switches destinations every week**, so only one world is reachable per week,
rotating its set of challenges.

- **Naigtal** — a fungal, arcane-rich world held by the insidious ethereal
  faction **Hal'hadar**. World boss: **Nexus-Captain Leth'ir** (faction leader
  stand-in for the showdown).
- **Val** — an icy world once inhabited by the Legion, now a bastion of renegade
  void forces under **Domanaar Imperator Pertinax**. World boss: **Imperator
  Pertinax**, found inside the **Void Acropolis** (enter via the Void Portal to
  the south of Val).

Each world offers **World Quests, rares, and events**, culminating in the
world-boss showdown. Weekly quests: **"Showdown on Val"** / **"Showdown on
Naigtal"** (whichever world is active that week), rewarding a **Riftstalker's
Cache** (Field Accolades, **Relic Coffer Key shards**, upgrade materials, gold).

### 12.1 retier — what changed on 2026-08-11

Every **crest** payout in Val/Naigtal moved to Season 2, but the **world-boss
gear did not** — that asymmetry is the whole story of this zone in 12.1:

| Source | 12.1 reward |
|---|---|
| World Quests, Rares, Elites | **Season 2 Adventurer crests** — in **both** Normal *and* Heroic World Tier |
| Rare equipment drops | still **Warbound Until Equipped**, now at **S2 Adventurer 1/6** (Normal WT) / **S2 Adventurer 4/6** (Heroic WT) |
| World Boss + Weekly Quests (crests) | **S2 Adventurer crests** (Normal WT) / **S2 Veteran crests** (Heroic WT) |
| World Boss **gear drops** | ⚠ **remain Season 1 items and can no longer be upgraded** |
| "Knocking off the Top (Heroic)" Mythic quest reward | ⚠ **remains a Season 1 reward and can no longer be upgraded** |

**Read this as: farm Val/Naigtal for crests and Warbound Adventurer catch-up
pieces, not for the world boss.** The world-boss kill is now a crest/emblem
errand; its loot is a dead-end Season 1 item you cannot put a crest into.

The Season 1 world-boss drop table is unchanged in *shape* — it is simply frozen.
For reference (set by the **June 30, 2026** hotfix, now historical): a
**Warbound Heroic 1/6** item on Normal WT or **Warbound Heroic 4/6** on Heroic WT
for the warband, **plus** a per-character **Soulbound** piece each kill —
**Champion 4/6** on Normal, **Heroic 1/6** on Heroic. Those are Season 1 tracks
and **no upgrade path exists for them in Season 2**.

**"Knocking Off the Top (Heroic)" — was the only open-world Myth reward, now
frozen:** on **Heroic World Tier**, each Val/Naigtal world-boss kill can award a
**Void Commander's Emblem** (item 277554), **one per weekly loot lockout**.
Collect **4** (≈4 weeks) to complete the quest (97256) for **one Myth 1/6 item,
ilvl 272 — your choice of Cloak, Belt, or Bracers** (same three slots for every
armor type). ⚠ **12.1 froze this reward at Season 1: it can no longer be
upgraded**, so a 272 Myth 1/6 piece stays 272. It is still worth finishing an
in-flight emblem set for a slot you have nothing in, but it is no longer a
Season 2 gearing path. (Quest/item IDs web-verified 2026-07-07 against Wowhead
quest 97256 / item 277554 + the Method Heroic World Tier guide; the freeze is
Tier-1 from the 12.1 notes.)

**Gearing currencies (12.1):**
- **Field Accolades** — earned from WQs, rares, the Showdown weekly, and Ritual
  Sites. Spent at **Maren Silverwing** (top of the Bazaar, Silvermoon City) on
  **Void-Touched Caches**, which 12.1 re-cut entirely: **the Season 1 gear caches
  have been removed**, replaced by a **Season 2 Adventurer Warbound cache for
  200 Accolades** and **Season 2 Veteran Bind-on-Pickup caches for 500** (random
  slot) and **750** (slot-specific). *(The 12.0.7 "slot-targeted Hero-track gear
  ~ilvl 259 for Accolades" line was that S1 stock and is gone with it.)* Accolades
  also still buy cosmetics/decor at **Fieldsmith Ventem** / **Zuronar** and the
  Val/Naigtal vendors (some priced in **Voidlight Marl**).
- **Relic Coffer Key shards** — assemble into keys that open **coffers** for gear.
  ⚠ 12.1 **retuned Coffer Key Shard amounts from multiple sources**, weighted
  toward Coiled Isle content, and Blizzard calls the tuning **ongoing and a work
  in progress** — treat any specific shard number as volatile.
- **Great Vault:** like Delves and Prey, Val/Naigtal world content **contributes to
  the World row** of the Great Vault (the same column delves fill — one shared
  counter, not a second row). ⚠ For the **first Season 2 vault** the World row is
  capped at **Champion 3/6**; **Hero 1/6** thereafter (see `endgame/great-vault.md`).
- **Rare farming:** rares **spawn frequently** and drop the Warbound-until-equipped
  gear retiered above; **Warp Riders** and **Blasktar Legion** enemies remain a
  dense crest source in Heroic WT (now paying **S2 Adventurer** crests like
  everything else here); **Dark Particles** drop in Val/Naigtal and **stack to 1000**.

See `planning/activities/val-naigtal.md` (zone farm) and `showdown-weekly.md`
(weekly capstone) for the planner-facing split.

## Timewalking — Turbulent Timeways (12.0.7) — ⛔ **ENDED 2026-08-11**

⛔ **This event is over.** Turbulent Timeways V ran **Jun 30 → the weekly reset on
Aug 11, 2026** and ended with the 12.1 maintenance. *Spawn of Vyranoth* is no
longer obtainable, and nothing below is actionable — it is kept as the historical
record of the event and its rewards. **No successor Timewalking event was
announced in the 12.1 notes**; if one appears it will come as a hotfix/blue post.
Remove it from any ranking (`planning/activities/turbulent-timeways.md`).

- **Dates:** Jun 30 – Aug 11, 2026 (six consecutive weeks). **Ended.**
- Timewalk a previous expansion each week, **Dragonflight bookended** (begins and
  ends with Dragonflight dungeons): Algeth'ar Academy, Halls of Infusion,
  Neltharus, Ruby Life Pools, The Azure Vaults, Brackenhide Hollow.
- Weekly Timewalking quest (**5 Timewalking dungeons**) grants a **Heroic Cache of
  Quel'Thalas Treasures** (ilvl **259–276**) — one Heroic-track piece per week.
- **XP buff loop:** 1st TW dungeon → *Knowledge of Timeways* (+5% XP); 4 dungeons →
  *Mastery of Timeways* (**+30% XP** to kills and quest turn-ins, 3h, **persists through
  death**; refresh by re-queuing). **Stacks with Darkmoon Faire** and other XP buffs, so
  banked delve/WQ quest turn-ins under Mastery are the fastest alt XP in the patch.
- **Mount:** *Spawn of Vyranoth* (new proto-drake, only from **Master of the Turbulent
  Timeways V**, achievement **61463**) requires earning **Mastery of Timeways in 4 of the
  event's 6 weeks** — you may **miss up to two** (the requirement was **reduced from 5 to 4**
  near launch, Wowhead #381738). Progress is **account-wide** and can be spread across alts;
  the achievement's week-counter criterion is what the planner/addon reads for progress.
- Note: this is a timewalking event, **not** a Mythic+ Season 1 structural change.
- Source note: the 4-of-6 requirement + IDs (achievement 61463, DF weekly quest 93497,
  cache item 250116) are tier-3 (Wowhead, 2026-07-09); mechanics corroborated by community
  guides (`yt:rliFXbEHghU` "Yeti", `yt:kUP8oqI7Ekc` "Two Angry Frogs"); the ilvl/window
  numbers are from the Revelations notes (tier-1). The older "4 of 5 / miss at most one"
  wording predates the launch reduction.

## Darkmoon Faire (monthly)

Runs the **first full week of every month** on **Darkmoon Island** — sets up on the
**first Sunday** and lasts ~7 days. **The August 2026 window (Aug 2–9) has closed;
next window ≈ Sep 6–13, 2026** (first Sunday = Sep 6). Enter via the portal
barkers outside Elwynn/Mulgore (or the monthly-recurring in-game prompt).

What it offers each month:
- **+5 profession skill** quest per profession — a small permanent skill bump.
- **Darkmoon rep/XP buff ("WHEE!"/carousel)** — +10% rep & XP for an hour. *(The
  old "stacks with Turbulent Timeways" alt-leveling overlap is gone — that event
  ended 2026-08-11.)*
- **Pet-battle** daily.
- **Darkmoon Prize Tickets** from carnival games + turn-in quests → **mounts, pets,
  heirlooms, transmog** at the ticket vendors.

Cadence/urgency: `time-boxed`, recurring → moderate FOMO (see
`planning/activities/darkmoon-faire.md`). ⚠ The only prior KB mention
(`endgame/weekly-checklist.md:117`) has **stale dates** — ignore them; this section +
the activity file are the live source.
