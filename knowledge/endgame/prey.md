# Prey (Midnight hunt system)

Repeatable hunt system introduced with Midnight. **Season 2 ("Prey: A
Slithering Threat") extends it onto the Coiled Isle.**

## ⚠ 12.1 timing — Prey ships in two steps

12.1 went live **2026-08-11** into a **pre-season week**; Midnight Season 2
opens with maintenance the week of **2026-08-18**. For Prey specifically
(Tier 1, "Season 1 Ending and Season 2 Information"):

| Now (week of Aug 11, pre-season) | Week of Aug 18 (Season 2) |
|---|---|
| **Normal + Hard Prey only.** Hard Prey drops **Season 2 Veteran** gear | **Nightmare Prey** returns, awarding **Season 2 Champion** gear |
| No Coiled Isle Nightmare hunts, no Ral'kala, no Curse of the Isle | *"A Slithering Threat"* questline, 4 new serpent targets, Ral'kala, **"The Curse of the Isle"** toggle |
| No Bountiful Delves ⇒ **no Afflicted / Tormented Souls** | Bountiful Delves return; T6+ Heavy Trunks drop the Souls |

Everything under "Season 2 additions" below is **upcoming, dated** — do not
plan around it before Aug 18.

## The basics (unchanged in 12.1)

- **Unlock**: Astalor Bloodsworn, Murder Row, Silvermoon City.
- **Where**: hunts across the 4 Midnight zones — **Eversong Woods, Zul'Aman,
  Harandar, Voidstorm** — plus, from Aug 18, **the Coiled Isle** (Season 2's
  serpent targets hunt there).
- **Weekly**: complete **3 Nightmare Prey hunts** for the weekly objective.
  (Not doable during pre-season week — Nightmare is off until Aug 18.
  @verify-ingame: what the weekly objective reads as during the pre-season week.)
- **Rewards**: **Season 2 crests** (12.1 re-pointed every Midnight crest source
  off Season 1 Dawncrests — see `dawncrests.md`), Restored Coffer Keys (delve
  chests), Great Vault slot progress. ⚠ Coffer Keys do **not** drop during
  pre-season, and 12.1 retuned Coffer Key Shard amounts across sources —
  Blizzard calls that tuning "ongoing and a work in progress."
- **The random gear map piece is low value once you out-gear its track.** Don't
  run Prey *for* the weekly gear roll — the durable draw is the **Nightmare
  terminal** and the Preyseeker's Journey ranks. That terminal, not the map, is
  why it stays on the list.

## Preyseeker's Journey (seasonal track)

- **10 ranks per season** (Season 2 track confirmed at 10 levels, Tier 1).
  Progress currency is `Preyseeker's Journey` (currency 3387); **a new reward
  unlocks every 4,000 progress**.
- Points: **first 4 hunts each week = 1,000 pts each; additional hunts =
  50 pts** (20× falloff — never grind past 4/week). 4 hunts = 4,000 = exactly
  one rank per week. *(Measured in S1; @verify-ingame that S2 keeps the same
  rates.)*
- **Season 1's Rank 4 → Nightmare difficulty**, via Astalor questline *Dark
  Mending* → *The Sheep or the Wolf*. Season 2's entry point is the
  *Prey: A Slithering Threat* questline from Astalor instead
  (@verify-ingame whether a rank gate still sits in front of it).
- ✅ **Nightmare hunts are available on a fresh 90 — the feared per-character Rank-4 gate
  does NOT block alts.** RESOLVED in-game 2026-07-10: a freshly-90 alt (Uncomplete) could run
  Nightmare Prey immediately (and jumped straight into T11 delves too). Whether that's because
  the Journey is account-wide or simply because everything unlocks at 90, the practical answer
  is the same: **an alt does not have to grind to Rank 4 itself before the "3 Nightmare hunts"
  weekly is doable.**
- One hunt per difficulty per zone weekly gives the full gear chest;
  repeats give a gear-less sack.
- **Nightmare rewards**: Champion-track gear (**Season 2 Champion** from Aug 18),
  Season 2 crests, Voidlight Marl, Tormented Tantalum chance — and, in Season 1,
  **Ascendant Voidshards** (`../systems/void-forge.md`). @verify-ingame whether
  Voidshards are still on the S2 Nightmare table or were replaced by an S2 mat.
- **Track rewards are bought, not granted.** Reaching a rank *unlocks* the item;
  you buy it from **Construct V'anore** (mounts / pets / transmog) or
  **Construct Ali'a** (housing decor, busts and effigies) at **Astalor's
  Sanctum, Silvermoon**, for **Remnant of Anguish**. The track itself is
  viewable in the Adventure Guide (Shift+J). *(Icy Veins also lists a third
  construct, E'nui — corroborate in game.)*
- 🟢 **12.1: Anguish costs for housing items at the Prey vendors were
  "substantially reduced," for both Season 1 and Season 2** (Tier 1, no numbers
  published). If you deferred a decor purchase on price, re-check it.

### Rank 10 — Custom Hunts (12.0.7 change, Season 1)

- **Rank 10** unlocks **Custom Hunts** via the *Preferential Killing* quest
  (generates a hunt with player-chosen parameters).
- **12.0.7 "Revelations":** the once-per-week cap on Custom Hunts is
  **removed**. After reaching rank 10 you may run Custom Hunts **as often as
  desired**; the first per week pays full rewards and **subsequent Custom
  Hunts in the same week give reduced rewards** (the old 20× falloff still
  governs farming past the weekly allowance).
- 12.0.7 also **increased hunt progression** generally and relaxed mode
  restrictions, so chaining hunts has less downtime than the 12.0.5 grind.
- @verify-ingame: whether Custom Hunts sit at Rank 10 of the **Season 2** track
  too, or whether S2 spends that rank on something else (Tier-3 reporting puts a
  mount at S2 rank 10).

## Season 2 additions — live from 2026-08-18

### The questline and the four targets

- **"Prey: A Slithering Threat"**, from Astalor in Silvermoon, opens the
  Season 2 track. **Nightmare Mode returns with four new serpent-themed
  targets** and hunts across the Coiled Isle (Tier 1).
- Target names (Tier 3, **corroborate in game**): **Janoa the Fang**, **Batani
  the Scaled**, **Kursak the Coiled**, and a fourth reported as *Kadani the
  Claw* (least-corroborated of the four). Killing all four is reported to award
  the *Prey: Coiled Nightmares* achievement.

### Ral'kala, Terror of the Isle — the group terminal

- Summoned by burning **Ossified Relics** at the **Haunted Braziers** around the
  Coiled Isle **while in Nightmare mode**. Blizzard explicitly designed it as
  **group content** — *"meant to be something you work with other players on."*
- **100 Ossified Relics** per summon, but the count is **pooled across the
  players at the brazier** (Tier 3 — the "100, shared" figure is the one number
  here worth re-checking in game, @verify-ingame).
- **Ossified Relics** come from skulls and slain creatures on the Coiled Isle
  and from Nightmare-hunt world quests; **Journey rank 3** adds bonus relic
  drops from Coiled Isle world quests. Spent relics are also reported to boost
  drop chances / repeatable currencies by 50%.
- Reported drops: the **Hexflame Reaver** mount and the **Pale Hexscale** battle
  pet — cosmetic, RNG, no gearing value.

### Afflicted / Tormented Souls — the delve ⇄ Prey crossover

- Both drop from **Heavy Trunks in Tier 6+ Bountiful Delves** (so: nothing
  during pre-season week, which has no Bountiful Delves).
- Used **during an active Nightmare hunt**: instantly reveals the next target's
  location (skipping the trap grind) **and** awards an extra piece of gear.
- **Afflicted Soul → Champion-track** bonus piece; **Tormented Soul →
  Hero-track**. **Once per week per character each** — i.e. up to two extra
  pieces a week, one of each track.
- Unlocked on the Journey at **rank 4** (Afflicted) and **rank 9** (Tormented)
  (Tier 3 — corroborate).
- ⚠ This is the actual reason to keep Prey on the list in S2: a Hero-track piece
  a week that no other outdoor activity hands out. Route Bountiful Delves
  **before** the Nightmare hunt so you have the Soul in hand.

### "The Curse of the Isle" — permanent Nightmare toggle

- A **toggleable, permanent Nightmare mode for the whole Coiled Isle**: it
  applies Nightmare affixes (and the danger) while you are **not** actively in a
  Prey hunt, for players wanting a harder open world.
- Toggled by speaking to the **Image of Astalor Bloodsworn at Tokka's Landing**
  once unlocked (Tier 3 — corroborate).
- Relevant because Ossified Relic gathering and brazier summons work in the
  Curse phase, not just inside a hunt.

## Season 1 close-out (historical)

- **Season 1 ended with maintenance the week of 2026-08-11.** Its Journey, its
  Dawncrest rewards and the Feat of Strength *Big Prey Hunter (Season 1)*
  (completing the Preyseeker's Journey in S1) are closed. **Season 1 vendor
  stock is still purchasable** — 12.1's Anguish price cut explicitly covers
  "Season 1 and Season 2" items, so unfinished S1 decor got cheaper, not gone.

## Value vs. the alternatives (12.0.7 measurement, re-check for S2)

- **12.0.7 buff:** experience earned from completing **Prey** quests is
  **significantly increased** (same pass that boosted first-time delves /
  Delver's Call, Midnight dungeon quests, and weekly Renown). Prey is a strong
  **leveling/Renown** activity, not just a gearing one.
- Per-hour, ritual sites still edged out prey for raw *gearing* (accolades +
  crests + coffer key shards + renown at once) — see `../systems/ritual-sites.md`
  (whose T4/T5/T6 recommended ilvls moved to **259 / 268 / 275** in 12.1). ⚠ The
  S2 Souls change the comparison in Prey's favour once Bountiful Delves return;
  this ranking has **not** been re-measured for 12.1.
- **Traps are no longer the fast path (CORRECTED 2026-07-10).** The readable 12.0.5 hotfix
  (Icy Veins, May 12 2026) did **not** reduce trap *count* — it **buffed non-trap progress**
  (Prey WQ, Ambushes/Ripostes, Coalescing Anguish spawns) and removed *Hunter's Momentum* on
  Hard/Nightmare. So pure trap-grinding is *relatively slower per minute*; ~10 traps to
  reveal the target is **normal**, not a mistake. (A separate Wowhead piece "Are Prey Traps
  Nerfed in 12.0.5?" was unreadable — whether count also dropped is unconfirmed; the readable
  hotfix says no. @verify-ingame). 12.0.7 further **increased hunt progression** and relaxed
  restrictions; typical hunt ~10–12 min.
- Still worth keeping:
  - **Weekly quest beacon** — awards a **Delver's Bounty map**. ⚠ **NOT an *extra* gear
    piece:** its Hidden Trove **shares the one-per-week reward lockout** with a delve-dropped
    Bounty map (see `delves/overview.md`). Using both in one week on one char = **one** trove,
    not two. Funnel spare maps to *other* characters.
  - Prey hunts accept **Nebulous Voidcore bonus rolls**. ⚠ 12.1: S1 Voidcores
    **converted to gold** at season end; S2 Voidcores come from the Great Vault
    and are **not available in the first S2 vault** (they arrive the week of
    Aug 25). So there is no Voidcore to spend on Prey until then.
  - Still a Liadrin spark-weekly objective option (3 hunts).

## Running a hunt efficiently (2026-07-10 loop; unchanged by 12.1)

The fast loop the guides converge on (T3 Icy Veins/Wowhead + T4, Midnight):

1. **Do the Prey World Quest first** — highest progress-per-minute source.
2. **Route the two uncontested trap hotspots.** Each zone has **3 fixed trap-spawn hotspots**;
   the crowded one is wherever the WQ marker points — **skip it**, hit the other two (less mobby).
   Carry up to 5 traps. *(hotspot mechanic = single T4 source — corroborate.)*
3. **Disarm-and-mount** between traps (spam mount key while disarming) to stay out of combat.
4. **Throw "Disarmed Trap" items at the ambush prey elite** — knocks ~50% HP and forces a
   flee/riposte. **The fix for slow single-target kills** (undergeared/tank).
5. **Kill the terminal target before Torment climbs** — the final arena **locks you in, no reset**.

From Aug 18, add: **hold an Afflicted/Tormented Soul** and pop it on the
Nightmare hunt to skip straight to the target — that is the intended shortcut
around steps 2–4 once a week.

### Torment — the stacking "Nightmare" debuff
- **+4% damage taken per stack, 1 stack / 60s**; on **Nightmare** it's **doubled and on a pure
  wall-clock timer** (decoupled from progress) — a 20-min hunt ≈ +80% damage taken. This is why
  slow hunts spiral.
- **Clear it:** **rested areas wipe all stacks** (pulling back to reset is legit); **Coalescing
  Anguish removes stacks** too. You **can't outrun the tick**, only dump it — and not in the
  final arena. Nightmare is **solo-only** for the 3-weekly (no group help) — note this does
  **not** apply to Ral'kala, which is deliberately group content.
- Nightmare affixes to know: **Bloody Command** (kill *any* creature within ~20s or take a
  heavy DoT — keep trash/critters in reach) and **Echo of Predation** (a fixating spirit — kite,
  it can't hit you while you reposition).

  of these two — the 12.1 notes reference new Prey challenges without listing
  the affix set.
