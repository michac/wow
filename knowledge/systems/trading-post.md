# Trading Post & Trader's Tender

**Trader's Tender** is the account-wide cosmetic currency spent at the Trading Post
(Orgrimmar / Stormwind). It buys only the current month's rotating stock plus the
Post's permanent staples — it is never a power currency.

## Recurring income — 1,000 / month, and that's the ceiling

| Source | Amount | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Collector's Cache** | **500** | Automatic each month; click the chest at the Trading Post to claim |
| **Traveler's Log** | **up to 500** | Monthly activity list (Adventure Guide → Traveler's Log, `Shift-J`) |

The Traveler's Log rotates a themed activity set every month — quests, dungeons,
raid bosses, pet battles, PvP, holiday content, emotes at NPCs. Each completion
fills the monthly bar; **the bar caps at 500 Tender, so you do not need to finish
every activity** — do the cheapest ones that fill it.

Rare exceptions push a month above 1,000: anniversary months (WoW's, and February
for the Trading Post's own) occasionally add extra Tender.

## One-time / promotional sources

- **Tour the Trading Post** intro quest — 500
- **Trading Post Enthusiast** achievement — 500
- **Midnight** expansion purchase — up to 2,000, depending on edition
- **Anubisath Guardian Pack** — 700
- Plunderstore purchases (Plunderstorm) — 250–500
- BlizzCon 2026 viewing — 200
- **Nine-Tenths of the Law** — 5

Previous-expansion editions (The War Within) and older bundles granted 500–1,000
each but are no longer obtainable.

## Carryover and cap

**Unspent Tender rolls over indefinitely and has no expiry and no holding cap.**
The 1,000-point figure people cite as a cap is the *monthly earn* limit, not a
wallet limit — balances well above it are normal (the roster here sits at
~3,800–4,200). Saving across months is the intended way to afford the expensive
mounts.
