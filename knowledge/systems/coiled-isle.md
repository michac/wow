# The Coiled Isle (12.1)

The new **12.1 "Curse of Ula'tek"** outdoor zone: the fog-shrouded island off
the **east coast of Zul'Aman**, opened when the fog lifted with the patch on
**2026-08-11**. It continues the **Zul'jan** story — you join **Zul'jarra** as
she pursues her brother to bring him and the others home to Zul'Aman, while the
isle's long-buried Amani history and the ancient venom-thing **Ula'tek** come
back up out of it.

> ✅ **The outdoor zone is live NOW.** Blizzard explicitly lists Curse Surges and
> the Vaults of Atal'Utek as **available during the pre-season week**. What is
> *not* live until **2026-08-18** is Season-2-gated content that happens to sit
> on the isle: the **Venomous Abyss** raid, **Prey Nightmare mode / "The Curse of
> the Isle"**, Bountiful Delves, and the Tidebound Grotto lair above World
> difficulty. Those are covered in their own files, not here.

> ⚠ **Read every power/threat claim below against 12.1's global combat retune,
> not against 12.0.7 numbers.** 12.1 raised **player health and creature damage
> by 25% at max level** (health consumables rescaled; several DPS/Tank healing
> and absorb spells retuned), lowered major DPS cooldowns while raising
> steady-state damage on several specs, reset **diminishing-return categories
> after 20s** (was 16s), and added a **"missed" visual + sound** to interrupts
> used on a non-casting target. These apply everywhere, including outdoors on
> this isle — so percentage-of-health effects here (Virulent Mucus' 5% gate,
> Gorgoneion Gaze's 50% trigger) sit on a health pool that just grew.

## Getting there / unlocking

- Renown with **Zul'jarra's Forces starts accruing before you ever make
  landfall**, from the **Zul'Aman** quests with **Lady Liadrin**, **Orweyna** and
  **Zul'jarra** that lead into the patch campaign. Do those first; they are the
  on-ramp, not a side branch.
- The **Amani Pass** between Eversong Woods and Zul'Aman **no longer dismounts
  you** as of 12.1 — the travel fix that makes the overland approach bearable.
- **Tokka's Landing** is the zone hub: quartermaster **Jan'sari the Watchful**,
  the tortollan captain **Tokka**, and (once Nightmare unlocks on Aug 18) the
  **Image of Astalor Bloodsworn** who toggles "The Curse of the Isle". The
  fishing-reward vendor **Second Mate Sluggs** is *not* here — he is at
  **Tokka's Folly** (below), and the **Skull of Er'inye** vendor plus the
  **Altar of Corrosion** are down in the Vaults.
- The **Vaults of Atal'Utek** are unlocked by the quest **"Into the Vaults of
  Atal'Utek"**, from **Warleader Abdumati**. That single quest opens access to
  the whole repeatable loop inside — Temple Patrols, Strikes, Incursions and
  Ancient Foes.
- **Amani Windcallers** inside the Vaults transport you around them and can use
  the wind to **hide you temporarily from hostile creatures** as you travel. As
  you progress through the Vaults you gain **additional traversal points**,
  making it easier to cross the pools of venom.

## The Vaults of Atal'Utek

The ancient Amani complex inside the mountain, and **the heart of the isle's
repeatable content**. Blizzard's framing: "challenging group content and
rotating public events" where "taking part in these public events will
ultimately build up to a boss fight."

Four activity tiers, escalating. The four tier **names** are Tier-1 (the news
post lists them); the player counts and respawn timers below are Tier-3 —
@verify-ingame those numbers only:

- **Temple Patrols** — the entry tier, spawning roughly every **10 minutes**
  across the Vaults; soloable.
- **Temple Strikes** — trigger after several Patrols complete; recommended
  **3–5 players**.
- **Temple Incursions** — the top tier, a Vaults-wide event recommended for
  **15–20 players**…
- **Ancient Foes** — …which culminates in an Ancient Foe boss battle. This is
  the "public events build to a boss fight" loop the notes describe.

**Rewards from the loop:** **Corrosive Souls**, **Corrosive Coins**,
**Trovehunter's Bounty**, **Venom-Cursed Fragments**, and **Zul'jarra's Forces
renown**.

⚠ Structurally this is the Void-Assault pattern (`void-incursions.md`) — small
objective → chain → large payoff phase → boss — moved indoors and given its own
currency stack. Do not assume the two share rewards; they do not.

## The Altar of Corrosion — the zone talent tree

The isle's own progression system: a **zone-scoped custom talent tree** granting
**exploration, combat, mobility and reward bonuses** across the Coiled Isle.
The Altar itself sits **in the Amani Foothold, inside the Vaults of Atal'Utek** —
it is *not* a surface location, so reaching it means clearing the "Into the
Vaults of Atal'Utek" unlock first. You can **respec at the Altar at any time**.

- **Fed by Corrosive Coins.** Coins are the Vaults' own currency — *"Spirits of
  the Amani within the Vaults of Atal'Utek deal exclusively in this phantasmal
  token."*
- **The coin multipliers are the first thing to understand.** A row of four
  nodes across the top of the tree unlocks **automatically** as you spend, and
  multiplies the Corrosive Coin you earn **within the Vaults**:

  | Node | Effect | Unlocks at |
  |---|---|---|
  | Corrosive Spirit | +25% Corrosive Coin | spend 5 more points |
  | Corrosive Spirit II | +50% Corrosive Coin | spend 10 more points |
  | Corrosive Spirit III | +75% Corrosive Coin | spend 15 more points |
  | Corrosive Spirit IV | +100% Corrosive Coin | spend 20 more points |

  Because they are automatic and cumulative with depth, **early spending pays
  for itself** — there is no "save your points" play here.
- **Named main-tree nodes** (Tier 1, from the published tree):
  - **Venomous Hunt** (top) — identify venom-enhanced enemies; defeating them
    occasionally grants **Volatile Venom** and extra Corrosive Coin. The
    published text reads *"increasing your Strength by 5% for 5 Minutes"* — read
    that as **primary stat** pending an in-game check on a non-Strength class.
    @verify-ingame
  - Traversal/safety: **Venomwalking** (−75% damage from venom pits),
    **Venom Neutralization** (−25% damage from venom-crazed foes),
    **Venom Ward**, **Venomous Sanctuary**, **Wondrous Wards** (2 ranks).
  - Combat: **Spirit Guardian**, **Spirit Strikes**, **Venom Coating** (your
    abilities inflict poison) and **Venom Infusion** (2 ranks),
    **Volatility Overflowing**.
  - Reward/economy: **Ofi's Offerings**, **Corroded Grounds** (Corrosive Coin
    while gathering), **Slithering Secrets** (keys to hidden treasures).
  - **Discovery-gated choice pairs** — each pair needs a discovery item:
    **Spirit Walk / Spectral Shipping** (Feather of Tok'jara) ·
    **Glideways / Swift Steps** (Mummified Lynx's Paw) ·
    **Surge Seniority / Spiritual Succession** (Lost Med'jal Amulet) ·
    **Egg Specialist / Egg Evasion** (Eye of Szarith).
- **Spirit of Corrosion** is a *separate*, discrete grant, not a coin: renown
  **rank 8** (turn in the **Corrosive Relic**) and renown **rank 14** (the
  **Corrosive Bijou**) each send you to **Er'inye** for one Spirit of Corrosion
  to spend at the Altar. Two renown ranks are therefore worth real Altar
  progress. @verify-ingame what a Spirit actually buys.

### The Skull of Er'inye vendor — the other Coin sink

Corrosive Coins are **not** only talent points. The **Skull of Er'inye** vendor
sells, for Coins: the **Caustic Venomfang** flying mount (**10,000**), companion
pets **Corrosive Writhling** / **Volatile Venomfang** (**5,000** each),
**Arsenal: Venom-Cursed Arms** (**25,000**), the four **Venom-Cursed** armor
ensembles (**10,000** each), profession recipes (e.g. Recipe: Liquid Luster —
2,500 Coins + 150 Artisan Alchemist's Moxie) and a long housing-decor list
(**500 / 750 / 1,500** Coins, up to **2,500** for the *Altar of Corrosion*
decor). Budget Coins against the tree accordingly — the mount and arsenal alone
are 35,000.

### Corrosive Powers (the Corrosive Codex) — a *different* track

Do not conflate these with the talent tree. **Corrosive Powers** are unlocked
with **Corrosive Souls**, not Coins.

- **Unlock path:** a short questline beginning with **"Corrosive Gifts:
  Corrosive Power"**, which itself requires the **Codex of the Soul Coilers**
  item to start. The Codex then lives at the Altar of Corrosion.
- **Corrosive Souls** are **warband-wide** and drop from across Coiled Isle and
  Season 2 content: Vaults activities and bosses, **Curse Surges**, the Prey
  boss **Ral'kala**, the **Nymrissa Wavecaller** lair, Bountiful Delves and Prey
  hunts. Your **first Curse Surge boss each day has a chance to drop one**.
- Powers unlock **in any order**. After unlocking **8** you may have **2 active
  at once**, for deliberately synergistic pairs.
- ⚠ **They work in Midnight outdoor zones and inside Delves only — they do not
  follow you into dungeons or raids.** So this is open-world/Delve power, and
  must never be modelled into a dungeon or raid rotation.
- A **projection of the Altar of Corrosion appears inside Midnight Delves** once
  the system is unlocked, so you can re-spec your power pair **at the start of
  any Delve** rather than travelling back to the Vaults.

**There are exactly 12 Corrosive Powers.** The list is published in full and is
the Tier-1 floor — an editorial "about 20" is wrong:

| Power | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Ophidian Maw** | damage has a chance to call a giant snake, Nature damage split between nearby enemies; Poisoned non-boss creatures below 20% health are executed |
| **Insidious Venom** | chance to poison *yourself*: secondaries/tertiaries down, primary greatly up, 12s |
| **Viperine Grasp** | harmful abilities may pull up to 2 nearby enemies to your target (up to 3 more if Poisoned), Nature damage |
| **Virulent Mucus** | on entering combat, 5 layers each negating an instance of damage above 5% max health |
| **Mephistic Cloud** | chance to apply Mephitic Cloud to nearby party + enemies for 10s (allies' abilities poison; enemies' abilities heal their target) |
| **Accursed Poison** | chance to poison the strongest enemy for 1 min; **triggers as an interrupt** on its next interruptible cast |
| **Gorgoneion Gaze** | **when *you* drop below 50% health**, petrify all enemies within 10 yds for 10s (stunned + invulnerable); on break they are Poisoned and slowed 50% for 5s |
| **Plague of Corrosion** | stacking Poison on nearby enemies for 1 min; on death it transfers with its remaining duration |
| **Lithic Plumage** | taking damage may reduce incoming damage 75% up to a cap; the cap scales with nearby Poisoned enemies |
| **Miasma Geyser** | geyser erupts after 10s: Nature damage split between enemies, heals allies inside; Poisoned units take/receive more |
| **Ouroboric Cycle** | tertiaries 5s → secondaries 5s → primary 5s; a kill adds another Cycle; +1s per Cycle while you are Poisoned |
| **Ula'tek's Gift** | 5 stacks of a Nature DoT (10 while you are Poisoned); at 20 stacks it Corrodes for its remaining damage at once |

⚠ Note the **Gorgoneion Gaze** trigger: it keys off **your own** health dropping
below 50%, not the enemy's — the actor matters, because it inverts when the
power is useful.

## Curse Surges & the rare elites

- Curse Surges **regularly spawn rare elites at five rotating locations** across
  the isle — waves of corruption that culminate in the rare.
- **Each successful Curse Surge opens a "Cursed Fishing pool" at that
  location.** This is the gating relationship worth remembering: fishing spots
  are *earned*, per site, and they also feed standing with **Captain Tokka's
  Crew**.
- Surges award **Corrosive Souls**, loot, and **Zul'jarra's Forces renown**;
  from **renown 4** the first Surge boss of the day has a **Corrosive Soul
  chance**, and from **renown 8** Surge bosses have a daily chance at a piece of
  **Warbound Veteran equipment**.

## Venom Fishing and Tokka

A **local story** with the tortollan sea captain **Tokka** and his crew at
**Tokka's Landing**. He teaches you **Cursed Fishing**; you level his friendship
faction with dailies, strange artifacts and spirits from his past, help him take
down the remnants of his traitorous crew, and in exchange he teaches you to fish
**cursed water, venom, and even inside the Vaults of Atal'Utek**. There is also
a voyage against his nemesis, **Nageressies, the Great White Serpent**.

- The faction is **Captain Tokka's Crew**, a 5-rank track: **Stranger → Doomed
  Sailor → Cursed Angler → Venom Trawler → Bloodsworn Crew**.
- ⚠ **The rewards vendor is Second Mate Sluggs, at Tokka's Folly** — *not*
  Jan'sari (who is the Zul'jarra's Forces quartermaster at Tokka's Landing) and
  not Tokka himself. Sluggs' stock is priced in **Voidlight Marl, Coiled
  Filament, Artisan Moxie and gold**, gated by Captain Tokka's Crew rank. Tokka
  himself is the progress tracker for the crew.
- **Anglin' Score** is Midnight-wide, not isle-only: each fish is worth up to
  **100 points**, and **2,500** score earns the achievement **The Briny Best**
  and the title **"Briny"**. Blizzard calls it purely cosmetic fun.
- Headline rewards (all Tier-1 published):
  - **The Coiled Huntress** — the new Epic fishing rod with interchangeable
    boons, **6,000 Voidlight Marl**. +152 Perception, +18 Midnight Fishing, and
    it turns you into a **snake underwater in Midnight zones** (water breathing
    + fast swim). Fishing the Coiled Isle siphons **Venom** into it, which
    **Seasage Polo** converts into **Coiled Filament** by ritual.
  - **Sea-Dwelling Isle Serpent** (flying mount) — **2,500 Coiled Filament**
    *and* **Bloodsworn Crew** rank.
  - **Eerie Lure** (see unique isle fish, 30 min) — **10 Voidlight Marl**.
  - Envenomed weapons at Bloodsworn Crew (**500–1,000 Coiled Filament**),
    profession recipes for **Artisan Moxie**, cooking/fishing recipes for
    Voidlight Marl, and tortollan housing decor.

## Zul'jarra's Forces (pointer)

The zone's renown faction: **20 ranks**, quartermaster **Jan'sari the Watchful**
at Tokka's Landing, paid in **Voidlight Marl** and **Artisan Moxies**. Rank
rewards include Cursebreaker's Bracers I/II (Veteran → Champion wrist), Curse
Surge drop upgrades, crafting recipes, **Spirit of Corrosion I/II** (ranks 8 and
14), the **Spirit of Tok'jara** mount questline at **rank 10** (started from
**Du'gal**, in the Vaults of Atal'Utek), and the title
**"Hash'ura of Zul'jarra"** at **rank 20**. Full table:
`../factions/zuljarras-forces.md`.

## Also on the isle

- **Venom-Soaked Satchels** drop throughout the isle: **materials for upgrading
  gear**, gold, and more.
- **New wild pets** to collect (12.1 also added Pet Battler achievements for
  Outland and Cataclysm).
- **Coiled Isle profession recipes** come through Zul'jarra renown — see
  `professions.md`.
- **New snake/venom enemy variants** were also seeded into the *existing*
  Midnight Delves, not just the new ones.
