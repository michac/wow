# Voidforge (12.1)

> ⚠ **12.1 reset the bonus-roll half of this system across the season
> boundary.** Part 0 (building the forge) and Part 2 (Ascendant upgrades) are
> untouched by the 12.1 notes; **Part 1 changed substantially** — read it before
> planning a week. And note the split: **12.1 went live 2026-08-11, but Season 2
> does not open until 2026-08-18**, so several Part 1 rules below are dated.

Two-part system from **Decimus**, added in 12.0.5.

## Part 0 — Unlocking / building the Voidforge

You must **build** the forge before any of the rewards below exist.

- **Where**: **Decimus** at **The Howling Ridge, Voidstorm** —
  `/way #2405 51.3 68.6` (the Voidforge site itself).
- **Prerequisite**: progress the **Voidstorm campaign** until Howling Ridge /
  Decimus become available.
- **Start quest**: **"Building the Voidforge"** (pick up from Decimus), then
  the **repeatable** follow-up **"An Elementary Voidcore."**
- **Build material — `Elementary Voidcore Shards`**: each turn-in needs **3
  shards** → transmuted into **1 Elemental Voidcore**. The forge needs **6
  Elemental Voidcores total = 18 Elementary Voidcore Shards.**
- **Shards drop from**: **M+ final chest** (every completed run), **Bountiful
  Coffers** (Bountiful Delves), and **Season 1 raid bosses — all difficulties
  incl. LFR**, **1 guaranteed shard per boss**. So normal endgame already
  stockpiles them — a player running keys is likely sitting on the build
  currency without realising it.
  - ⚠ **12.1**: those sources are Season-1-era and the 12.1 notes say nothing
    about re-pointing them at Season 2 content. During the **pre-season week
    (Aug 11–17) there are no keystones and no Bountiful Delves**, which leaves
    the S1 raids as the practical shard source; whether **Venomous Abyss** and
    the S2 M+/Bountiful pool also drop shards from Aug 18 is **unconfirmed**.
    Most warbands finished this part months ago — it matters only to a fresh
    max-level character. @verify-ingame
- **Fastest catch-up for the last cores**: an **LFR The Voidspire** clear
  (6 bosses) = **6 shards = 2 Elemental Voidcores** in one week. Wing 1 alone
  (3 bosses) ≈ 1 core in ~15 min incl. the Decimus turn-in. (Raid loot is
  weekly-locked per boss, so it's one pass, not a re-farm.)
- **Warband-wide** progress, with **full catch-up** for late starters — so a
  **leveling alt cannot farm shards** (the sources are all max-level: M+,
  Bountiful T8+ coffers, S1 raids; effective entry ~**ilvl 220**, level **90**
  cap), but it **inherits the built forge for free** the moment it dings 90.
  Build on your main; alts ride along.

> ⚠ Three different "void" items — do not confuse:
> 1. **Elementary Voidcore Shards** → *build* the forge (3 → 1 Elemental
>    Voidcore; 18 shards total). This Part 0.
> 2. **Nebulous Voidcores** → weekly *bonus-roll* tokens, after the forge is
>    built (Part 1). **In Season 2 these come out of the Great Vault**, not a
>    weekly vendor exchange.
> 3. **Ascendant Voidshards** → *upgrade* weapons/trinkets past cap (5 → 1
>    Ascendant Voidcore), gated behind the separate Nilhammer chain (Part 2).

## Part 1 — Nebulous Voidcores (bonus rolls)

*(Blizzard's 12.1 notes spell it **Nebulous Voidcore**, one word — the
"Void Cores" spelling used earlier in this KB is ours, not theirs.)*

- **Unlock**: finish Part 0 (the forge must be built).

### What 12.1 changed

- **Your Season 1 cores are gone.** Voidcores held at the end of Season 1 were
  **converted to gold** at the week-of-Aug-11 maintenance, and they may **no
  longer be used in Season 1 content**. A stockpile saved "for the new tier"
  did not survive the boundary.
- **From the start of Season 2 (2026-08-18), Voidcores are a Great Vault
  reward** — you take one out of the vault the way you take a piece of gear.
  This is the only acquisition route the 12.1 notes name for S2.
  - ⚠ **Not in the first S2 vault.** Voidcore bonus rolls appear in the vault
    from the **week of 2026-08-25**, and only for a character who has
    **unlocked at least 3 panes** that week.
- **Raid re-roll now costs 1 core** (was 2).
- **Orin Straylight relocated near the Catalyst in Silvermoon** and will grant
  **one extra Voidcore per week starting week 8 of Season 2**.
- **Lairs** (the new instanced world-boss format) are a spend target: a Voidcore
  may be used **once per week per lair**.
- **Removed source:** the **Tier 6 Advanced Ritual Studies** quests no longer
  offer a Voidcore bonus-roll reward (the quests remain completable for the
  achievement). See `systems/ritual-sites.md`.
- ⚠ **Unconfirmed:** whether the old **Voidforge weekly exchange** — gold /
  Voidlight Marl / **Veteran Dawncrests** → 2 cores from Decimus — still runs in
  Season 2. Dawncrests are the *Season 1* crest (S2's are **Mistcrests**), so at
  minimum the price is stale, and the 12.1 notes describe only the vault route.
  Do not budget a week around the exchange until it is seen live.
  @verify-ingame

### Spending a core

- A core = bonus roll on eligible content: **Delve completion**, **Prey hunt on
  Nightmare**, **M+ end-of-run**, **raid bosses** (1 core), and **Lairs** (once
  per week per lair). Delves and Prey remain the solo-accessible uses.
- **Reward item level = the Great Vault reward for that activity.** Blizzard
  restated this rule unchanged for S2 — so reason from the *vault* tracks, not
  from a memorised per-activity table. Those tracks moved in 12.1:
  - LFR / Normal / Heroic raid → **the first step of the next harder
    difficulty's track** (a Heroic-raid reward is **Myth 1/6**).
  - Mythic raid → **Myth 6/6**, except **Very Rare items and loot from the
    penultimate and final bosses**, which are **Myth 9** whether they came from
    the boss or the vault.
  - ⚠ The Season 1 shorthand "M+10 → myth-track, Delve/Prey → hero-track" is
    **not carried forward** — the S2 vault rows have their own caps (e.g. the
    World row is capped at **Champion 3/6** for the first S2 vault, **Hero 1/6**
    thereafter). Take the current numbers from `endgame/great-vault.md`.

## Part 2 — Ascendant Voidcores (weapon/trinket overcap)

- **Unlock**: warband-wide **Ascendant Nilhammer** questline from
  Decimus. **Starts**: after the Voidforge is built, Decimus offers
  **"New Tools, New Heights"** at **Howling Ridge, Voidstorm (~51.2,
  68.4)**. 4-week chain: charge a **Hungering Oblivium** through
  endgame content each week. No Voidcores drop until the chain is done
  (came online ~mid-May 2026; Blizzard News, tier 1).
- Combine **5 Ascendant Voidshards → 1 Ascendant Voidcore**.
- Voidshard sources: raids, M+, **Bountiful Delves, Nightmare Prey Hunts**
  (so solo players have a path).
- A Voidcore raises a **fully-upgraded** weapon or trinket past cap. ⚠ **The
  numbers below are Season 1 values and are almost certainly stale**: the 12.1
  notes do not restate them, but the whole gear ladder shifted **+45** in S2
  (Myth now tops out at **334**, was 289 — see `endgame/dawncrests.md`), so an
  S2 overcap target has to be re-measured, not inferred.
  - *(S1)* 6/6 Myth **289 → 298**
  - *(S1)* max-quality spark-crafted **285 → 295**
  - 6/6 Hero also eligible (resulting ilvl never confirmed)
- Weapons and trinkets **only**; item must already be maxed.
- The Ascendant chain itself (Nilhammer questline, 5 shards → 1 core, the shard
  sources) is **unchanged by 12.1** — no mention in the notes. @verify-ingame

### 12.0.7 change — crafted eligibility fix

- **12.0.7 hotfix:** fixed eligibility so **pre-12.0.5 crafted weapons and
  trinkets** can now receive an Ascendant Voidcore upgrade (previously they
  were wrongly excluded). (Change Ledger, Tier-1 hotfix distillation.)

## Related — Abundant Harvest (Shards of Dundun)

> The Voidforge bonus-roll/upgrade system is **separate** from the void
> *harvesting* loop below; they share the void theme, not the currency.

- **12.0.7:** Abundant Harvest now lets you harvest with **multiple Shards of
  Dundun at once — 1, 2, 4, or 8** in a single action. The rewarded
  **Unalloyed Abundance scales directly** with the number of Shards consumed
  (8 shards ≈ 8× the single-shard reward).
- **Hotfix (post-12.0.7):** fixed a bug where Abundance could not begin while
  in **Voidstorm**; affected players were **refunded via Unalloyed Abundance**.
- Source: Revelations content update notes (Tier 1) + Icy Veins (Tier 3),
  corroborated against Warcraft Wiki.
