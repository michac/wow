# Void Assaults — Void Incursions & Void Strikes (12.1)

Rotating open-world assault content that loops between **Eversong Woods** and
**Zul'Aman**, swapping weekly. Collectively called **Void Assaults**; it is a
repeatable loop that awards **Field Accolades**, gear-related rewards, and
**Great Vault** world-content progress.

## Unlock / gating

Pick up the intro quest from **Ranger Captain Lilatha** in Silvermoon City,
which unlocks the **"Void Strike"** quest. This opens the weekly assault wrapper
for whichever zone is active that week.

## The two activities

- **Void Strikes** — the foundational, rotating objectives. Only one Strike is
  active at a time across the zone; completing it spawns another elsewhere,
  forming a chain. Each Strike fills a shared progress bar.
- **Void Incursions** — the larger payoff phase, triggered once enough Strikes
  fill the bar. Structured: ~2-minute prep window → enemy waves → support
  objectives → final boss (Void Imperator).

## Rewards & currencies

- **Field Accolades** — the primary event currency, from Field Pouches (Strikes)
  and larger Field Satchels (Incursions).
- **Season 2 Adventurer crests** (*Adventurer Mistcrest*) — **new in 12.1**:
  Void Strikes, Void Incursions **and the weekly wrapper quest** all award them.
  This replaces the Season 1 crest award. Adventurer Mistcrests upgrade
  Adventurer-track gear across **ilvl 269–282**.
- **Dark Particles** — farmed during the loop; spend at vendors (below).
- **Bulging Satchels** — chance to drop on completing Strikes / Incursions;
  contain cosmetics.
- Strike pouches also include **Voidlight Marl** and gold.
- Contributes to the Great Vault **world** slot.

### Vendors (Silvermoon City)

- **Triam Dawnsetter** (Bazaar) — sells cosmetic pouches matching **Bulging
  Satchel** contents (including cosmetics from Ritual Sites end chests) for
  **150 Dark Particles** each. *(New in 12.0.7.)*
- **Maren Silverwing** (above the Bazaar) — the **Void-Touched Cache** gear
  vendor (*Cache of Void-Touched Armaments: \<slot\>*). **The whole Season 1
  lineup was removed in 12.1** and replaced with a Season 2 one:

  | Cache | Cost | Slot | Binding | Drops at |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | **S2 Adventurer** | **200** Field Accolades | slot-specific | **Warbound until equipped** | ilvl **266** (Adventurer 1/6) |
  | **S2 Veteran** | **500** Field Accolades | **random** | **Bind-on-Pickup** | Veteran 1/6 (ilvl **279**) |
  | **S2 Veteran** | **750** Field Accolades | slot-specific | **Bind-on-Pickup** | ilvl **279** (Veteran 1/6) |

  ⚠ **The Veteran caches are BoP, not Warbound** — the Season 1 caches ended the
  season Warbound (the Heroic cache flipped BoP → Warbound in the June 26/30
  2026 hotfixes), and 12.1 **does not carry that forward**: only the 200-Accolade
  Adventurer cache gears alts. Plan Accolade spending per character accordingly.
  *(The currency itself has always been character-bound; only the cache binding
  ever varied.)*
- Also here: **Field Accolade Pouches** — **100 Dark Particles → 10 Accolades**,
  the conversion path from the cosmetic currency into the gear currency.

## 12.1 "Curse of Ula'tek" changes (live 2026-08-11)

- **Void Strikes, Void Incursions and the Weekly Quest now give Season 2
  Adventurer crests.** Live in the pre-season week — you can bank Adventurer
  Mistcrests before Season 2 formally opens **2026-08-18**.
- **Void-Touched Caches re-tiered to Season 2** (table above): new **200**-Accolade
  Adventurer **Warbound** cache; new **500** (random slot) and **750**
  (slot-specific) Veteran **Bind-on-Pickup** caches. **The Season 1 gear caches
  have been removed** — the old Champion 75/100 and Hero(ic) 500/750 caches are
  gone, so an Accolade balance saved from Season 1 now buys the S2 lineup only.
- Nothing changed about the Strike/Incursion loop itself, the zone rotation, or
  the Great Vault world-slot contribution.

## 12.0.7 "Revelations" changes (live 2026-06-16, historical)

- **XP from completing a Void Strike or Void Incursion is doubled.** XP is now
  also awarded for **most individual steps** within Strikes/Incursions, to
  balance the ones with more steps. The **weekly wrapper quests** also had their
  XP significantly increased (alongside other similar wrappers).
- **Increased drop rate of Dark Particles** from Strikes and Incursions.
- **Increased drop rate of Bulging Satchels** from Strikes and Incursions.
- **New cosmetic vendor:** Triam Dawnsetter (Dark Particle cosmetic pouches,
  150 each — see above).
