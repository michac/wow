Collect **the** Great Vault reward — one weekly chest, **one** pick from whichever slots
last reset's M+/delve/raid/PvP progress unlocked. Free power, trivial time, **expires at
reset**. The activities that fill its columns (`mplus`, `delve-bountiful`) are their own
entries; this row is only the *claim*. Gate hides it once claimed / when empty.

## ⚠ Pre-season week (Aug 11–17): the vault is paying out Season 1

12.1 went live **2026-08-11**; **Season 2 opens 2026-08-18**. During the week between:

- **The vault you open now pays out on your *final Season 1* week's activity** — S1 rows,
  S1 tracks, S1 item levels. None of 12.1's new reward rules apply to this claim.
- **Season 2 vault credit starts accruing now.** What you do Aug 11–17 fills the panes you
  **claim on 2026-08-18**, so the filler activities still rank this week — the *claim* just
  doesn't pay S2 numbers yet.

## Season 2 rules (from the first S2 vault, claimable 2026-08-18)

- **Raid row jumps a difficulty.** LFR / Normal / Heroic vault rewards now come in at the
  first step of the *next harder* track — every **Heroic** raid vault reward is
  **Myth 1/6**. **Mythic** raid vault rewards are **Myth 6/6**, except **Very Rare** items
  and loot from the **penultimate and final bosses**, which are **Myth 9** whether they
  came from the boss or the vault. This is the biggest reason to keep the raid row filled.
- **World row is capped, and the cap moves.** **Champion 3/6** max in the *first* S2 vault;
  **Hero 1/6** max in every vault after. The S1 habit of treating the world row as a
  near-free top-track slot in week 1 is dead.
- **Nebulous Voidcores become a vault reward** from the start of S2 — but **not in the
  first S2 vault**. Bonus rolls arrive the **week of 2026-08-25** and need **≥3 panes**
  unlocked (`voidcores.md`). S1 Voidcores convert to gold at S1's end.

Full tables, slot-unlock thresholds and the S2 M+ vault ilvls: `../../endgame/great-vault.md`.
