The Zul'Aman fishing activity — **Depthdiver Jeju** at 68.2, 20.0 off the Zul'Aman coast
(north-east of the zone). A 12.0.5 harpoon-diving event: suit up, dive the Depths, spear
fish for points, spend them on diver-suit upgrades (swim speed / oxygen → deeper dives)
and fishing collectibles. Repeatable, standing, low-stakes collectibles/flavor
(`goal:collectibles`, cosmetic → R floored unless urgency lifts it). Included for breadth
of the standing solo tail; `gate: always` so it only surfaces as leftover fill-time once
the expiring weeklies are cleared.

**12.1 verdict: unchanged, and a SIBLING of the new Venom Fishing — not superseded.**
12.1 adds **Venom Fishing** ("Cursed Fishing") on the **Coiled Isle**: each Curse Surge
cleared at one of the isle's **five rotating rare-elite locations** opens a cursed fishing
pool *there*, with a local story and a **separate reputation track** under the tortollan
sea captain **Tokka** at **Tokka's Landing** (5 ranks: Stranger → Doomed Sailor → Cursed
Angler → Venom Trawler → Bloodsworn Crew), and an **Ula'tek Snakehead Lure** fishing
recipe sold by **Jan'sari the Watchful** at **Zul'jarra's Forces renown 5**.

That is a **different loop in a different zone with different rewards and its own rep**;
nothing in the 12.1 Tier-1 notes or the `patch-notes/12.1.md` archive touches Abyss
Anglers, Depthdiver Jeju, or the Zul'Aman Depths, and the Coiled Isle coverage never
mentions them. So this row stays active and unmodified; **Venom Fishing is tracked under
`coiled-isle`, not here** — do not fold the two together.
