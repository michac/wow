## The design in one paragraph

Destruction is **Demonology's rotation with the resource in the same place and everything else
harder**. Soul Shards are readable (R3, never-secret) and the two Diabolist transforms are the
same two — **Ruination rides Chaos Bolt** and **Infernal Bolt rides the filler** — so half the
work carries straight over. What does not carry over is the *shape of the ordering reasons*:
where Demonology's decisions are resource comparisons, Destruction's two most frequent ones are a
**DoT's remaining time** and a **ritual buff's remaining time**, and both are sealed durations
with no authored band. So the honest row set is smaller than the button list in a specific way —
**every row is bound, and three of them carry no ordering cue at all**. Against that, two facts
pay for themselves. **Conflagrate's `charges>=2` rung is the cleanest `capped` in any catalog**:
R6 reads charges exactly at full and nowhere else, which is precisely the state the rung asks
about. And **Backdraft's rung is `stack<1`** — *absent*, not *below two* — so the `aura` latch's
plain boolean answers it, and the pilot's sealed count is not needed for the single-target
rotation at all. Ten cues do the work — **A** Soul Fire overcap, **B** Conflagrate's banked
charge, **C** its Backdraft hold, **D** its overcap, **E** Immolate's target-DoT hold, **F**
Cataclysm's talent gate, **G** starved, **H** Shadowburn's proc hold, **I** its promotion, and
**J** the single-target skip — two of them positive, on different rows, which is the invariant
pass 1 rests on.

### The Charges column

**Conflagrate is the only charge row, and it is the only one in any catalog whose charge state
does ordering work.** Two charges, ~13 s recharge *[T1: `ability-inventory.tsv` @ 12.1.0.69214;
`abilities.md`]*, and R6's measurement is on **this exact spell** —
OBS-066: at 2/2 everything is readable; at 1/2 and 0/2 `currentCharges` is secret and `isActive`
is `true` in both, so it cannot tell castable-1 from dead-0.

**That measurement is why cue B works and why the pilot's estimator is withdrawn.** R6 says the
one thing cap can read exactly is *"charges are at maximum"* — and `actions.default` rung 2 is
`conflagrate,if=action.conflagrate.charges>=2`, which asks exactly that and nothing else. The
`capped` predicate is shipped and reads it. The napkin estimator `../spec.md` §3.5 proposed —
seed out of combat, debit on a press, credit on a `ChargeGained` alert, clamp, re-seed — was
built to recover the *number*, and **no rung in this priority wants the number**. It is not
authored, and R6 keeps it on the shelf for a spec that does.

No other bound row is a charge spell: Soul Fire (45 s), Cataclysm (30 s), Havoc (30 s), Summon
Infernal (120 s, 90 s with Inferno) and Immolate, Chaos Bolt, Shadowburn, Incinerate, Rain of Fire
(no cooldown) all carry a plain cooldown or none.

## The authored row order

`Anchor.lua` re-anchors the Essential viewer to the catalog's `entries` array, so left-to-right
**is** the priority order and the elimination walk in `scenarios.md` reads it in that direction.

| # | Entry | `actions.default` rung | `actions.aoe_dia` rung |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Soul Fire | 1 | 11 |
| 2 | Conflagrate | 2, 4, 10 | 4, 13 |
| 3 | Summon Infernal | 5 | 1 |
| 4 | Immolate | 9 | 12 |
| 5 | Cataclysm | 12 | 7 |
| 6 | Chaos Bolt / **Ruination** | 3, 13 / **11** | 2, 10 / **6** |
| 7 | Shadowburn | 7 | 5 |
| 8 | Incinerate / **Infernal Bolt** | 15 / **14** | 14 / **9** |
| 9 | Rain of Fire | — | 3 |
| 10 | Havoc | — | 8 |

**The order is `actions.default` flattened, with two rows moved and three documented conflicts.**

**Move 1: Shadowburn drops from rung 7 to position 7.** Its rung sits above Immolate and Chaos
Bolt, but *reaching* that rung requires either a Fiendish Cruelty proc or something cap cannot
see (below). Position 7 is right in every state cap **can** see; cue **I** promotes it in the one
state it can see and the position is wrong about. This is the same trade Retribution makes on
Blade of Justice — encode the common case, correct the rarer one — taken in the positive
direction because the rarer case is a *promotion* rather than a skip.

**Move 2: Rain of Fire and Havoc sit at the right end** although `aoe_dia` ranks them 3 and 8.
Both are single-purpose AoE buttons; cue **J** removes them from single target entirely, and in
AoE the one press position 9 would need to beat is Chaos Bolt at position 6 — which `aoe_dia`
rung 2 puts **above** Rain of Fire anyway on a Diabolist build (*"don't cast Rain of Fire until
~8+ targets"*, `rotation.md`). So the right end is right for the target counts this catalog is
actually authored against, and the very-high-target-count state is a documented misordering.

**Conflict 1: Chaos Bolt has two rungs and both of their conditions are sealed.** Rung 3 is
`chaos_bolt,if=talent.diabolic_ritual&(demonic_art|(variable.ritual_length<action.chaos_bolt.execute_time))&target.health.pct>20`
— *spend the armed Art, or spend now because the ritual is about to complete*. Rung 13 is
`chaos_bolt,if=…talent.diabolic_ritual&variable.ritual_length>4` — *the ordinary shard dump*.
`variable.ritual_length` is the **sum of three aura remaining times** and is sealed; `demonic_art`
is partly visible through R7 and partly not (*Defeats*, item 2). Position 6 encodes rung 13, and
**the dead zone is small and measured**: the ritual wheel is continuous, one of the three
Diabolic Ritual stages is always up, so `ritual_length` is only between `execute_time` (~2.5 s)
and 4 s for a sliver of each ~13 s stage. Outside that sliver position 6 is right for one rung or
the other. **This is a documented misordering with a bounded cost, not an un-eliminable row.**

**Conflict 2: Conflagrate has three rungs pointing two ways.** Rung 2 (`charges>=2`) is near the
top; rungs 4 and 10 (`soul_shard<=4.2` / `<=4.4` and `buff.backdraft.stack<1`) are lower. Position
2 encodes the top rung, cue **B** marks the state that rung is about, and cues **C** and **D**
stand the row down when the *lower* rungs' conditions fail. All three terms are readable.

**Conflict 3: Immolate's rung is a refresh, and cap can only see up/down.** Rung 9's real content
is a pandemic window — refresh at under 30 % of duration, with Internal Combustion and
Cataclysm/Soul Fire interlocks. cap sees *is Immolate on the target*, and cue **E** holds the row
for the whole DoT. The refresh is therefore **late by up to the pandemic window**, every time.
*Defeats*, item 1 — and this one has a shipped, measured recipe waiting for it.

**Documented misorderings — what is left after the cues.**

1. **Shadowburn in execute range, on a Conflagration of Chaos build**, is un-badged and
   un-promoted. *Defeats*, item 3. This is the catalog's largest named exposure.
2. **Rain of Fire above Chaos Bolt at very high target counts.** `aoe_dia` rung 3 fires at
   `active_enemies>=3` with enough shards; the catalog's position says otherwise, and
   `rotation.md`'s Tier-3 colour (*"don't cast Rain of Fire until ~8+ targets"* — Diabolist keeps
   pressing Chaos Bolt because it feeds the ritual) is the reason the row order takes that side.
   cap models no enemy count, so it could not take the other side even if it wanted to.
3. **`target_if` on eight of `aoe_dia`'s fourteen rungs is not modelled** — Conflagrate at the
   most-Immolate'd target, Shadowburn at the shortest-lived, Havoc at the longest-lived, Soul
   Fire and Immolate at the least-Immolate'd. *Defeats*, item 4.
4. **Every `raid_event` / `fight_remains` / `target.time_to_die` term stays out** as a product
   rule: cap does not model the encounter. Six rungs carry one.

## The groups, and why the priority falls out of them

Every bound row is a default ready-self scan member (no row here declares a `scan_when`); the
groups below are prose — how the roster reads, not anything the engine holds.

- **Pressed on sight** — Soul Fire, Summon Infernal, Cataclysm, Havoc: the placed cooldowns.
  They light when ready; the only thing that says wait is cue **A** (a readable resource
  comparison) and cue **F** (a readable talent gate). ⚠ **This catalog authors no
  `sealed-cooldown-range` at all** — not one rung in `actions.default` reads another ability's
  `cooldown.X.remains`. Havoc's and Retribution's signature mechanism has **zero consumers
  here**, exactly as it has none on Devourer, and for the same reason: the ordering reasons are
  resource- and buff-shaped rather than cooldown-alignment-shaped.
- **The decision surface** — Conflagrate, Immolate, Chaos Bolt / Ruination, Shadowburn, Rain of
  Fire, plus **the filler row's second life**: while row 8 is displaying Infernal Bolt it is a
  shard builder worth pressing over the ordinary filler.
- **Reached by subtraction** — Incinerate in its base life, the filler. It has a drawn subject
  on every build.

Membership + cues, read together, reproduce the priority; that is the §3.1 point.

## Roster — player problem → fact → recipe → treatment

### Pressed on sight

- **Soul Fire** (`6353`, default 1). *Problem:* it is the top of the priority and a shard
  generator, so at cap it is the one placed cooldown that can be actively wasted — and its icon
  cannot say so. *Facts:* `ready` (R2); **readable Soul Shards** (R3). *Treatment:* scan +
  - `sf_overcap` — **readable** `overcap`, cue **A**: `{ "resource", ">=", 5 }`, gated on
    `ready(soul_fire)`. That is rung 1's `soul_shard<=4`, read as waste.
  ⚠ **12.1 made this row matter more than the pre-patch guides suggest.** Soul Fire took **+45 %**
  and is the biggest single-ability buff the spec got
  (`knowledge/classes/warlock/destruction/builds.md`), which is why it holds rung 1 rather than
  sitting near Cataclysm.
- **Summon Infernal** (`1122`, default 5). *Problem:* none a cue solves — it is the burst window
  and everything else syncs to it, but the syncing is done by *other* rows and by the player's
  potion/trinket habits, which cap does not model. *Fact:* `ready` (R2). *Treatment:* scan,
  no cues.
- **Cataclysm** (`152108`, default 12). *Problem:* on a build without **Lake of Fire** it is not
  in the single-target priority at all, and the icon looks identical either way. *Facts:*
  `ready` (R2); `talent(lake_of_fire)`. *Treatment:* scan +
  - `cata_awaits_talent` — **readable** `blocked`, cue **F**: `!talent(lake_of_fire)`, gated on
    `ready(cataclysm)`. That is rung 12 exactly, negated. On a Lake of Fire build the marker is
    never authored and position 5 — between Immolate (rung 9) and Chaos Bolt (rung 13) — is
    simply correct.
  ⚠ Cataclysm's `aoe_dia` rung 7 carries `raid_event.adds.in>15`, which is simulation state and
  is not modelled. The `talent.lake_of_fire` half of that same rung is, so the cue is right in
  AoE too.
- **Havoc** (`80240`, `aoe_dia` 8). *Problem:* it does nothing in single target. *Facts:*
  `ready` (R2); cap's `/cap aoe` toggle. *Treatment:* scan + cue **J** (below).
  ⚠ **Havoc is a choice node against Mayhem**, and `builds.md` records Mayhem as preferred for
  passive cleave uptime. On a Mayhem build this row does not exist and the entry costs nothing —
  the same shape as Retribution's Crusader Strike on a Crusading Strikes build.

### The decision surface

- **Conflagrate** (`17962`, default 2, 4 and 10). *Problem:* three of them, and they are the
  reason this row carries more vocabulary than any other. It banks charges and loses them; it
  grants Backdraft and stacking a second one is waste; and it generates shards, which at cap is
  also waste. *Facts:* `capped` (R6 — readable **only** at full, which is what the top rung asks);
  `aura(backdraft)` (the CDM alert-edge latch); **readable Soul Shards** (R3). *Treatment:*
  scan, `charged = 2`, +
  - `conflag_capped` — **readable** `capped`, cue **B**: `capped(conflagrate)`. Rung 2 is
    `action.conflagrate.charges>=2` with **no other term**, and it sits second in the whole
    priority. This is the catalog's one deliberate positive cue and the argument for it is below.
  - `conflag_backdraft` — **readable** `blocked`, cue **C**: `aura(backdraft)`, gated on
    `!capped(conflagrate)`. Rungs 4 and 10 read `buff.backdraft.stack<1` — **absent**, not
    *below two* — so the `aura` latch's plain boolean is the whole term and no count is needed.
    ⚠ **This is the pilot's claim, narrowed and improved.** `../spec.md` §3.5 sealed Backdraft as
    a *count* at two stacks, which is right for `aoe_dia` rung 13's `buff.backdraft.stack<2` and
    is **not** what the single-target list asks. The sealed count is still available (S2, and
    OBS-065 verified it on this exact aura) and this catalog does not need it.
  - `conflag_overcap` — **readable** `overcap`, cue **D**: `{ "resource", ">=", 5 }`, gated on
    `!capped(conflagrate)`. Rungs 4 and 10's `soul_shard<=4.2` / `<=4.4`, read as waste.
  ⚠ **Both lower cues are gated on `!capped`, and that gating is the whole design.** At 2/2
  charges rung 2 fires with no shard and no Backdraft term, so a red badge there would contradict
  the gold one on the same icon. The gate makes the three cues a ladder rather than a pile.
  **Badge-slot arithmetic:** `capped` is positive and packs onto the corner at rank 2; `blocked`
  is slot 1 and `overcap` slot 2, and they never co-occur with `capped` because of the gate.
- **Immolate** (`348`, default 9). *Problem:* the DoT is the spec's floor and its refresh timing
  is invisible on an icon that looks the same whether the debuff has 18 seconds or 2. *Fact:*
  `aura(immolate)` on the **target** — a readable up/down latch off the Cooldown Manager's
  `OnAuraApplied` / `OnAuraRemoved` alert edges, the same recipe Retribution's cue G is built on
  (R8-adjacent; `knowledge/addon-dev/cdm-rider-patterns.md` §6.2, flown in combat on a hostile
  target `[client 2026-08-19]`). *Treatment:* scan +
  - `immolate_up` — **readable** `blocked`, cue **E**: `aura(immolate)`.
  ⚠ **This holds the row for the whole DoT, including the window the APL wants to refresh in**,
  which makes every refresh late by up to the pandemic window. That is a real cost and it is
  *Defeats*, item 1 — with a shipped, measured recipe already waiting (R8's `PandemicIcon`
  boolean, which is never secret).
  ⚠ **The latch is SEEDED, not defaulted, and the enablement risk is Retribution's exactly.**
  Aura secrecy is combat-gated, so cap mirrors aura truth out of combat and freezes at
  `PLAYER_REGEN_DISABLED`; edges mutate it from there. On a setup where the player never added
  Immolate to their tracked rows there is no `cooldownID`, nothing is written, and `World` reports
  **UNKNOWN** — the marker goes dark rather than reading "the DoT is down". The failure direction
  is a missing hold, not a wrong one. ⚠ Whether `PLAYER_TARGET_CHANGED` raises aura alert edges
  decides whether this tracks the *current* target or the one held at the last edge
  (`knowledge/addon-dev/cooldown-manager.md` §5.1) — Destruction survives a stale answer as a
  cleave annoyance, and it is the same open item Retribution flagged.
- **Chaos Bolt / Ruination** (`116858` → `433885`, default 3 and 13 / 11). *Problem:* it is the
  payoff spender and it is shown castable when you cannot pay for it; and when to *pool* rather
  than press rests on the ritual clock. *Facts:* `affordable` (R1); `identity` (R7).
  *Treatment:* scan (one row — both lives are shard spenders) +
  - `cb_starved` — **readable** `starved`, cue **G**: `!affordable(chaos_bolt)`.
    `Sense.buildReads` asks affordability of the **live** id (`info.override or row.primary`), so
    on the transformed row this is **Ruination's** cost, which is none, and on the base row it is
    Chaos Bolt's two shards. One marker covers both lives correctly, and R1's warning applies
    exactly: a spender with no cooldown raises no `Available` / `OnCooldown` edge, so `ready`
    stays latched true whatever the resource.
  ⚠ **The pooling half is not authored.** `variable.ritual_length` is a sum of three sealed aura
  remaining times, and cap has no aura-remaining band. *Defeats*, item 2.
- **Shadowburn** (`17877`, default 7). *Problem:* it is the spec's most valuable spender and it is
  usable only in two very different situations — under a Fiendish Cruelty proc, or on a target

### Why this catalog spends two positive cues, and what stops it spending a third

**There is no positive-cue budget** (`../backlog.md`), and the constraint that actually binds is
mechanical: **no single button may wear two positive cues**, because pass 1 says "press the
positive cue" and says nothing about which of two wins. Conflagrate wears one and Shadowburn wears
the other; no row wears both.

**Cue B is the textbook `capped`.** `../render-shelf.md` Part 0.5 scopes `capped` to *impending
loss*, and it earns its exception because *you are wasting a charge right now* is urgent
regardless of rank and has **no negative phrasing**. Both halves hold here, and the third fact
that clinches it is R6's measurement: **at 2/2 charges the recharge is stalled and everything is
readable; below full the count seals.** The one state cap can read exactly is the one state the
cue is for, and the APL puts it at rung 2 with no other term. There is nothing to argue about.

**Cue I is a `priority`, and it is spent on rank the row order genuinely cannot carry.**
`priority` means *"press this one — the scan would reach it late"*. A Fiendish-Cruelty Shadowburn
is rung 7, above Immolate (9) and both Chaos Bolt rungs (3 and 13), and it sits at position 7
below all of them. The reason position 7 is nonetheless right is that in **every other state**
Shadowburn's rung is unreachable, so the alternative — hoisting it to position 4 and badging
Immolate, Cataclysm and Chaos Bolt down in the common case — would add three markers to remove
one. Part 0.5's test for a promotion is that it should **remove** authored vocabulary, and this
one does.

**And what is refused.** Five Soul Shards looks like `capped` and is not: it is not a loss in
progress (nothing is lost until you press a generator), it has a negative phrasing that is already
authored twice as cue A and cue D, and *"spend now"* is a **rank** claim, which the left-to-right
scan already carries. That is Retribution's argument unchanged, and it applies to Soul Shards for
exactly the reasons it applies to Holy Power.

## New mechanism this catalog implies

Measured against the source vocabulary as it stood when this was written (`Catalog.PREDICATES` =
`ready`, `proc`, `identity`, `capped`, `affordable`, `resource`, `talent`, `aura`, `aoe`;
`Catalog.DISPLAYS` = `player-aura-stacks`, `sealed-power-percent`, `sealed-cooldown-range`).
⚠ **THAT DISPLAY LIST IS STALE AND THE ARGUMENTS BELOW REST ON IT** (2026-09-01).
`player-aura-stacks` and its `min = 2` guard were **retired** on 2026-08-22, and the live set has
grown `sealed-count-bands`, `sealed-count-bar`, `sealed-pandemic` and `sealed-proc-bar` since. Run
`uv run python -m wowkb.capart export catalog` for the current list rather than trusting this one.
Two of the Defeats below argue from *"no display kind exists"* and are re-opened by that growth —
see the ⚠ notes on Defeats 1 and 2:

1. **One new predicate is wanted, and it is not built here.** `pandemic(<row>)` — the readable
   boolean R8 already measures: `item.PandemicIcon ~= nil` on a Cooldown-Manager tab-1
   (target-DoT) row mirrors `IsInPandemicTime` exactly, is recomputed every frame, and is
   **never secret** (`knowledge/addon-dev/cooldown-manager.md`, `[client 2026-07-31]`). R8 records
   it with *"no cap consumer yet"*; **Destruction is that consumer**, and cue E is 90 % of a DoT
   rule with the last 10 % missing. Naming it is stage 3's job; building it is stage 6's, and the
   standing rule is not to prebuild vocabulary. *Defeats*, item 1.
2. **No new display kind, and no display at all.** This catalog authors **zero** sealed cues:
   no `sealed-cooldown-range` (nothing reads another ability's cooldown remaining), no
   `sealed-power-percent` (Soul Shards are readable), and — the surprise — **no
   `player-aura-stacks`**, because Backdraft's single-target rung asks `stack<1`, which is the
   `aura` latch's boolean. **Every marker in this catalog is a readable Lua condition.** It is
   the first catalog of which that is true.
3. **First catalog to use the `aura` predicate on a target DoT as an ordering rule.**
   Retribution authored the first `aura` marker (cue G, Expurgation) and used it as an *opener*
   gate; this uses it as a maintenance rule that fires every twenty seconds for the whole fight.
   The mechanism is unchanged; the airtime is not, and that is a flight question.
4. **First catalog to spend two positive cues.** `one_positive_per_entry` is the invariant that
   makes it safe and it is satisfied by construction — the two live on different rows.
5. **No new marker shape or channel pairing**, so under `../authoring.md`'s **marker seam** (stage 6's renderer test, the definition-of-done for Phase 9.4)
   this spec should edit **nothing** in `Treatment.lua` / `Overlay.lua` and be authored purely as
   catalog data.

## Defeats — the scenarios that beat this catalog, and what reopens them

Written to `../authoring.md`'s standard: the **scenario**, the **rung** it died on, and **what
would reopen it**. None of these is a bare "cannot".

1. **The DoT pandemic window.**
   *Scenario:* Immolate has 4 s left on an 18 s DoT. Rung 9 wants it refreshed — the pandemic
   window is open and clipping nothing — and cue E is still holding the row, so the walk points
   at Chaos Bolt and the DoT falls off or is refreshed late.
   *Rung it died on:* **the shelf, by one predicate.** Not the client: R8 measured the exact fact
   and found it **readable and never secret** — `item.PandemicIcon ~= nil` on a tab-1 row mirrors
   `IsInPandemicTime`, recomputed every frame `[client 2026-07-31]`. What is missing is a
   `pandemic` entry in `Catalog.PREDICATES` and a `readPandemic` in `Sense.lua`. The *number*
   stays sealed (`item.pandemicStartTime` is secret and `IsInPandemicTime(timeNow)` throws) and
   the catalog does not want it.
   *What would reopen it:* that predicate. Cue E becomes `aura(immolate) and !pandemic(immolate)`
   and rung 9 is expressed. ⚠ This is the single highest-value unbuilt thing for this spec, and
   it is a **boolean on a shipped, measured, never-secret field** — the cheapest reopening in any
   catalog.
   ⚠ **REOPENED 2026-09-01 — AND IT NO LONGER NEEDS THE PREDICATE.** V19 *Pandemic window*
   (`../render-shelf.md`) was promoted **2026-08-22** and does not go through `Catalog.PREDICATES`
   at all: `AddPandemicRegion` drives a client-owned region off `GetRefreshExtendedDuration −
   GetAuraBaseDuration`, per spell, and it is the one sealed display cap authors **no threshold**
   for. Its two-sided pair is exactly cue E's job — a gold hatch across the face while the aura is
   up but **outside** its refresh window (*do not refresh yet*), the badge once inside it — and it
   is recipe `S9` in `../authoring.md` with two shipped consumers (`demonology/catalog.md`'s
   `db_doom_window`, `protection/catalog.md`'s `ha_weapon_healthy`). Immolate's DoT `157736` is
   already established here as a Category-2 row in set 884, which is the subject V19 needs.
   ⚠ **This is the one thing in this file a stage-6 transcription would bake in.** Authoring
   `immolate_up` as a whole-DoT `blocked` from today's text ships a defeat that is expressible.
   Resolve this before writing `catalog.json`.
2. **The ritual clock.**
   *Scenario:* Diabolic Ritual's current stage has 3 s left. Rung 3 says spend a shard **now**, to
   consume the stage before it expires into an Art; rung 13's `ritual_length>4` says the ordinary
   dump does not apply. cap points at Chaos Bolt in both cases and cannot tell the two apart, and
   in the sliver between the cast time and 4 s it points there when the APL would not.
   *Rung it died on:* **the shelf.** `variable.ritual_length` is
   `buff.diabolic_ritual_mother_of_chaos.remains + buff.diabolic_ritual_overlord.remains +
   buff.diabolic_ritual_pit_lord.remains` — a **sum of three sealed aura remaining times**. cap
   has an aura-*duration* display (S5, `FormatRemainingDuration` → FontString, which renders in
   combat) but no aura-remaining **band**: nothing turns an aura's remaining time into a badge
   the way `sealed-cooldown-range` turns a cooldown's into one.
   *What would reopen it:* an S-form that runs S4's step curve on a `LuaDurationObject` obtained
   from `C_UnitAuras.GetAuraDuration` rather than from `GetSpellCooldownDuration`. Both return the
   same type and `Channel` already evaluates one; the shape is `sealed-cooldown-range` with a
   different acquisition. ⚠ The **sum** does not follow from it — three curves cannot be added
   without reading them — but the wheel is deterministic and exactly one stage is up at a time
   (`knowledge/classes/warlock/demonology/diabolist-sequences.md`, Tier 2), so a band on
   *whichever* stage is live is the same number. **That equivalence is an assumption and would
   need checking.** @verify-ingame
   ⚠ **The "no aura-remaining band" half is FALSE as of 2026-09-01 and the defeat must be
   re-scoped to the SUM.** Two later primitives take an aura's remaining time: V19's outside hatch
   *"rides `SetDurationText` band tables on the aura's remaining SECONDS"* and ships on Protection
   at `outside_s: 6`; V20's proc bar drains an aura's own remaining duration into a client-owned
   bar, and one of its declared consumers is *the armed Demonic Art on the Shadow Bolt row* — the
   same Diabolic Ritual mechanism this defeat calls inexpressible. So the missing sink is not
   missing. **What survives intact is the sum**, and the Tier-2 "exactly one stage is live"
   equivalence that would let a single-subject band stand in for it — which is what the
   `@verify-ingame` above is actually about. Re-scope this defeat to that assumption; do not carry
   "no display kind exists" into `catalog.json`.
3. **Shadowburn in execute range.**
   *Scenario:* the target is below 20 % health, no Fiendish Cruelty proc is up, and Conflagration
   of Chaos **is** talented. Rung 7 fires — its second conjunct is satisfied by the talent, its
   first by the execute clause — and cap draws nothing: cue H is gated off by the talent and cue
   I needs the proc. The walk points at Chaos Bolt.
   *Rung it died on:* **the client, and it is a shared open fact rather than a new one.** The gate
   is Shadowburn's own castability — *"Only usable on enemies that have less than 20 % health"*
   *[T1 @ 12.1.0.69214]* — which is `C_Spell.IsSpellUsable`'s **first** return. R1 says to use the
   second return and never the first, because `isUsable` is true for a spell on cooldown; that

   *What would reopen it:* one measurement — does `isUsable`'s first return read plain and correct
   in restricted combat for a spell gated by target health? If yes, an `usable` predicate expresses
   the execute gate with no new *kind* of read, and Retribution's Hammer-of-Wrath trick (the
   override *is* the gate) is not needed. @verify-ingame / @pending-test
   ⚠ **This was written as a measurement shared with Devourer and it is one no longer.** Devourer

   ⚠ **And note what the player sees meanwhile.** Out of execute without the proc, Blizzard

## Contract boundary

- **Emphasis and markers (Lua conditions) use only readable facts:** `ready` (R2), `affordable`
  (R1), `identity` (R7), `capped` (R6, at full only), `resource` (R3 — Soul Shards,
  never-secret), `proc` (the spell-activation overlay boolean), `aura` (the CDM alert-edge latch,
  player and target), `talent` (the trait config), and `aoe` — which is not a game read at all
  but cap's own toggle.
- **No sealed fact reaches any sink.** This catalog authors **no cue at all** in the sealed route:
  no `sealed-cooldown-range`, no `sealed-power-percent`, no `player-aura-stacks`. That is a
  first, and it is a consequence of the rungs rather than a policy.
- **No cooldown remaining, no aura duration, no aura stack count and no target health ever enters
  a Lua condition**, in either polarity.
- **Unknown never becomes confidence,** including through negation. A refused `affordable`,
  `resource`, `proc`, `capped`, `aura`, `identity` or `talent` withholds; it does not assert the
  opposite. Load-bearing in three places: cue **H** is a double negation, cue **E** rests on a
  latch whose row the player may never have enabled, and cue **B** rests on `capped`, whose
  underlying read **seals below full** — a refused `capped` must read as "not at maximum", which
  is the safe direction and is also, per R6, the truth.
- The catalog declares **no** graded resource curve, no aura-stack display, no continuous grade,
  no silence list, no elapsed counter, no cast sequence, no coverage rule, no countdown bar, and
  no vocabulary a cue does not use.
