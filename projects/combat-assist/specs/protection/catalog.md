## The design in one paragraph

Protection is **Retribution's mirror, and the surprise is which way**. Same class, same Holy
Power, and Holy Power is just as readable here as it is there — but **this catalog declares no
`power` field and authors no `resource` term at all**, because the 12.1 priority list contains no
`holy_power` term anywhere. The spender is placed by *position*, not by a number. What the
catalog spends instead is the two things Lightsmith actually hands the Cooldown Manager. The first
is an **override transform**: Sacred Weapon has no Cooldown-Manager row anywhere in the game, so
"which armament is next" — the APL's `next_armament`, which reads like an inaccessible internal —
is simply **which spell row 4 is displaying**, and `identity` (R7) reads it with no new vocabulary.
The second is a **sealed aura count**: Divine Guidance's stacks are the pivot the whole middle of
the priority turns on, and they are secret. So this is the first catalog where a sealed count
**rules out a row it does not belong to** — Divine Guidance's stack table is armed on *Avenger's
Shield* and hatches it, which is `spec.md` §3.1's readable-relationship rule applied to a fact cap
may not read. Seven cues do the work: **A** Avenging Wrath waiting for Divine Toll, **B** Divine
Toll waiting for Avenging Wrath on the build that has that rule, **C** the starved spender, **D**
Holy Bulwark banking for the window, **E** Avenger's Shield yielding to an armed Hammer of Wrath,
**F** Judgment yielding to an empowered hammer, and **G** Word of Glory without its free proc —
plus **H**, a talent gate that withholds **B** on the build almost everyone plays.

**No positive cue is spent, and this is the first catalog where that is a close call.** See *Why
this catalog does not spend a positive cue*.

*whether* there is a transform and says nothing about **which way round the identity reads** —
whether `base` is Holy Bulwark and `transformed` is Sacred Weapon, or the row simply displays
whichever armament is next with no override at all. **The DIRECTION is what is marked.**

**What the transforms buy.** Hammer of Wrath's execute gating is a target-health question cap has
no vocabulary for, and it does not need one: *the button's existence is the gate*. The same is
true of `next_armament`, which reads like sim-internal state and is in fact the only fact on row 4
cap can see for free.

⚠ **An absent swipe does not mean the underlying ability is ready.** For any transforming row the
Cooldown Manager's dial answers a question about the button **currently displayed** — the cooldown
branch resolves `overrideSpellID` before reading
(`knowledge/addon-dev/cooldown-manager.md` §3.1.1, settled 2026-08-18 on Retribution). This is
load-bearing here and not merely a caveat: cue E's `ready(judgment)` term means *Hammer of Wrath
is off cooldown* while the row is transformed, which is exactly the fact rung 16 needs.

### The Charges column

**No row in this catalog declares `charged`, and four rows are marked ⚠ open rather than left
blank.** `charged` is a static catalog declaration that switches a row's *readiness tracking* onto
the charge path (`Sense.seedBaseline`), and **cap has no talent-conditional form of it** — so
declaring one would let a talent choice decide whether a row reads ready at all. Retribution's
rule is the precedent and it is unchanged: *an unmeasured fact must never render as a measured
one.*

- **Judgment.** Its second charge comes only from **Crusader's Judgment `204023`**
  *[T1: `ability-inventory.tsv` @ 12.1.0.69214, `origin = talent-passive`]*, so the count is
  1-or-2 by talent. This is Blade of Justice's case exactly.
- **Holy Armaments.** Rung 23 is `holy_armaments,if=next_armament=holy_bulwark&charges=2`, so the
  APL is certain it has at least two — and **our Tier-1 data does not carry a charge count for it
  at all**. `ability-inventory.tsv` records `cd=0` and no charge column exists. Declaring
  `charged` here would be inventing the number the flag needs.
- **Shield of the Righteous** and **Crusader Strike** are marked for the same absence: neither
  carries a Tier-1 charge count in this repo, and the APL puts no charge term on either.

**Two consequences, and both are named as defeats rather than swallowed.** Rung 23 and rung 17
(`judgment,if=full_recharge_time<=gcd*2`) are the two rungs whose whole content is a charge fact,
and neither is authored — *Defeats*, items 4 and 5. And because there is no `charged`, **there is
no subject for the `capped` cue**, which is half of why this catalog spends no positive one.

## The authored row order

`Anchor.lua` re-anchors the Essential viewer to the catalog's `entries` array, so left-to-right
**is** the priority order and the elimination walk in `scenarios.md` reads it in that direction.

| # | Entry | `actions.default` rung |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Avenging Wrath | 5 |
| 2 | Divine Toll | 7 |
| 3 | Shield of the Righteous | 9 |
| 4 | Holy Bulwark / **Sacred Weapon** | **10** / 14, 23 |
| 5 | Avenger's Shield | 13 / 18 |
| 6 | Consecration | 15 / 19, 24, 29 |
| 7 | Judgment / **Hammer of Wrath** | 17, 22 / **16** |
| 8 | Crusader Strike / **HotR / Blessed Hammer** | 20, 21 / 25, 26 |
| 9 | Word of Glory | 28 |

**The order is the flattened APL with four documented conflicts, and every one of them is a row
whose rungs straddle another row's.** Protection has no sub-lists to compress, so unlike
Retribution there is no interleave to encode — what there is instead is four rows that each
appear two, three or four times in one list.

**Conflict 1: Consecration has the widest spread in any catalog so far — rungs 15, 19, 24 and
29.** Rung 15 (`buff.divine_guidance.stack>=5`) sits **above** Avenger's Shield 18, Hammer of
Wrath 16 and Judgment 17; the other three sit at the bottom. Position 6 encodes rung 19 — the
common case, correctly below Avenger's Shield 18 and above Judgment 22 — and the rung-15
promotion is the one the sealed count has to correct. **It corrects it from the other side**: the
hatch goes on *Avenger's Shield*, not on Consecration, because a sealed display can rule out the
row it is armed on and can never promote one. That asymmetry is the finding of this pass and it
is written up in full under *Defeats*, item 7.

**Conflict 2: Holy Armaments spans 10, 14 and 23 — one high rung and two low ones, and the
identity is which.** Rung 10 is the Sacred Weapon life, above everything from 13 down. Rungs 14
and 23 are the Holy Bulwark life: 14 is the Wings bank, 23 is the charge-cap dump below Judgment
22. Position 4 encodes rungs 10 and 14; cue **D** stands the row down for the gap between 14 and
23, and the charge-cap half of 23 is a defeat.

**Conflict 3: Judgment spans 16, 17 and 22, and only one of the three is readable.** Rung 16 is
Hammer of Wrath unconditional — above Avenger's Shield 18 and Consecration 19 — and it is
readable through the identity. Rung 17 is the second-charge dump and is not. Rung 22 is the plain
Judgment at the bottom, which position 7 encodes. **Position 7 is chosen deliberately over the
alternative** of putting Judgment above Avenger's Shield: rung 22 is the common state and rung 16
is the rare one, and `../render-shelf.md` Part 0.5's whole model is to badge the state the player
is in less often.

**Conflict 4: Crusader Strike's row spans 20–21 and 25–26.** Rungs 20 and 21 are the Blessed
Assurance empowerment, above Judgment 22; rungs 25 and 26 are the plain filler, below it. Position
8 encodes the filler, and **the correction lives entirely on Judgment** (cue F) — the row that has
to move. Badging the *press* would be a promotion and this catalog does not spend one.

**Documented misorderings — what is left after the cues.**

1. **Divine Toll on a Righteous Protector build wears nothing at all**, so at position 2 a ready
   Divine Toll stops the walk in every state where the APL holds it. This is the largest hole in
   the catalog and it is *Defeats*, item 1.
2. **A Consecration whose ground effect is still down cannot be told from one whose is up**, so
   rungs 19 and 24 are not modelled and position 6 over-fires against rungs 22, 25, 26 and 28.
   *Defeats*, item 2.
3. **Consecration is one rung late while Hammer of Wrath is armed and the Divine Guidance aura is
   absent, and the cause is a deletion rather than an error.** The yield on Consecration is the
   sealed complement, gated on Divine Guidance being below five; at five or more with Hammer of
   Wrath armed, rung 15 outranks rung 16 and the walk correctly stops on Consecration. The
   **absent-aura** state — a **Blessed Assurance** build, plus one global after a five-stack
   Consecration on a Divine Guidance build — was covered by a readable marker, and that marker was
   **correct there**: rung 15 is `consecration,if=buff.divine_guidance.stack>=5`, so without the
   Divine Guidance talent the count can never reach five, **rung 15 is dead on that build**, and
   Hammer of Wrath's rung 16 genuinely outranks every surviving Consecration rung (19, 24 and 29).
   The marker was deleted for the **density budget**, not for being wrong (*Defeats*, item 7), so
   what remains is a one-rung throughput loss in exactly that state and never a forbidden press.
   The deletion is a density trade, and this line is where it is paid.
4. **Rung 17 and the charge half of rung 23 are not modelled** (*The Charges column*).
5. **`apex.3` on rung 13's second disjunct is not a game fact.** It is absent from `talents.json`,
   from `ability-inventory.tsv` and from the whole repo; it is a sim-side Apex rank. The
   `buff.vanguard.up` half of the same rung stands and is authored.

## The groups, and why the priority falls out of them

One row conditions its membership — Shield of the Righteous declares
`scan_when = { { affordable(self) } }`, because a spender whose cost is the only thing between
it and a press raises no readiness edge for that cost. Every other row is a default ready-self
scan member, and the groups below are prose — how the roster reads, not anything the engine
holds.

- **Pressed on sight** — Avenging Wrath, Divine Toll, Holy Armaments: rungs 5, 7, 10/14/23, the
  top of the list. Two of the three carry a `sealed-cooldown-range` hold and the third is
  withheld by a talent gate, which makes this the **most sealed pressed-on-sight group in any
  catalog**: not one of these three rows carries a readable reason to wait. That is the reverse
  of Demonology, whose only hold on a placed cooldown is a readable resource comparison.
- **The decision surface** — Shield of the Righteous, Avenger's Shield, Consecration, Judgment /
  Hammer of Wrath. The spender leads the group by *position alone* — there is no `resource` term
  to place it with — and the three builders behind it are ordered by two readable holds and two
  sealed count tables.
- **Reached by subtraction** — Crusader Strike (as Hammer of the Righteous or Blessed Hammer)
  and Word of Glory. Both rows have a drawn subject on every build: the spec choice node at 3,19
  guarantees one of the two hammers exists, so unlike Retribution's Crusading Strikes case the
  row is never absent.

Membership + cues, read together, reproduce the priority; that is the §3.1 point. **This catalog
has no conditional-membership transform** — no row's membership moves with its identity.
Retribution's row 3 needed one because a transform changed what *kind* of button the row was;
neither transform here does. Sacred Weapon and Holy Bulwark are both armament grants, Hammer of
Wrath and Judgment are both builders, and Blessed Assurance makes the hammer *better* rather
than different. **A rank change within one kind is what the scan already carries**, so a
membership condition would be a claim with no consumer.

## Roster — player problem → fact → recipe → treatment

### Pressed on sight

- **Avenging Wrath** (`31884`, rung 5). *Problem:* Wings is not a press-on-cooldown button on this
  build. Its value is set by what lands inside it — Divine Toll's five-target Holy Power dump,
  and on Lightsmith the extra Sacred Weapon that Blessing of the Forge summons — so pressing it
  while Divine Toll is most of a minute away spends the window on nothing. The icon says nothing
  about Divine Toll's clock. *Facts:* `ready` (R2) for membership; **Divine Toll's cooldown
  remaining**, which is sealed. *Treatment:* scan +
  - `aw_awaits_toll` — **sealed** `blocked`, cue **A**: S4 `sealed-cooldown-range`,
    **`beyond = 10`** on Divine Toll, gated on the readable `ready(avenging_wrath)`.
  ⚠ **The sense is `beyond`, and getting it backwards would invert the button.** Rung 5 *fires* at
  `cooldown.divine_toll.remains<=10`, so the **hold** is the complement — Divine Toll has at least
  ten seconds left, which is `beyond`'s meaning exactly: *"it is nowhere near, so this is not its
  moment."* `within = 10` would hold Wings in the one window the APL presses it. The band also
  reads nothing at zero remaining, which is the correct behaviour here rather than a caveat: a
  *ready* Divine Toll satisfies `remains<=10` and must not hold anything.
  The `ready` gate is the standard one — `Treatment.For` passes cues through for rows the Cooldown
  Manager has already swiped, and a hold badge on a greyed icon says nothing.
- **Divine Toll** (`375576`, rung 7). *Problem:* it is the spec's largest single Holy Power
  injection and the seed of its area damage, so it belongs *inside* Wings; pressed outside, the
  window it was saved for arrives without it. *Facts:* `ready` (R2); `buff.avenging_wrath.up`;
  Avenging Wrath's own cooldown remaining (sealed); `talent(righteous_protector)`. *Treatment:*
  scan +
  - `dt_awaits_wrath` — **sealed** `blocked`, cue **B**: S4 `sealed-cooldown-range`,
    **`within = 30`** on Avenging Wrath, gated on `ready(divine_toll)` **and** the readable
    `!talent(righteous_protector)` (cue **H**).
  ⚠ **The rung has two disjuncts and cap can express only the one almost nobody plays.** Rung 7 is
  `buff.avenging_wrath.up | (!talent.righteous_protector.enabled & cooldown.avenging_wrath.remains>30)`.
  The first disjunct is Avenging Wrath's **buff**, and Avenging Wrath's buff lives on a
  **Category-3 (TrackedBar)** row in set 637 (OrderIndex 27) rather than a TrackedBuff one.
  Whether a TrackedBar row raises the `aura` latch's `OnAuraApplied` / `OnAuraRemoved` alert edges
  is **unmeasured**, and a latch built on an unmeasured edge is a confident hold that may never
  release. **Not authored.** *Defeats*, item 1. @verify-ingame
  ⚠ **So the whole of cue B is the second disjunct, and the talent gate is what keeps it honest.**
  `within = 30` is the complement of `remains>30`, and on a build without Righteous Protector it
  is correct everywhere the band can read at all: during the 20-second window the remaining time
  is 120 → 100, well beyond the band, so the badge is dark exactly when the press is right.
  ⚠ **The one place it says nothing is `remains = 0`.** A `within` band means *imminent*, never
  *ready* (`Channel.HoldPoints`), so while Avenging Wrath sits off cooldown and uncast the hold is
  the correct reading — the APL presses Divine Toll on neither disjunct there — and cap draws no
  badge. That is a missed hold rather than a wrong one, and it is the same zero-remaining blind
  spot every sealed band has; it is named here because *"correct across the entire cooldown"* is
  what this paragraph used to claim, and the cooldown has an endpoint the band cannot see. **Righteous Protector halves that cooldown** *[T1 tooltip @ 12.1.0.69214, via
  `builds.md`]*, which both deletes the disjunct from the APL and would leave a 30-second band
  covering half of a 60-second cycle — a rule the priority does not contain. Righteous Protector
  is **near-mandatory** (`builds.md` → *the load-bearing talents*), so **cue B is withheld on the

#### Why no row wears `overcap`

**This catalog spends `overcap` nowhere, and unlike Retribution the reason is not a judgment call
— there is no number to author it from.** `actions.default` contains **no `holy_power` term of any
kind**: not on Divine Toll, not on Avenger's Shield, not on Judgment, not on Crusader Strike.
Retribution's `dt_overcap` was authored *from the APL's own `holy_power=5`*, and the provenance
was the whole argument for the number; here there is no such term to lift. Authoring `>= 5` anyway
would be deriving the threshold from a maximum, which is precisely what Retribution's note refuses.

**And position already does the work the badge would do.** Every generator in this catalog —
Avenger's Shield (5), Consecration (6), Judgment (7), Crusader Strike (8) — sits **below** Shield
of the Righteous at position 3, so at cap the walk stops on the spender and never reaches them.
The one generator *above* the spender is **Divine Toll at position 2**, and badging it would be
Retribution's Wake of Ashes mistake in a new coat: rung 7 sits above rung 9 with no resource term
on it, so the APL genuinely presses Divine Toll before the spender **at any Holy Power**, and what
that button is worth is the window, not the generation. The badge that would be honest about
overflow is the badge that would be **wrong about the press**.

**The consequence is that this catalog declares no `power` field.** It is the first Paladin
catalog to declare none, and the first catalog of any spec whose primary resource is *readable*
and goes unread. `Catalog.Check` requires `power` only when a `resource` term exists; none does.

### Why this catalog does not spend a positive cue

**There is no positive-cue budget** (`../backlog.md`), so the answer has to be on merits — and here
it is the closest call in any catalog so far, because unlike Retribution and Demonology this one
has a state where the density rule genuinely asks for a promotion and cannot have it.

1. **`capped` has no subject.** It is scoped to impending loss of a *charge*, and **no row in this
   catalog declares `charged`** (*The Charges column*). Two rungs — 17 and 23 — are pure charge
   facts, and both are defeats rather than cues. There is nothing to lose that cap can see.
2. **`priority` has a candidate, and the candidate is unreachable.** In one real state the walk
   cost **three** budgeted holds before the press: Shield of the Righteous unaffordable (unbudgeted
   — `starved` restates a resource the player is already reading), Holy Armaments held by cue D,
   Avenger's Shield held by cue E, Consecration held by the readable marker that covered the
   absent-aura case, press Hammer of Wrath. ⚠ **That state is a Blessed Assurance build**
   (`scenarios.md` → *PROT-13*), not the Divine-Guidance-below-five state this item first named:
   with the aura present Consecration is `ruled-sealed` and unbudgeted, which is two holds and
   passes. `../render-shelf.md` Part 0.5 is explicit that three or more holds is where *"elimination is the
   wrong tool"* and the answer is to promote the press instead — which here would be a `priority`
   marker on the Judgment row reading `identity(judgment) == transformed` **and**
   `ready(judgment)`. **Both terms are readable, and the promotion is still wrong**, for a reason
   that is not about readability at all: pass 1 is pre-emptive, so a promotion must be true against
   *everything* to its left, and Hammer of Wrath is outranked by Consecration whenever Divine
   Guidance is capped. Gating the promotion on that would mean cap comparing a sealed count. So the
   promotion fails on the boundary itself. *Defeats*, item 7, and it is the finding of this pass.
3. **Nothing else is a loss in progress.** A lapsed Sacred Weapon is the nearest thing, and cap
   cannot see it (*Defeats*, item 3) — its reopening condition is an aura-remaining band, not a
   positive cue.

⚠ **The walk settled it, and it deleted a hold rather than adding a badge.** The density rule is
enforced per **scenario** in `wowkb.capart check` and cannot be evaluated from this document, so it
was deferred to `scenarios.md`, which found **two** overflowing trios rather than one and deleted
their common term — Consecration's readable half of cue E. *Defeats*, item 7 carries the whole
argument; the catalog still spends no positive cue.

⚠ **Two mutual exclusions bound the pressed-on-sight group at two budgeted holds**, and they are
the reason no third trio can be built out of that group. **Cues A and B can never both draw**: A needs Divine
Toll on cooldown, B needs it ready. **Cues A and D can never both draw**: A needs Avenging Wrath
ready, D needs it beyond five seconds. Both fell out of the walk and neither was stated when the
cues were authored.

## New mechanism this catalog implies

Measured against the current source vocabulary (`Catalog.PREDICATES` = `ready`, `proc`, `identity`,
`capped`, `affordable`, `resource`, `talent`, `aura`, `aoe`; `Catalog.DISPLAYS` =
`sealed-count-bands`, `sealed-count-bar`, `sealed-pandemic`, `sealed-proc-bar`,
`sealed-power-percent`, `sealed-cooldown-range`):

⚠ **`sealed-proc-bar` was missing from that list until 2026-08-26, and what it dates is the
*Defeats* section below.** The list was written before V20 shipped and before V19 grew
`outside_s`, so every *"no S-form exists for this"* claim under *Defeats* was measured against a
shorter vocabulary than the one in `Catalog.lua` today. Those claims have **not** been re-argued
here; treat each as needing a re-read against the current forms before it is cited.

1. **No new predicate and no new display kind.** Every readable term above is one of the nine, and
   both sealed forms are ones the shelf already declares. `proc`, `resource`, `capped`,
   `sealed-power-percent`, `sealed-count-bar`, `sealed-pandemic` and `sealed-proc-bar` are all
   unused — the largest unused fraction of the vocabulary in any catalog, and the reason is the
   priority list rather than the design.
2. **First sealed display armed on one entry to rule out that entry on ANOTHER row's aura.**
   `as_guidance_capped` puts Divine Guidance's count on Avenger's Shield. The mechanism is
   unchanged — `Channel.Plan` has always read `display.ability` independently of `entry.ability`,
   and Demonology exercised the *field* — but every shipped use so far has been a self-test, and
   this is the first time the statement is about a **relationship** between two rows. It is
   `../spec.md` §3.1's readable-relationship rule extended to a fact cap may not read, and it is
   worth a test.
3. **First load-bearing readable gate on a V17 complement, and the first time a gate makes a band
   TRUE rather than merely narrow.** Demonology's `db_doom_window` gate decided whether a display
   was painted at all; `cons_awaits_hammer`'s gate decides whether the complement's low band is an
   honest elimination. Without the gate the same table would be a lie. The
   `when`-beside-`display` shape (fixed 2026-08-22) is what makes it possible and this is its
   second consumer.
4. **First catalog with no `power` field whose primary resource is readable.** Havoc and
   Demonology had a resource story because their APLs carried resource terms; Retribution's was the
   whole design. Protection's 12.1 list carries none, so the field is absent by measurement rather
   than by omission — and that is worth writing down, because "Paladin ⇒ declare `HolyPower`" is
   exactly the kind of inference a second Paladin catalog invites.
5. **The unwritten S-form is the same one Retribution wanted: an aura-remaining band.** Rung 10's

   `C_UnitAuras.GetAuraDuration`'s duration object takes the same step curve S4 already puts on a

6. **A wanted mechanism that is NOT an S-form, and probably cannot be one.** A **positive cue the

   described precisely under *Defeats*, item 7, and the reason it is listed here is that the next
   author should not spend a round rediscovering that it is a boundary rather than a gap.

## Defeats — the scenarios that beat this catalog, and what reopens them

Written to `../authoring.md`'s standard: the **scenario**, the **rung** it died on, and **what
would reopen it**. None of these is a bare "cannot".

1. **Divine Toll outside the window, on the build almost everyone plays.**
   *Scenario:* Righteous Protector is talented, Avenging Wrath is down, Divine Toll is ready. Rung
   7 reduces on that build to `buff.avenging_wrath.up`, which is false, so the APL skips it — and
   cue B is withheld by the talent gate, so Divine Toll wears nothing and the walk stops on it at
   position 2.
   *Rung it died on:* **the client.** Avenging Wrath's buff is a **Category-3 (TrackedBar)** row in
   set 637 (OrderIndex 27), not a TrackedBuff one, and whether a TrackedBar row raises the `aura`
   latch's `OnAuraApplied` / `OnAuraRemoved` alert edges is unmeasured
   (`knowledge/addon-dev/cooldown-manager.md` §5.1). A latch built on an edge that may never fire
   is a hold that may never release, which is worse than silence. This is Demonology's Dominion of
   Argus defeat on a different aura, and it is now **two specs blocked on one measurement**.
   *What would reopen it:* one measurement — does a Category-3 row raise the alert edges? If it
   does, `aura(avenging_wrath)` is a readable boolean and cue B is replaced by a marker that is
   correct on **both** builds, deleting the talent gate H with it. @verify-ingame
2. **Consecration whose ground effect is still down — or still up.**
   *Scenario:* Consecration is off cooldown and its ground effect is still ticking under the
   player. Rungs 19 and 24 (`!consecration.up`) are false, so the APL presses Hammer of the
   Righteous, Judgment or Word of Glory instead and only reaches Consecration at rung 29. cap
   draws nothing and position 6 takes the press.
   *Rung it died on:* **the client, the same rung as item 1.** Consecration's duration is a
   Category-3 (TrackedBar) row in set 637 (OrderIndex 24).
   *What would reopen it:* the same single measurement. Both defeats close together or neither
   does, which is the argument for doing it. @verify-ingame
3. **A Sacred Weapon buff about to lapse.**
   *Scenario:* row 4 is displaying Sacred Weapon and the buff has four seconds left. Rung 10 fires
   on `buff.sacred_weapon.remains<6|!buff.sacred_weapon.up`, putting it above everything from rung
   13 down; cap draws nothing and the row is indistinguishable from the same row with eighteen
   seconds left, which the APL skips.
   *Rung it died on:* **the shelf, for the first term, and the census for the second.** An
   **aura-remaining band** is an S-form nobody has written — S4's step curve is authored on a
   cooldown, and the same curve over `C_UnitAuras.GetAuraDuration`'s duration object is the missing
   piece (`../authoring.md`'s recipe index, S5). The `!buff.sacred_weapon.up` half is a different
   problem: this repo's DB2 census does not establish that Sacred Weapon's buff holds a Category-2
   row in set 637, so even the boolean is unsourced.
   *What would reopen it:* writing the aura-remaining S-form — which Retribution also wants, so it
   now has two consumers — plus one census read for the Category-2 row. Neither is a client
   question. @verify-ingame
4. **A Holy Armaments charge about to cap.**
   *Scenario:* row 4 is displaying Holy Bulwark, both charges are up, and Avenging Wrath is a
   minute away. Rung 23 (`…&charges=2`) puts it above the filler; cue D holds it anyway, so the
   walk steps over a press the APL makes and a charge may be lost.
   *Rung it died on:* **catalog grammar, and missing Tier-1 data.** `charged` is a static
   declaration with no talent-conditional form, and Holy Armaments' charge count is not in this
   repo's Tier-1 game data at all (`ability-inventory.tsv` carries no charge column and records
   `cd=0`). Declaring it would be inventing the number, and `capped` needs it to have a subject.
   *What would reopen it:* a measured charge count for Holy Armaments — at which point `capped`
   becomes authorable on this row and would be the catalog's one positive cue, since a stalled
   recharge at two charges is loss in progress with no negative phrasing.
5. **Judgment's second charge about to cap.**
   *Scenario:* Crusader's Judgment `204023` is talented, one charge is banked and the second is two
   globals from full. Rung 17 (`full_recharge_time<=gcd*2`) puts Judgment above Avenger's Shield 18
   and Consecration 19; cap draws nothing.
   *Rung it died on:* **the same rung as item 4**, plus one that is specific to it — the charge
   count is **talent-conditional** (1 without Crusader's Judgment, 2 with), and `charged` cannot be
   made conditional. This is Retribution's Blade of Justice case verbatim.
   *What would reopen it:* a talent-conditional `charged`, which is a `Catalog.lua` + `Sense.lua`
   change and not a client question. Note that item 4 needs a **measurement** and this one needs a
   **mechanism**; they do not close together.
6. **`apex.3`.**
   *Scenario:* rung 13's second disjunct is `buff.avenging_wrath.up&apex.3`.
   *Rung it died on:* **the source.** `apex.3` is not a talent, not a buff, and appears nowhere in
   `talents.json`, `ability-inventory.tsv` or this repo — it is a sim-side Apex rank, which is
   simulation state and not a player fact, exactly as Retribution treats `raid_event.adds`.
   *What would reopen it:* nothing, and it should not. The `buff.vanguard.up` half of the rung is
   authored and carries the readable content.
7. **A sealed count cannot promote a row, and that is the finding of this pass.** ⚠ **RESOLVED
   2026-08-23 by `scenarios.md`, by deleting a hold** — the general finding below stands unchanged
   and is the part worth carrying.
   *Scenario:* a **Blessed Assurance build** in execute range, so the Divine Guidance aura does not
   exist and neither count table has a subject. Shield of the Righteous is unaffordable, Holy
   Bulwark is armed with Avenging Wrath far away, Hammer of Wrath is armed and off cooldown,
   Avenger's Shield and Consecration are both ready. Cue D, `as_awaits_hammer` and the readable
   `!aura(divine_guidance)` marker stand down three rows in sequence before the walk reaches Hammer
   of Wrath — `../render-shelf.md` Part 0.5's budget is **two**. That is `scenarios.md` → *PROT-13*.
   ⚠ **The state this item first named — Divine Guidance below five with the aura PRESENT — is not
   the state that overflows.** There Consecration is `ruled-sealed` by the V17 low band:
   client-drawn, carrying no cue, and outside a budget that counts only `blocked`. It costs **one**
   budgeted hold and passes, and it is *PROT-8*.
   ⚠ **And there are two such trios, not one.** `{D, as_awaits_hammer, cons_no_guidance}` and
   `{A, as_awaits_hammer, cons_no_guidance}` — cue A substitutes for cue D whenever Avenging Wrath
   is ready with Divine Toll more than ten seconds out, in the same execute state.
   *Rung it died on:* **the boundary, not the shelf and not the client.** Part 0.5's prescribed
   remedy is to promote the press with `priority`, and the promotion's own condition
   (`identity(judgment) == transformed` and `ready(judgment)`) is fully readable. What defeats it
   is that **pass 1 is pre-emptive**: a promotion is a claim about every row to its left, and
   Consecration outranks Hammer of Wrath whenever Divine Guidance is capped (rung 15 above rung
   16). Gating the promotion on that would require cap to compare a sealed count, which is §3.6
   itself. And the obvious inversion does not exist either: a `sealed-count-bands` band carries no
   cue and takes no part in pass 1 — `tokens.verdicts["ruled-sealed"]` declares `eliminates`, which
   is pass 2 only. **Stated generally, and this is what to carry to the next catalog: a sealed
   display may DEMOTE the row it is armed on, and may never PROMOTE any row, because promotion is a
   comparison across rows and cap is the only party that can perform one.**
   *What would reopen it:* a **readable** substitute for the Divine Guidance count, which would
   make the promotion's gate expressible and delete three markers with it — the shelf's own test
   for a promotion is that it should *remove* authored vocabulary, and here it would remove four.
   Not a shelf gap: giving a band a pass-1 cue would mean cap reading which band fired.
   *How it was resolved:* a hold was deleted rather than a badge added. The walk removed
   `cons_no_guidance`, the **common term of both trios** and the only marker in the catalog whose
   whole job is converting a free `ruled-sealed` elimination into a budgeted one; the two
   alternatives cost more (cue D moves the press to Holy Bulwark at rung 23, `as_awaits_hammer`
   costs two rungs in every execute state on every build). The price is *documented misordering 3*:
   Consecration presses one rung late in the absent-aura state. **It was deleted for density, not
   for being wrong** — on a Blessed Assurance build rung 15 is dead, so it was correct there.
   *What reopens it:* a **readable** substitute for the Divine Guidance count, or a measured
   Category-2 row that makes the count readable — either makes the marker free again and makes the
   promotion's gate expressible.

8. **A three-hold trio that survives the deletion, on a doubly-rare build.**
   *Scenario:* a **non-Righteous-Protector, Blessed Assurance** build with Avenger's Shield *and*
   Consecration both swiped. Divine Toll is held by cue **B**, Holy Armaments by cue **D** and
   Judgment by cue **F**, so three budgeted holds stand before the press lands on Blessed Hammer.
   *Rung it died on:* **rung 25** — the filler — with `../render-shelf.md` Part 0.5's budget of two
   already spent. `scenarios.md` leaves it in place deliberately: every term of it is a conjunction
   of rarities (the build gate H exists to withhold from, the half of the hero choice node the
   sealed tables cannot serve, and two swiped decision-surface rows at once), and deleting a second marker
   to cover it would cost more than the state is worth.
   *What would reopen it:* a flight that finds the state at all. ⚠ **If one does, cue B is the
   candidate to delete, not cue D** — B exists only on that build, and *Defeats*, item 1's single
   measurement would replace it outright.

## What is cut, and why

An ability with no named player problem gets no row; so does one with no Cooldown-Manager row.

- **Ardent Defender `31850`** (Essential, OrderIndex 21) and **Guardian of Ancient Kings `86659`**
  (OrderIndex 22). Both are in the Essential set and **neither appears in any `actions.default`
  rung** — `actions.default` is a *damage* priority and schedules no survival cooldown at all. Per
  stage 2 an ability with no named player problem gets no row, and "press this when you are about
  to die" is not a problem a rotation hint solves. ⚠ Guardian of Ancient Kings reads
  **`cd = 8`** in `ability-inventory.tsv`, which is **correct data and not a bug**: 12.1 gave it an
  8-second *initial* cooldown on a two-stage cooldown *[T1: `knowledge/_meta/patch-notes/12.1.md`,
  via `builds.md`]*. A future author reading `cd=8` as corrupt would be wrong.
- **The three Templar rungs** — 8 `hammer_of_light`, 11 `hammer_of_wrath,if=buff.hammer_of_light_ready.up…`
  and 12 `judgment,if=buff.hammer_of_light_ready.up…`. Rungs 11 and 12 collapse into the later
  unconditional 16 and 22, so filtering them costs nothing; rung 8's button does not exist on this
  build. A spec-and-hero pair is the unit, and Templar is a separate catalog.
- **The whole of `actions.precombat`** — `rite_of_sanctification`, `rite_of_adjuration`,
  `snapshot_stats`, `devotion_aura`, `lights_judgment` and an opening `consecration`. The two Rites
  are an hour-long out-of-combat choice node, Devotion Aura is a permanent aura, `lights_judgment`
  is a racial and the Consecration is a row already bound. Zero new cap rows.
- **`auto_attack` (1), `use_item,name=algethar_puzzle_box` (2), `use_items` (3), `potion` (4)** —
  not spells.
- **`fireblood` (6) and `arcane_torrent` (27)** — racials, present on two races. A button four
  fifths of the playerbase does not have is not a roster entry.
- **`apex.3`** — sim-side (*Defeats*, item 6).
- **Sacred Weapon, Hammer of the Righteous, Blessed Hammer and Hammer of Wrath** — not cut as
  abilities but folded into the rows the game data puts them on. Merges, not cuts.
- **Rebuke `96231`** — the spec's interrupt, and it does not appear in `actions.default` at all.
  Cut for Retribution's reasons, unchanged: its press condition is *"the target is casting
  something interruptible"*, which is target-cast state cap has no vocabulary for; it lives in the
  **Utility** viewer, which `Anchor.lua` does not order and the elimination walk does not read; and
  an interrupt competes with the whole rotation for one global, so nothing cap could draw would
  change when you press it.
- **The 16 Category-1 and the other 39 Category-2 rows in set 637** — in the set, not in the
  priority.

## Contract boundary

- **Emphasis and markers (Lua conditions) use only readable facts:** `ready` (R2), `affordable`
  (R1), `identity` (R7), `talent` (the trait config), and `aura` — the CDM alert-edge latch's
  boolean, on four Category-2 rows and nowhere else. **`resource` is not used and the catalog
  declares no `power`**; `proc`, `capped` and `aoe` are not used either.
- **Cues and displays carry sealed facts to client-owned sinks:** three `sealed-cooldown-range`
  bands (Divine Toll's cooldown once, Avenging Wrath's cooldown twice) and two
  `sealed-count-bands` tables, both on Divine Guidance's application count and running in opposite
  directions. cap authors the window in seconds and the breakpoints in whole counts, and nothing
  else. The client evaluates every one of them and cap reports `offered` / `armed` / `refused` and
  never reads back. **Accepted is not drawn** (`../authoring.md` → *Accepted is not drawn*).
- **No cooldown remaining, no aura duration, no aura stack count and no target state ever enters a
  Lua condition**, in either polarity. ⚠ That holds with both count tables drawing: a band table is
  a rule cap hands over, not a comparison cap performs, and cap never learns which band fired. The
  one readable thing about an aura anywhere here is the `aura` latch's boolean — *is there one at
  all* — which is a different fact from *how many*, and the two are never mixed in one condition.
- **Unknown never becomes confidence,** including through negation. A refused `affordable`,
  `identity`, `talent` or `aura` withholds; it does not assert the opposite. This is load-bearing
  **three times over on the `aura` and `talent` latches**, and every one of those is a
  **negation**: cue G's whole content is `!aura(shining_light)`, cue E's Avenger's Shield half turns
  on `!aura(vanguard)`, and cue H is `!talent(righteous_protector)`. (A fourth,
  `!aura(divine_guidance)`, went with the marker deleted at *Defeats*, item 7.) An unbound
  Cooldown-Manager row — a player who never enabled that tracked buff — must leave those rows
  unbadged rather than held. Cue C's `!affordable(shield_of_the_righteous)` is a fourth negation and
  is listed apart because R1's failure mode differs: a spender with no cooldown of its
  own raises no readiness edge, so a refused affordability leaves the row clean rather than latched.
- The catalog declares **no** graded resource curve, no sealed radial, no pandemic window, no
  continuous grade, no silence list, no elapsed counter, no cast sequence, no coverage rule, **no
  countdown bar**, no two-band entry, and no vocabulary a cue does not use.
