## The design in one paragraph

Havoc's defining constraint is that its primary resource, **Fury, is secret** (R3): cap may
*display* Fury but never branch on its value. The roster maps onto the §3.1 role lanes the way
the Tier-1 APL does — **cooldown-dominated**, with the raw Fury dump (Chaos Strike /
Annihilation) at rung 21/23, near the **bottom**, rising only inside a window. So the
**COOLDOWN** lane carries the burst / window buttons, the **ROTATION** lane carries the
build/spend core, and cap uses cap's **two tools** — *emphasis* (readable on/off) and *cues*
(additive display of sealed values) — to reproduce that order. Seven cues do the work: **A**
affordability, **B** overcap, **C** hold/sync, **D** demon-form promotion, **E** charges capped
(the one positive cue), **F** the talent gate that withholds E, and **G** the single-target skip
that delivers the one target-count term the APL has. Over it all runs the **demon-form
transform** as the readable identity spine (R7).

### The Charges column, and what it does *not* mean

**Charges are a render fact, not a priority fact.** `../render-shelf.md` V2 says an ability wears
exactly one border and that the **CHARGES** lane *replaces* the role lane when the client reports
charges. So the `Lane` column above stays exactly as the rotation authored it — Immolation Aura is
**ROTATION**, and always was — and this column only tells the renderer that its border will draw
purple instead of blue. Nothing here re-ranks anything.

Sources, per `knowledge/classes/demon-hunter/havoc/abilities.md` (12.1.0.69214):

- **Immolation Aura — 2**, with **A Fire Inside**. Without that talent it is a single-charge
  button, which is one of the reasons the "don't cap the charges" cue (R6) is the interesting one
  on this row at all.
- **Fel Rush — 2** (~10 s recharge), baseline.
- **Throw Glaive — `yes`**, ~9 s recharge. The *count* is not Tier-1 sourced: the tsv's `cooldown`
  column is `max(RecoveryTime, CategoryRecoveryTime)` and returns the **GCD** for a charge ability,
  so it settles nothing, and the real recharge lives in `SpellCategory.ChargeRecoveryTime` which is
  unreachable without breaking the build pin. `yes` is deliberately not a guessed number — the
  border only needs the boolean.
- **Vengeful Retreat — ⚠ open, and it therefore draws COOLDOWN.** It read **25 s** in the
  12.0.7.67808 tsv and **0.5 s** at 12.1.0.69214, which is the charge-ability artifact, and 12.1
  gives Devourer's Hungering Slash *"a temporary charge of Vengeful Retreat"*. That is suggestive,
  not measured, and an unmeasured fact must never render as a measured one. `@verify-ingame`
  (Vengeful Retreat cooldown / charges) is already open in `abilities.md`; when it resolves, this
  cell becomes a number and the border colour follows.

⚠ **A finding, surfaced rather than hidden:** both FALLBACK abilities in this catalog (Throw Glaive,
Fel Rush) have charges, so under the substitution **no Havoc row ever draws a FALLBACK border**.
The lane is still declared and still correct; it simply has no subject in this spec. `capart build`
prints this as a page-level note rather than letting the lane silently vanish, and it is
`../render-shelf.md` Part 5's question 3 — whether a fourth colour that displaces the third is
carrying meaning or eating it.

## The authored row order

`Catalogs/Havoc.lua`'s `entries` array **is** the authored priority, and `Anchor.lua` re-anchors
the Cooldown Manager's Essential viewer to it. Twelve entries:

| # | Entry | APL rung |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Vengeful Retreat | 5 — but see below |
| 2 | Metamorphosis | 3 |
| 3 | The Hunt | 4 |
| 4 | Eye Beam | 14 |
| 5 | Essence Break | 17 |
| 6 | Blade Dance / Death Sweep | 18/19 |
| 7 | Immolation Aura / Consuming Fire | 20 |
| 8 | Chaos Strike / Annihilation | 21/23 |
| 9 | Felblade | 22/24 |
| 10–12 | Demon's Bite · Fel Rush · Throw Glaive | outside the 12.1 damage priority |

**The order is talent-invariant.** Nearly every talent that touches this priority changes a
*condition*, and a condition is a cue, not a rung: Chaotic Transformation gates Metamorphosis's
hold, Eternal Hunt gates The Hunt's, Violent Transformation and Demonic Intensity add transient
Immolation Aura rungs. None of them moves a button's steady-state rung, so `Anchor.lua` needs no
talent awareness and no dynamic plan. (Aldrachi Reaver *inserts* a button — `reavers_glaive`,
rung 9 — rather than reordering the shared nine, which is what makes a later one-catalog merge
viable.)

**Vengeful Retreat sits at 1 even though the APL evaluates it at rung 5**, because it is **off
the global cooldown**: simc evaluating it after The Hunt does not mean "press it after The Hunt",
it means it fires *alongside* the global-cooldown press. List position carries no ordering
information for an off-GCD ability. In play the sequence is Vengeful Retreat **then** The Hunt —
the retreat is what the charge closes — and position 1 matches how the row is read: glance at the
weave, then scan for the press.

### Immolation Aura's rung is set by CHARGES, not by target count

This is the correction that matters most, because an earlier draft of this section got it wrong
in a way that made the row look worse than it is. Immolation Aura has **three** live rungs, and
the highest of them carries no target term at all:

| Rung | Condition | Where it sits | What carries it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **10** | `a_fire_inside & burning_wound & (charges=2 \| full_recharge_time<gcd.max*2)` | above the spenders **and above Eye Beam** — at **any** target count | the gold `capped` badge, through **pass 1** of the reading model |
| **20** | `active_enemies>1 & (a_fire_inside \| burning_wound)` | above Chaos Strike | **row position 7** |
| **25** | unconditional | **below** Chaos Strike | the red `immolation_single_target` badge |

⚠ **The earlier claim that Immolation Aura's single-target rung "is 25" simply ignored rung 10**,
which has no `active_enemies` term whatsoever. The structure is charge-driven with a target-count
term layered on top, not the reverse. That is also why row position alone was never going to be
the whole answer: rung 10 would demand position 4-or-better in every state, which would be wrong
in the far commoner state where no charge is banked. A **positive** cue is the right tool for a
rung that jumps the queue on a condition, and this is the one the vocabulary already spends.

### Documented misorderings — what is left after the badges

1. **Single target, no banked charge, Chaos Strike unaffordable.** The skip badge clears when you
   cannot pay for Chaos Strike (it means *press the spender instead*, and there is no spender to
   press), so the row leads to Immolation Aura at position 7 while the APL would press Felblade
   at rung 22. Narrow, and mild — Immolation Aura generates Fury too.
2. **Neither A Fire Inside nor Burning Wound.** Rung 10 and rung 20 are both unreachable, leaving
   **15** (the Demonsurge Immolation Aura — gated on *Demonic Intensity*, not A Fire Inside, so
   it survives on Fel-Scarred) and **25**. Single target is handled by the skip badge; at 2+
   targets the row over-ranks Immolation Aura, since position 7 encodes a rung this build does
   not have. Fringe build, and only the AoE half of it.
   *(Dropping **one** of the two is not a misordering: rung 20 needs only
   `a_fire_inside|burning_wound`, so position 7 stays right at 2+ targets, and rung 10 — which
   needs both — is gated off, so the gold badge correctly goes quiet.)*
3. **Felblade below Chaos Strike at Fury ≤ 100.** Rung 22 (`felblade,if=hero_tree.felscarred&
   fury<=100`) puts Felblade *above* Chaos Strike's 23; its unconditional rung 24 puts it below.
   The row uses 24. Fury is secret, so the promotion is not expressible as an order — only as the
   `overcap` readout on Felblade, which speaks in the *opposite* direction (see cue B below).

## The lanes, and why the priority falls out of them

Read the rung order against the lanes:

- **COOLDOWN** = the top of the priority (Meta, The Hunt, Eye Beam, Essence Break, Vengeful
  Retreat). They light when ready; a hold cue (C) says when to wait.
- **ROTATION** = the middle-to-bottom, where the cues do the ordering. The raw spender is *low*
  by default and only rises when cue **D** promotes it inside a readable window; the generator
  rises (relatively) when cue **A** dims the starved spender; the generator is pushed off by cue
  **B** near overcap; Immolation Aura wears the gold `capped` badge at full charges and nothing
  below it.
- **FALLBACK** = pure filler (Throw Glaive, Fel Rush).

Tier + cues, read together, reproduce that priority — which is the §3.1 point. No lane is a
strict rank; the cues cross lanes (a promoted Death Sweep in a window outranks a lit Eye Beam,
exactly as the APL's rung-12 windowed spender outranks the rung-18 baseline one).

## Roster — player problem → fact → recipe → treatment

### COOLDOWN lane

- **Metamorphosis** (`191427`, rung 3). *Problem:* ~2 min burst whose payoff is its **reset** of
  Eye Beam and Death Sweep — pressing it while either is ready, or moments before Eye Beam
  returns on its own, wastes that reset. *Fact:* the APL's whole condition is
  `!cooldown.blade_dance.up & cooldown.eye_beam.remains>8`, which splits across the
  readable/sealed line: Blade Dance's readiness and Eye Beam's readiness are **readable** (R2),
  Eye Beam's *remaining time* is **sealed** (S4). *Treatment:* COOLDOWN + **three markers unioned
  onto one red `blocked` badge** (the band grammar is AND-only, so naming one cue three times is
  the OR):
  1. `meta_wastes_death_sweep` — readable, Blade Dance is *ready*.
  2. `meta_wastes_eye_beam` — readable, Eye Beam is *ready*.
  3. `meta_awaits_eye_beam` — sealed (S4 range step-curve, `within = 8`), Eye Beam's cooldown is
     running and ends within 8s.
  Nothing satisfied → nothing drawn, and Meta is simply the leftmost button no cue rules out
  (`scenarios.md` ST-1).
  ⚠ **These three markers are a SLICE of rung 3, and one hold is missing.** The full rung is
  `(!talent.chaotic_transformation | !cooldown.blade_dance.up & cooldown.eye_beam.remains>8) &
  !action.death_sweep.demonsurge_available & !action.annihilation.demonsurge_available`. The two
  **Demonsurge** terms — *don't recast Metamorphosis while empowered casts are still owed* — are a
  third real hold and cap draws nothing for it, because whether an owed empowered cast is readable
  is unmeasured. It is filed in `../backlog.md` as an optimisation over the baseline (`proc`
  already exists in the grammar, so it is likely cheap) and recorded in *Open facts* below. Read
  "nothing rules Meta out" as *nothing cap can see*. ⚠ **Marker 2 is not redundant with marker 3 and must not be folded into
  it.** The sealed band deliberately reads *nothing* at zero remaining, and here that gap is
  load-bearing in the wrong direction: Metamorphosis sits at row position 2, **left of** Eye
  Beam, so a quiet Meta is pressed before the eye ever reaches Eye Beam. Elimination cannot save
  this one, which is exactly why Essence Break — sitting *right* of Eye Beam — needs only the
  sealed half.
- **Eye Beam** (`198013` → Abyssal Gaze ⚠ in Meta, rung 14). *Problem:* keep the demon-form window
  rolling — it enables everything downstream. *Fact:* `ready` (R2). *Treatment:* COOLDOWN, no
  hold. Same row across the FS override via R7.
- **The Hunt** (`370965`, rung 4). *Problem:* the polarity is the opposite of what an earlier
  draft asserted, and the axis was wrong too. The APL casts The Hunt when
  `cooldown.eye_beam.remains<10 & cooldown.metamorphosis.remains>15` **or**
  `!cooldown.eye_beam.up & cooldown.metamorphosis.up`. Negate that and there are **two** holds,
  not one:
  1. `hunt_awaits_eye_beam` — **Eye Beam is FAR (≥10s)** and Metamorphosis is not ready. *This is
     the common one*, covering most of a 90s cooldown's return moments, and the costliest to get
     wrong: casting here burns Eternal Hunt's empower up to ~20s before the Eye Beam it should
     ride. An earlier draft had only hold 2 and missed this entirely.
  2. `hunt_awaits_meta` — **Metamorphosis is NEAR (≤15s)** but not yet here. Save the empower for
     the Eye Beam the reset is about to hand you. **Meta *ready* ⇒ cast**, which is the polarity
     inversion; the band reads nothing at zero remaining, so readiness clears it for free.
  *Fact:* two sealed remaining-times, on two different abilities. *Treatment:* COOLDOWN + **two
  sealed bands unioned onto one red `blocked` badge** — Eye Beam `beyond = 10` and Metamorphosis
  `within = 15` — **both gated on the readable `talent(eternal_hunt)`**, because without that
  talent rung 4 is unconditional and there is no hold at all. Hold 1 additionally stands down
  while Metamorphosis is *ready*, since that is the APL's other cast case. ⚠ Each band is a sound
  **slice** of the hold rather than its literal negation; neither ever fires in a state where the
  APL would cast.
- **Essence Break** (`258860`, rung 17). *Problem:* mandatory in S2 (+49% initial, tier-set keys
  off it); open the amp window and flood it. *Fact:* `ready` (R2), plus the sealed hold. The APL
  line is `essence_break,if=cooldown.eye_beam.remains>4` — **the number Icy Veins was already
  saying, now confirmed Tier 1.** *Treatment:* COOLDOWN + **sealed hold** (C2): hold while Eye
  Beam's cooldown has ≤4s remaining (don't clip the amp window into Eye Beam) — an S4 range
  step-curve on Eye Beam's cooldown → texture alpha, painted client-side; cap never reads the
  clock. ⚠ **There is no Fury term on Essence Break in the APL at all.** The `35/maxFury`
  banked-Fury cue an earlier draft specified and parked is **deleted**, not deferred: it was
  authored from prose no Tier-1 source supports.
- **Vengeful Retreat** (`198793`, rung 5, off the GCD). *Problem:* maintain-on-cooldown press —
  holds **Exergy** (or arms Inertia, build-dependent) and refreshes **Initiative** — but *not* a
  free on-cooldown weave. Its line is **alignment-gated**: it fires on the Eye Beam weave
  (`gcd.remains<0.3 & cooldown.metamorphosis.remains & cooldown.eye_beam.remains<gcd.max*0.3 &
  !buff.initiative.up`) or on the Meta path (`cooldown.metamorphosis.up &
  cooldown.eye_beam.remains & cooldown.blade_dance.remains & !buff.eternal_hunt.up`). *Fact:*
  `ready` (R2) for the lane; the alignment is two sealed remaining-times. *Treatment:* COOLDOWN +
  **two sealed bands unioned onto one red `blocked` badge**, `vr_awaits_eye_beam` (`within = 8` on
  Eye Beam) and `vr_awaits_meta` (`within = 4` on Metamorphosis). The player reads one meaning:
  **hold, something is coming.** Three things this does *not* claim:
  1. **Neither band is the APL's literal condition.** The literal hold is broad — everything that
     is not the Eye Beam weave or the Meta path is technically a hold — and authored broadly that
     is a negative badge lit in the steady state, the same failure that retired
     `immolation_recharging`. These narrow bands are the **actionable slice**, authored the way
     the Felblade break point is.
  2. **The widths price a real cost.** Every second Vengeful Retreat is held is Initiative uptime
     not gained. 8s and 4s are guesses until flown.
  3. **It may fire less than expected.** Cycle of Hatred shaves 2.5s off Eye Beam per cast,
     stacking to 10s; at full stacks Eye Beam (~20s) is *shorter* than Vengeful Retreat (~25s),
     so Eye Beam waits on Vengeful Retreat and the badge never lights. Airtime is build- and
     stack-dependent.
  ⚠ **Nothing in the APL is held *for* Vengeful Retreat** — it is the reverse. Practically that
  is mild, since its ~25s cooldown and Eye Beam's ~30s are close enough that pressing it when it
  is up usually lands in the right place; *"press on cooldown, ideally just before Eye Beam"* is
  a fair player-facing rule, and the badge is the "ideally" half made visible. No gate impact:
  Vengeful Retreat is off the GCD and the elimination check skips `weave` entries.

### ROTATION lane

- **Chaos Strike / Annihilation** (`344862` → ⚠`201427`). *Problem:* shown castable even when
  Fury-starved, and it is the *low-priority* dump — except inside a window. *Facts:* `affordable`
  (R1) + `identity` (R7). *Treatment:* ROTATION; **dimmed when `insufficientPower`** (cue A); the
  demon-form promotion (cue D) brightens **Annihilation** while transformed — which is why the
  raw spender, ~#20 baseline, correctly rises in its window. Re-skins across the flip.
- **Blade Dance / Death Sweep** (`188499` → ⚠`210152`). *Problem:* the empowered Death Sweep is
  what you flood windows with; it costs Fury. *Facts:* `ready` (R2) + `identity` (R7); optionally
  `affordable` (R1). *Treatment:* ROTATION; **promoted as Death Sweep in demon form** (cue D).
- **Felblade / Demon's Bite** (`232893` / `344859`). *Problem:* the generator to favor when
  starved — and the one to skip when Fury is already high. *Facts (A):* generators have no Fury
  cost, so `insufficientPower` is never true — they **stay lit** while starved spenders dim; the
  relative shift is the whole signal. *Facts (B):* an authored break point on secret Fury-%,
  pushed engine-side (S1 graded) as a color curve, red at/above the break. `maxFury` is readable
  (`UnitPowerMax`, R4 — measured **170**, do not hardcode 120); cap authors one number and
  performs one division. **The two generators break at different points, from different
  sources:**

  | Generator | Break | Source |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | **Felblade** | `100 / maxFury` ≈ **59 %** | rung 22, `felblade,if=hero_tree.felscarred&fury<=100` — an absolute Fury *threshold*, authored as `threshold = 100` |
  | **Demon's Bite** | `(maxFury − 25) / maxFury` ≈ **85 %** | true overcap: an **authored static generation** table (no API, R4 — Demon's Bite rolls 20–30, midpoint 25) |

  ⚠ **Felblade's break stopped being an overcap number.** It reads `100/maxFury`, not
  `(maxFury−15)/maxFury` ≈ 91 %, because rung 22 is not an overcap rule at all — it is a
  *promotion* rule, putting Felblade above Chaos Strike while Fury is low. The badge says the
  negation of it: **above 100 Fury, spend before you generate.** `overcap` is the closest cue the
  shelf declares and it is the one used; widening the vocabulary is a shelf decision, not a
  catalog one.
  ⚠ **And the badge is informational, not eliminating.** Felblade sits at row position 9, right of
  Chaos Strike; the walk only reaches it when Chaos Strike is unaffordable, i.e. below ~40 Fury —
  far under either break. So neither generator's `overcap` badge has ever changed which button
  elimination reaches, and the old ≈91 % number never did either. Fixing it makes the readout
  *true*, not louder. *Treatment:* ROTATION + cue A + cue B. Felblade additionally rises on a
  **readable Inertia proc** *if* that proves readable (open, below).
  ⚠ **Demon's Bite is absent from the 12.1 APL, and that is not evidence against it.** The
  sim's build takes **Demon Blades**, which replaces the button with a passive — there is no
  button to press. On a build without Demon Blades it is a real button and the cue is correct.
  It binds only if the row exists, so keeping it costs nothing.
- **Immolation Aura / Consuming Fire** (`258920` → ⚠ in Meta). *Problem:* the one Fury decision
  that matters — don't sit on capped charges (2/2 with A Fire Inside). *Fact:* the charge state is
  **readable in BOTH directions** — `GetSpellCharges().isActive` is annotated `NeverSecret`, and
  it is false exactly when charges are at max (a recharge that is not running). This is stronger
  than the R6 limit an earlier draft was built on, which said charges are readable *only* at full
  (`currentCharges` plain iff at max, secret below) and therefore left "below max" as an unknown.
  It is not an unknown: it is the second state. *Identity (R7):* Immolation Aura is *the* transform
  that corrupts charge math (→ Consuming Fire, different id/charges) — R7's job here is reading the
  **right spell's** charges across the flip, not a priority override. *Treatment:* ROTATION plus
  **one** badge, and only in the capped direction:

  | Charge state | `isActive` | Badge | Says |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | at max, recharge stalled, **A Fire Inside taken** | `false` | gold `capped` (slot 3), positive | rung 10 — you are losing a charge right now |
  | below max, or the talent absent | `true` | red `blocked` at one target, **none** in AoE mode | rung 25 vs rung 20 — see below |
  | the read refused | `nil` | none | unknown is not a state — draw nothing |

  ⚠ **The below-max red `blocked` badge (`immolation_recharging`) is DELETED.** Its condition was
  literally `capped` negated, so it drew a negative badge whenever Immolation Aura was not at max
  charges — which contradicts rungs 20 and 25, where Immolation Aura is pressed unconditionally at
  one charge. It fired in the **steady state**, which is precisely the noisy-negative failure the
  negative-by-default vocabulary exists to avoid, and it duplicated `capped` as the inverse half
  of one binary. Under the current row order Immolation Aura sits at position 7 and elimination
  does not need it.

  The gold badge is the one place the style says something other than "skip" — see cue E below for
  why impending loss earns that exception, and why "about to cap" is not attempted.

  **The talent gate (cue F).** The gold badge serves rung 10, whose condition is
  `talent.a_fire_inside & talent.burning_wound & (charges=2|full_recharge_time<gcd.max*2)`, so it
  is gated on **both** talents. Without them the rung does not exist and the badge is asserting a
  rank this build has not got. cap reads the talents from the **trait config**, not from
  `IsSpellKnown` and not from a max-charge count: both are proxies for what the APL keys off, and
  a proxy that diverges fails silently.
  ⚠ **The gate is not there to stop a badge that would otherwise be stuck on.** An earlier draft
  of this file said a one-charge Immolation Aura would wear the gold badge for the whole fight. It
  would not. `Sense.readCapped` returns `nil` when `maxCharges <= 1` — a guard that long predates
  this work — so on such a build `capped` is **UNKNOWN**, and unknown withholds: the badge would
  have been *blind*, never lit. What the gate buys is that the behaviour is **deliberate** rather
  than an accident of a guard elsewhere in the engine. It is stated where the rule is, and it
  survives someone changing that guard.

  **The cooldown gates (also cue F).** Rung 10 sits **below** rungs 3 and 4, so with a charge
  banked and Metamorphosis or The Hunt *ready*, the APL presses those. A positive cue **overrides
  the elimination scan by design**, so an ungated one here would point the eye straight past a
  cooldown that outranks it — the exact failure a positive cue is dangerous for. The badge
  therefore also requires Metamorphosis **not** ready and The Hunt **not** ready.

  ⚠ **The principle, which generalises past Havoc: ONE SECRET, MANY READABLE GATES.** A graded or
  sealed cue may curve on exactly one secret — that is the platform limit — but it may be gated on
  **as many readable facts as you like**, and those gates cost nothing and hide nothing. So the
  correct shape for a cue is one secret with a fence of readable conditions around it, and
  **anywhere a cue looks over-eager, a missing readable gate is the first thing to check** —
  before reaching for a cleverer curve, a new predicate, or a treatment change.

  **The single-target skip (cue G).** Rung 25 puts Immolation Aura *below* Chaos Strike, and row
  position 7 says the opposite. The skip badge corrects it: Immolation Aura wears the red
  `blocked` badge while **AoE mode is off**, the button is **ready**, **Chaos Strike is
  affordable**, and **rung 10 is not in play**. Each term earns its place — the mode is the
  target-count substitute (cap has no readable enemy count and would not compute one); readiness
  keeps the badge off a greyed-out recharging icon, which `Treatment.For` would otherwise let it
  stand on, since it passes cues through for swiped rows; affordability clears the skip when
  there is no spender to be sent to; and the last term keeps the skip out of rung 10's way, so one
  button can never wear a gold "press me" and a red "skip me" at once.
  ⚠ **That last term is authored as a UNION of THREE markers** — `!capped`,
  `!talent(a_fire_inside)`, `!talent(burning_wound)` — because what it means is
  `!(capped & a_fire_inside & burning_wound)`, the exact negation of the gold badge's own rung-10
  test, and a marker's band grammar is AND-only. **De Morgan through the union is the general
  move: `!(P & Q & R)` is three markers, `P & Q & R` is one.**
  ⚠ **A bare `!capped` would not do**, for a reason that is easy to get backwards. `readCapped`
  returns `nil` at `maxCharges <= 1`, so on a build without A Fire Inside `capped` is **UNKNOWN**,
  and `!unknown` is still unknown. A single `!capped` marker would be permanently **blind** there
  and the skip would never draw at all. The talent halves are what answer in that state, and that
  is the genuine defect the union patches.

⚠ **C1 and C2 are one badge with two drivers, and the split is not stylistic.** A readable
marker answers *"is it ready?"*; a sealed band answers *"is it about to be?"* — and the band
reads nothing at zero remaining, by design (`Channel.HoldPoints`). So a hold that must also
cover *ready* needs **both**, and that is true exactly when the held button sits **left** of the
button it waits on: Metamorphosis (position 2, waiting on Eye Beam at 4) needs both; Essence
Break (position 5, right of Eye Beam) needs only the band, because elimination reaches Eye Beam
first anyway. Position in the row decides which halves a hold needs.

⚠ **Cue E is positive, and that is a deliberate, scoped exception** (`../render-shelf.md` Part 0.5).
It reports **impending loss**, which is urgent regardless of priority rank — and rank is the only
thing a left-to-right scan can express, so there is no negative phrasing of it. **It is allowed to
override the elimination scan**, and on Havoc it must: rung 10 outranks Eye Beam and Essence Break
and no row position can say so. That override is why cue F's fence around it matters — a positive
cue pointing past a cooldown that genuinely outranks it is the one way this exception goes wrong.

⚠ **"About to cap" is NOT expressible and is not attempted.** R6 (`../pattern-shelf.md:125`) and
OBS-066 measured that below full `currentCharges` is secret and `isActive` reads `true` at **both**
1/2 and 0/2 — it means *recharge running*, not *which charge*. (That is also exactly why the
below-max state could never have carried a countdown toward the cap.) So a threshold on the recharge
duration cannot tell "about to cap" from "about to get your first charge back", and would fire the
warning hardest while the player is charge-starved. Cue E therefore fires on the exact, readable
**full** state only. Closing the gap needs R6's napkin estimator, whose named worst case is
Immolation Aura itself (R7: the demon-form id/charge change) — so it is a decision, not a tweak.

Sealed-form names here are **the shipped ones**: cue B is `sealed-power-percent`; every cue-C2
hold is `sealed-cooldown-range`. ⚠ `spec.md` §3.6 calls the second one `sealed-duration-range`,
which is not what `Catalogs/Havoc.lua`, `Channel.lua` or `Catalog.lua` accept. These docs follow
the code; **the `spec.md` mismatch is a tracked follow-up** and is not fixed here.

## New readable providers / renderer work this catalog implies

Per §9.4, a genuinely new mechanism becomes a *small named mechanism*, never spec-specific
Blizzard plumbing. Three, each with one characterization example, each confirmed against
`knowledge/addon-dev/` before building:

1. **"Capped" readable provider** — "charges readable-at-full" (R6): `currentCharges` reads plain
   iff at max; the plain read *is* the capped signal. Override-aware max + re-seed on transform
   (R7). First consumer: Immolation Aura.
2. **Sealed Fury threshold cue (B)** — one S1 graded **color curve** mechanism, authored break
   point on secret Fury-%, negative polarity only. It is authored **two ways**, because the two
   generators' break points come from different kinds of number:
   - **`generation`** — a static approximation of what the press would add, breaking at
     `(maxFury − generation)/maxFury`. Consumer: Demon's Bite (25).
   - **`threshold`** — an absolute resource level lifted straight off an APL condition, breaking
     at `threshold/maxFury`. Consumer: Felblade (100, from `fury<=100`).
   ⚠ **`threshold` is new mechanism** (`Channel.ThresholdBreak`), added because the APL's Felblade
   rung is an absolute number and `generation` cannot express one without smuggling a hardcoded
   `maxFury` into the catalog. Both forms keep the contract exactly: cap authors one number, the
   client owns the max, cap performs one division and never learns which side the secret fell on.
3. **Sealed hold marker (C2)** — a **step-curve → texture alpha** on a sealed duration object
   (S4), in **two senses**. `within` closes the band again past the threshold (three points:
   nothing at zero, the cue inside the window, nothing beyond). **`beyond` is new** and needs only
   **two** points — a rise at the threshold with nothing after it — because Step holds the last
   point's value out to infinity. That is the whole of "the dependency is far away", and it needed
   no new machinery beyond the field. Both keep the property that matters: **zero remaining reads
   nothing**, so a band never lights on a cooldown that has come back. Consumers: Essence Break
   (Eye Beam within 4s), Metamorphosis (Eye Beam within 8s), The Hunt (Meta within 15s **or** Eye
   Beam **beyond** 10s), Vengeful Retreat (Eye Beam within 8s **or** Meta within 4s).
   ⚠ **What the mechanism still cannot do is AND two sealed facts.** A marker carries one
   `display`, and two markers union rather than intersect — so "Eye Beam is far **and**
   Metamorphosis is far" is inexpressible, which is why Vengeful Retreat's everything-is-far hold
   is a documented gap rather than a band. Before building, **check the
   current desktop renderer's marker vocabulary**: if it already has a `hold` marker slot
   (`Overlay.lua` `SLOTS`), C2 reuses it and edits nothing in Treatment/Overlay (the 9.4
   definition-of-done). The C1 **readiness marks** (Metamorphosis's two reset targets) drive the
   *same* `blocked` badge from a readable fact instead of a sealed one, so they add no new
   provider either — and because C1 and C2 render identically, the renderer needs **one** marker,
   not two. Demon-form promotion (D) is plain emphasis on a readable fact — no new renderer.
   ⚠ **Cue D is authored but not currently drawn:** the render shelf's single positive cue is
   spent on charges-capped (cue E), so the promotion has no treatment today. It stays expressible
   and is **parked, not refuted** — `../render-shelf.md` Part 0.5.
4. **The `talent` predicate (cue F)** — a readable gate answering *"is this talent taken?"*, so a
   rule authored for one build stops firing on another. `Catalog.PREDICATES` gains `talent`, with
   its argument drawn from a new `talents` declaration table (id + trait **node** + **entry**)
   that mirrors `abilities`; `Signal.subject` gains a `world.talent` lookup; `Talents.lua` folds
   the trait config into that map and caches it, dropping the cache on the CDM/talent event set.
   Consumers: `immolation_capped` (A Fire Inside + Burning Wound), both of The Hunt's bands
   (Eternal Hunt), and the skip's De Morgan union.
   ⚠ **A sealed marker may now carry readable gates.** `Catalog.Check` used to demand *exactly
   one* of `when` / `display`, which made a talent-gated band inexpressible. A `when` beside a
   `display` never contributes a cue of its own — the client paints that from the curve — it only
   decides whether the paint is allowed. `Signal` reports it as `verdict.gates[markerID]`, and
   `Overlay` hides a gated-off badge with the status `gated` rather than `refused`, because
   nothing failed. **One secret, many readable gates.**
   ⚠ **It reads the trait config, not a proxy.** `C_SpellBook.IsSpellKnown` answers about a
   *spell* and a max-charge count answers about a *button*; each stands in for the talent rather
   than being it, and a proxy that diverges fails silently. Key off what the APL keys off,
   whenever that is readable. *(An earlier note in this file's lineage proposed `IsSpellKnown`.
   It is superseded.)*
   ⚠ `[gap]` **The exact call is unmeasured.** `knowledge/addon-dev/` records nothing about
   `C_Traits.GetNodeInfo` — not its shape, not whether `ranksPurchased` / `activeEntry` survive
   combat restriction. Nothing asserts that they do: every read is `pcall`ed and every
   unrecognised shape becomes `nil`, which `Signal` turns into UNKNOWN and which **draws
   nothing**. A refused read costs the gold badge; it can never invent one. Resolve by measuring
   (*Open facts* 6), not by trusting the code.
5. **Per-marker badge instances for graded cues.** Two sealed bands naming one cue is how the
   grammar expresses an OR — but a *shared* badge frame cannot carry that OR: each band writes the
   frame's alpha from its own curve, the values are **secret** and so may not be compared or
   maxed, and whichever wrote last would win. `Overlay` now gives every graded marker its own
   badge instance stacked at the same slot, so the OR happens in the compositor: either alpha
   being opaque makes the badge visible. Without this, The Hunt's two bands and Vengeful Retreat's
   two bands would each have shown one band's answer at random.
6. **The `aoe` predicate (cue G)** — `ns.Mode.IsAoE()` routed into `Signal` as a subject-less
   readable term. It is cap's **own** state, so there is no secret, no restriction and nothing to
   measure; the only wiring beyond the predicate is that `/cap aoe` now calls `Bind.Evaluate`, so
   the row repaints when the toggle flips. First consumer: `immolation_single_target`.

## Contract boundary

- **Emphasis (Lua conditions) uses only readable facts:** `ready` (R2), `affordable` (R1),
  `identity` (R7), the "capped-at-full" charge read (R6), the trait-config `talent` read, and
  `aoe` — which is not a game read at all but cap's own toggle. Fury value, Fury-%, and every
  remaining-duration are **never** read into a Lua condition.
- **Cues carry sealed facts to client-owned sinks:** the cue-B color curve (secret Fury-%) and
  the cue-C2 range curve (sealed duration) go straight to a draw call; cap reports `offered` /
  `armed` / `refused` and never reads back.
- **Unknown never becomes confidence,** including through negation. A refused `affordable` or
  `ready` withholds; it does not assert the opposite.
- The catalog declares **no** continuous grade, no resource gate (Fury is secret), no silence
  list, no cast sequence, no coverage rule, and no vocabulary a cue does not use.
