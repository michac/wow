⚠ **Two of these have no licence recorded.** They arrived as local downloads from an effects
pack, and nothing here establishes what may be done with them. That is fine for a lab entry on
one machine and **not** fine for anything published: if either treatment is ever promoted out of
Part 7, its licence has to be established first, or the effect re-authored the way `sparkler.png`
was. `wowkb.sparkler` exists partly to make that an easy answer rather than a blocking one.

⚠ **Only `sparkler.png` is neutral** (0.000 mean saturation) and can therefore be re-hued by
`SetVertexColor`. The other four carry a baked hue and always draw in it. That is allowed for a
VFX sheet — an explosion is a gradient, not one colour multiplied — but each entry must DECLARE
which it is (`tint: "none"` vs `"lane"`), and `capart check` fails a declaration the art
contradicts.
