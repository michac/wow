Scenario Stepper

states of one Cooldown Manager row, walked left to right. Each icon carries a verdict — what Combat Assist Plus concluded about that button — and the walk names why every available button is skipped until the press.

The reading model: scan left to right and press the first button that is not ruled out. The press is not something cap draws — press and open render identically. Blizzard's swipe says skip this; a red badge says skip this, and why. The vocabulary is negative by default: a satisfied dependency draws nothing at all. Two cues are positive and scoped — capped (impending loss) and priority (press this one) — and no single entry ever wears more than one of them.

Rendered from specs/ and specs/render-tokens.json. Real Blizzard icon art at the client's 56 px, real vendored sprite frames at their real frame counts, and cap's treatments composited the way the client composites them — SetVertexColor as a multiply, never a hue rotation. This is a preview of what the addon would draw, not a diagram of it.

Per-ability states

The authored source of truth, drawn. Every row of every table below comes from catalog.json — the same file that generates Catalogs/<Spec>.lua — so the addon, this page and the definition cannot disagree by construction. A scenario walk shows one line through these states; this shows all of them, including the ones no walk reaches.

Under each state, its provenance: the rung of the upstream priority list it comes from — resolved and quoted where it resolves — or one sentence saying why no rung covers it. Every state carries exactly one of the two. Beside it, the primitives that put it on screen, by registry id. ⚠ The rung citation is display-only: nothing here asserts that the quoted line still says what it said when it was cited. That is wowkb.simc --kb --check’s job.
