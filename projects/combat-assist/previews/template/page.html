Scenario Stepper

states of one Cooldown Manager row, walked left to right. Each icon carries a verdict — what Combat Assist Plus concluded about that button — and the walk names why every available button is skipped until the press.

The reading model: scan left to right and press the first button that is not ruled out. The press is not something cap draws — press, press-promoted and below render identically. Blizzard's swipe says skip this; a red badge says skip this, and why. Every cue is negative: a satisfied dependency draws nothing at all.

Rendered from specs//scenarios.md and specs/render-shelf.md. Real Blizzard icon art at the client's 56 px, real vendored sprite frames at their real frame counts, and cap's treatments composited the way the client composites them — SetVertexColor as a multiply, never a hue rotation. This is a preview of what the addon would draw, not a diagram of it.
