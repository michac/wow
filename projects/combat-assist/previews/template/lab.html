The lab no authority

Nothing on this page is the style. These are experiments — drawn so they can be looked at, not adopted. Nothing a scenario can reach may reference them (capart build errors if it does), they never appear in a CDM row on any spec page, and a treatment leaves here by being moved into the declared style, never by being cited from here. Each entry states the question it exists to answer.

⚠ An entry is judged against its own control cell, not against the page next door. The lab used to be appended to every spec page, where the declared style happened to be one scroll away; it is not any more. So an entry that needs a comparison has to draw it — the way hotkey-l1 did as the explicit control — rather than relying on the reader remembering what the shipped treatment looks like.
